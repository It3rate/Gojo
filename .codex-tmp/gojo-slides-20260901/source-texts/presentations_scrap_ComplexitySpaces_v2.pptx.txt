Introduction
Quantities Drive Everything
Every thought, perception, and meaning we have comes from comparing and manipulating quantities — not just in math, but in all cognition
Tradition, Not Inquiry
"What is a number?" and "How can things be measured?" — these foundational questions are answered by tradition, never revisited

Machines have no tradition. We need a framework of related perspectives, each with micro-intelligence, rather than flat lists for some higher intelligence to digest

First-Class Unknowns
Limits and Resolution — the aha moment
?
Unknown Values
Something unknowable, unexamined, random, or not yet understood. Unknown is a first-class element — not an edge case.
⟷
Bounds
A dog can be many weights, but 500 tons is not a dog. The boundary is unspecified but its existence is part of the definition.
⊡
Resolution
1/2 is lower resolution than 1000/2000. A 7/16" button hole demands more precision than ½ cup of flour.
Precision and bounds aren't metadata — they are first-class elements of every measure.

Dimensionality Reduction
Complexity Spaces — how complexity collapses to simple math
The Apple Analogy
Apples are overwhelmingly complex — atoms, skin blemishes, aroma, color. But when adding weights of two apples, reduce to one dimension: weight. Simple addition works like all other addition.
The aroma still matters in some other perspective — maybe a blemish prevents it from reaching the scale. That matters, but to a different perspective running in parallel.

The Opportunity in Error
Error and Opportunity — why this changes everything
Current ML
Absorb all knowledge each pass
Correct errors globally
One-shot best guess at inference
Football-stadium computers, nuclear-reactor power, months of grinding
Distributed Intelligence
Each perspective detects its own error
Local correction, no global backprop needed
Every layer gains agency
Scales with far less compute — like biology vs brute-force calculation
Our math can never be 'wrong'.
No point is incorrect, no area is invalid. There is only measurement error — but it's invisible. If error is detectable within the system, every perspective gains agency to correct it. Each level can negotiate its own goals and contribute to outcomes it can't even perceive directly.
