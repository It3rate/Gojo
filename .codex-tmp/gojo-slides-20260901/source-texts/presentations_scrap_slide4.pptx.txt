Bounds and Resolution
Without infinity as a crutch, every measure must declare its range and resolution explicitly. Far from being a limitation, this reveals a richer structure
–
one where what you can't express is just as meaningful as what you can.
Resolution encodes reality
1/2 and 1000/2000 are the same value at low precision, but carry different information
. T
he denominator signals how carefully something was measured, and operations must respect that difference.
Bounds aren't metadata, they're meaning
A dog can't weigh five hundred tons. The approximate boundary is part of what the word dog means. Precision and bounds belong inside the measure itself, not written in the margins.
