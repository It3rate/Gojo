Recognizing Perspectives
Separating the signals from the noise.
Coexisting Perspectives
Multiple perspectives can coexist on the same elements simultaneously — a square sliding across a plane (2 DOF for position) while another view sees only an edge growing and shrinking (1 DOF for size).
Identifying Signals
The world is full of competing and cooperating actions that negotiate outcomes. The skill is identifying which perspectives carry signal and knowing how to interpret them.
