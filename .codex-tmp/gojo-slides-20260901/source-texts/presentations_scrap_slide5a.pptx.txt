Degrees of Freedom & Complexity Spaces
Every measurable or transformable value is calibrated against a "complexity space." By analyzing the degrees of freedom of simple objects within various spaces, we can understand the fundamental limits and rules governing those spaces.
Universal Calibration
From geometry to social interactions, all values exist within a framing space that defines how elements interact.
The Geometry of Freedom
A point's degrees of freedom depend on its container:
0 in a point, 1 on a line, 2 in an area, and 3 in a volume.
