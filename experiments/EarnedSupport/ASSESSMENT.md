# Earned support: first campaign

Earned backing kept useful activity running beyond the closed pool's exhaustion.
It did not produce monetary self-regulation, better supplier selection, or sensible
rest during unproductive periods. These outcomes support keeping the new native
feedback path while changing the participation policy before expanding the market.

Protocol: `666e3f6`. Implementation: `c409188`. All sixteen frozen trials completed
1,440 arrivals: 23,040 total. No parameter was adjusted after viewing results, and
no first-run output was replaced. Evidence is in [results](results).

## What was added

Each consumer now connects its later evaluated outcome to a signed, fading support
trace. Gojo equations calculate the recurrence, preserve signed division residue
and fractional payout residue, qualify the demanded outcome, and issue explicit
credits into the shared reservoir. Native accounting compares original supply plus
issued credits with every actual resource owner, including expenditure.

The trace is current mutable state. Completed market/support witnesses are released
after observation; pending forecasts, the operational equations, fractional carries,
and the once-only settlement boundary survive. The same observation cannot pay
again through a repeated read or normal checkpoint restoration.

This is an experiment-layer law assembled from existing Gojo capabilities. No core
code changed. The previous market and runner sources remain unchanged. In particular,
the four offers are still supplied rather than evolved, consumers still explore
cyclically, prices can only fall, and affordable challengers do not need to improve
on their incumbents. The new support pools globally; it does not preferentially
allocate spending to the consumer that produced the benefit.

## Long flat period and return

Both arms first observe 240 rising samples, then 960 flat samples, then 240 rising
samples again. Their first 240 arrivals have identical behavior: gross valuation
1,920 and expenditure 979. Earned support has issued 1,866 by that boundary.

| Gain | Issued | Gross valuation | Expenditure | Purchases | Purchases after the pattern returns |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | 1,916 | 4,000 | 1,382 | 0 |
| 1 | 1,914 | 1,916 | 5,914 | 1,991 | 0 |

The first flat window issues 48 deferred credits and has gross -4 from a boundary
forecast. By arrival 360 both support traces are zero. Every later fully funded
flat window nevertheless spends 660 credits per 120 arrivals, purchases 210
services, and produces zero additional benefit. Support decay works; participation
does not adapt. Both arms exhaust accessible funds before the useful pattern returns.

The causal policy gap is specific: a zero-benefit service fails immediate retention;
having no incumbent triggers another paid exploratory contact on the next arrival.
Failure therefore causes repeated shopping. Extra support funds 609 more purchases
with no extra aggregate benefit. Quietness after exhaustion is neither learned
dormancy nor evidence of a stable efficient structure.

This does not argue for eliminating exploratory waste. It argues for making its
rate responsive and keeping some ability to test a changed situation. The current
fixed population cannot invent a new interpretation while spending this budget.

## Unpredictable-sign control

| Gain | Issued | Gross valuation | Expenditure | Purchases |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | -2,436 | 4,065 | 1,563 |
| 1 | 577 | -2,752 | 4,642 | 1,796 |

At gain 1, both consumers have negative aggregate gross valuation: -68 and -2,684.
Direction error is 1,456 against baseline 1,439; fine error is 4,988 against baseline
4,317. Positive/harmful paid outcomes are 436/453 and 381/526 respectively. The
reward is therefore not simply a useful consumer subsidizing another poor consumer.

The law pays the positive part of a signed recent trace. Favorable bursts earn
spendable credits; later losses reduce future backing but do not reverse those
credits. This can fund a stream with negative total usefulness. The result exposes
rectification and short-horizon backing, not a discovered exploit or proof that
all provisional support is wrong. We did not retain per-consumer cumulative
issuance, so the final 577 cannot be divided between consumers from these summaries.

## What these quantities mean

Gross valuation is the supplied four-credit calibration of avoided error in each
consumer's basis. Direction and fine error are kept separate in reports; converting
both to credits is an explicit laboratory goal weighting, not discovered universal
value. `Spent` measures contact expense plus provider operating expense. Supplier
prices are internal transfers and must not also be deducted as whole-system cost.
Supplier reserve recycling similarly creates no value or new currency.

The symbolic cost schedule does not price actual CPU or allocation. Offers,
capacity, exploration cadence, monetary calibration and its reward gain are supplied.
Consequently this experiment can demonstrate usefulness funding later work, but
cannot establish that the system itself discovers the value of its currency or
that all costs, waste, and future learning are correctly valued.

## Preservation, verification, and resource use

- All sixteen supply ledgers remained balanced at every reported boundary:
  actual owners = 4,096 + explicit issuance.
- Nine focused check groups pass, including missing/demand gating, gradual signed
  feedback, zero-gain equivalence, exact residues, once-only settlement, supplier
  payments, native continuation and witness release. Captured pre-issuance market
  claims remain valid after later issuance.
- A real CLI continuation audit compared 32 arrivals with 17 plus resumed 15.
  The entire normalized native graph, current state, external generator state,
  and semantic totals match. Both issue 81 credits exactly once. Trailing report
  garbage is discarded; a too-short report is rejected without padding it.
- `dotnet test Gojo.slnx`: 1,752 passed. Release experiment build: no warnings/errors.

Each retained checkpoint has 279–301 carriers and 3,616–3,862 relations. Compressed
checkpoints are 55,305–61,055 bytes. All eighty result files total 1,451,408 bytes;
there is no retained frame per arrival. Native calculation still allocates about
4.20–5.22 GB cumulatively per 1,440-arrival run; report work allocates another
0.75–0.88 GB. These are allocation churn, not retained memory. Reused operational
structure does not yet eliminate temporary equation/witness construction. Wall
times are diagnostic, not controlled benchmarks; validation ran concurrently with
part of the campaign.

## Recommended next narrow experiment

Keep the earned-support path and first change how participation responds to its
evidence. Test a native slow backoff for repeated unhelpful service and contact,
with a bounded exploratory allowance or change-triggered wakeup. Preserve occasional
testing and inspectable uncertainty; compare useful work, exploration cost, and
recovery after the same flat interval. Do not call fewer contacts success if it
merely makes the structure unable to notice returning opportunity.

Then test comparative allocation: can a consumer redirect a limited budget toward
a more useful or cheaper attach point, with its actual later outcome checking the
choice? This supplies a missing response that larger currency balances cannot
produce by themselves. Qualified provisional versus sustained support should also
be challenged by the unpredictable control, without requiring every individual
exploration to pay immediately.

Only after those responses exist should nominal-price/willingness/exchange feedback
be taken as evidence for self-regulation. A controller that simply replenishes a
target balance would demonstrate its authored control rule. We want to learn whether
local useful interactions, costs, and constrained opportunities can regulate their
own participation and terms. No new market law is established by this round.
