# Connection assembly: reusable dynamic equations

These are local recognition and help results, not forecasts of future turns. The six readers receive identical raw inputs. Names and phase boundaries exist only in the environment and observer. A new probe is available on the next arrival; current receipts use the previous gates.

## Focused checks

- Warmup, unknown input and sequence gaps remain incomplete; duplicate and stale admissions do not mutate the world.
- Isolated square edges are large changes, never sustained small rise or fall.
- Continuous small-step zigzag has directional runs and reversals without jump or scale-change pulses.
- A newly proposed link cannot account for its discovery frame; it can account for a later arrival.
- The attached native product responds to actual producer/request perturbations and a zero gate blocks it.
- Unused support fades one packet per four complete arrivals, reaches zero and leaves no negative debt.
- Repeated read-only inspection does not refresh engagement, support or current pulses.
- Repeated signal changes retain the finite connection neighborhood without multiplying a live contribution.
- Probes physically attach a dormant publication; fade withdraws it and later probing reuses its identity.
- One connected equation responds to retargeted sites without replacing its instruction or mutating a pure read.
- Selected historical numeric samples remain fixed while their identified live network keeps changing.
- Changing patterns reuse actual node, constraint and operational equation identities.
- Retargeting an existing native constraint changes its recognition scope without rebuilding its node.
- The reusable equations themselves preserve absent input as unreadable, including after a previously complete frame.
- An unrepresentable feature read before settlement restores every retargeted operational site.
- An ordinary unresolved feature basis before settlement preserves its tension and rolls back admission-site movement.

## Interpretation limits

The six feature scopes, finite 30-slot neighborhood, scan order, support grants, cap and fade interval are supplied. No waveform name or future value is an input. This demonstrates native recognition and executable connection participation, not discovery of new primitives, exact timing prediction, new higher-order concepts, or genetic search. A larger-step zigzag deliberately challenges the fixed jump boundary. Shuffling preserves the pulse-increment multiset while changing order; similar recognition/help results expose the limited temporal model. Supported publications now physically attach; expired ones detach after the current pulses clear. The finite dormant relation templates remain available for later reattachment. The broad generic-help and elapsed-arrival fade policies are intentionally unchanged from the first probe, to isolate equation reuse and the selected evidence contract. The new consumer-specific interval-response read has separate focused coverage; it has not been substituted into these consumers.
