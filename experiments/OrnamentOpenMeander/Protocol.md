# Open meander follow-up, registered before training

The previous key score admitted a five-leg box and a departing lead. A post-hoc test of its frozen reserve results found no strong truly open return. Here a new trainer must produce an open angular course under pressure from competing judgments. It uses exactly the same native generator and all the same proposal operations.

**Matched arms.** Each arm devotes 4,096 paid proposals per replicate to four independent replicates, 64 generations of 64, 8 retained elites. Both start with the same proposals and use matched mutation/owner exchange streams, the same six original priors and free-grammar immigrants. Teacher scoring has two halves. Both arms receive the full frozen turning-key rubric for the first half. For the second, the old blend receives the previous registered returning-course score. The enhanced blend receives the new open-course score. Total 32,768 paid proposals, each evaluated on 6 training bases, followed by a separate eight-variant validation of the top 8. Lock all 8 finalists before issuing any final-case result. Every actual output remains reproducible from the first-wave interpreter, genome and input.

**Fresh cohort.** Enroll 6 training bases, 4 validation bases and 12 final bases, selected by fixed SHA ordering from positive A=2..9, B=2..8 and A != B. A basis is the ordered triple (A,B,pitch), identified up to common integer scale. Exclude every basis previously used anywhere in the primary manifest, and forbid repeats within and across the new splits. Half of each split has pitch=A+2B and half uses pitch=2A+B+1; accidental equalities belong only to the first family. Each final parent has two clock/phase/count settings, giving 24 scenes per arm/replicate. Numbers are supplied to realize the drawing; candidates only bind extent slots. Basis relations make connected repeats possible, without guaranteeing they are found. The two families constrain which generalization the results can test.

**Open witness.** Use one chronologically continuous owner chain within a bay. A diagonal, disconnection, cell change or reversal interrupts it. Any 5+-leg axial interval must have positive forward and backward X travel and both signs of Y travel. Consecutive legs must turn through right angles. Every pair of nonconsecutive segments must have positive planar clearance, including contacts at coincident but separately owned endpoints. Minimum projected reach, forward advance and clearance are scaled relative to pitch. At or below 6% pitch for clearance the witness is zero; at 18% it receives full clearance credit. A trajectory strictly excludes any repeated XY and keeps actual directed incidence throughout. Geometric separation alone merits partial credit (45%). Full credit also asks the first/last endpoint of the qualifying subcourse to hand off to a qualifying neighboring bay by actual site identity near the boundary. A non-self-touching small interval inside a larger unrelated sketch need not satisfy that handoff. Overdraw of the entire drawing still reduces the numerical history utility. Separate diagnostics preserve strict open-cell fraction, through-seam cell fraction, the old registered score and original aggregate.

**Feasibility control, excluded from trial seeds.** Express a motivated positive example as one owner's ordered rows: east B, north A, west B, north B, east A+B, south A+B, east B. This reaches the next anchor exactly when pitch=A+2B. A second control forms a rectangular loop and a lead, meeting the earlier witness but failing the open read. These are calibration controls; the open example is never entered as a training program. They can still arise by ordinary mutations from the shared grammar. Report the controls on the fresh cohort separately from search results.

**Four predictions.** Enhanced exceeds old-blend training on open history by 0.06 and strong open-bay fraction by 0.10; enhanced exceeds old-blend training on actual through-seam fraction by 0.10; the original full score is equivalent within 0.06. Average across the two realizations and four replicates within each of 12 final parents. Eight parents at or above margin supports a direction, eight at or below the negative margin contradict it; equivalence requires eight strictly in the band, and eight on the same outside side contradict it. Other results remain inconclusive. After locking, retain every parent, the first enrolled final parent's ordinary form and all four replicates at shared rendering scale. Choose the representative replicate by mean validation training-objective scores across both arms, lowest index breaking ties. Keep unsuccessful and identical drawings. These are construction claims and the experiment contains no human preference observations.
