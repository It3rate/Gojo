## Recorded final outcomes

All 96 enhanced finalist drawings had strong open intervals and neighboring native site handoffs in every bay. The matched earlier blend had strong open intervals in 72/96 drawings but no qualifying handoffs. Enhanced history utility averaged 0.879 versus 0.301. The original full score averaged 0.796 versus 0.893, and the expectation of original-score equivalence was contradicted in all 12 parents. Three predictions were supported and one contrary.

The distinction between a *bay* and a *band* matters. The winning rule checks each bay and a handoff to an eligible neighbor. A route in the next bay can still cross or retrace an earlier bay. We replayed every stored program in a fresh process and independently reproduced all 240 recorded open scores, strong-cell fractions, handoff fractions and edge counts. A further post-hoc diagnostic asked whether an uninterrupted native course showing a strong return in **every** bay traverses the full frame without revisiting **any** XY point. No trained finalist met this stronger whole-band standard. The enhanced set contained 48/96 drawings with a strong native course running across the whole band; all of these touched part of their own earlier path. The other outcomes can have local handoffs without a single uninterrupted pen sequence satisfying the full-band read. The supplied control had 12/24 whole simple bands and the box 0/24. The core grammar therefore can express this construction, although the searches did not discover that stronger invariant.
