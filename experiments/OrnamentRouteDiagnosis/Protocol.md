# Does relief attract later traffic?

This is a constructed positive and negative control for the unchanged primary pressure apparatus. In the main test, receipt-backed relief was rare compared with misses. Here the outlet names and external evaluator supply no rewards to the router. We give it a particular physical opportunity to see whether an actual relieved seam request changes which pen receives the next one.

## The fixture and the registered contrasts

One pen lies on the carrier and ends its course with an unconstrained upward step. A positive seam request may instead use the period extent to land on its next anchor. Another pen occupies a separate lane, leaves home, returns to its saved home site and intentionally stops. That endpoint already has a binding and its intended terminal is exempt from seam repair. Swap these same two physical programs between owner positions. This supplies a structural difference in ability while retaining equivalent unsubsidized geometry across the swaps.

In the **early** fixture the first pen's last row occurs on beat 6 of an eight-beat bay, leaving one beat before the machine reads the seam. In **synchronous** the last row coincides with the machine read. In **unreachable** the early pen first moves east and north, leaving a non-axial, non-diagonal remaining displacement to the expected seam. No legal offered extent can complete that seam. The fixed pen is bound in all three fixtures. The deterministic fixture tapes, rather than the names of their roles, execute in the native runtime. The only attached sensor and actuator are machine seam sensing and pen end binding. The seam shortfall is sensed from the actual ordered native endpoints. The machine treats the explicit stop as legitimate. There is no externally injected impulse.

We use 12 constructed bases with A=3+i, B=2+i mod 4 and pitch=13A+11B+1 (i=0..11), 5 slots each. The resulting period is much farther than ordinary earlier length calibrations but always reachable using the actual period slot from home. Compare 32 and 56 executed bays and both owner assignments, plus a uniform route prior versus a prior favoring only the bound pen by one position. Relative to the unchanged primary runtime this creates 288 different configurations. Each runs live, fixed routes, downward cut, upward cut, quiet and cyclic lane misbinding, 1,728 native drawings. No search or choice among these fixtures is performed.

The primary contrast averages horizons and swapped owner positions within each of twelve bases. Use the share of machine-downward seam admissions actually received by the useful pen. For an interval with no admitted units use zero and additionally count that missing opportunity. Report early (first eight bays), late (last eight bays), and post-relief counts measured from the first witnessed relief to this specific outlet. For the fixed control the post-relief window uses the *same boundary beat* as the matched live case. Report full-drawing useful seam settlements, live/fixed material differences, end debt, actual route strength changes, and all arms. An increase in traffic without a visible improvement remains only a routing result.

1. With uniform priors and the early fixture, predict live gains at least 0.08 of late downward admissions to the useful pen over fixed. Require 8/12 bases. Repeat separately for each owner assignment, without choosing between them. A positive claim about learning also requires at least one confirmed relief on the useful link and subsequent positive receipt traffic in each supporting base.
2. In the same fixture, predict at least +0.08 in useful seam-settling fraction under live compared with fixed on 8/12 bases. Count native strokes landing on the actual next site, including the last bay. The intentionally stopped owner is excluded from this endpoint obligation.
3. Predict equivalence of live and fixed late useful share for the unreachable fixture with uniform priors (margin 0.02). Without a physically effective draw the corresponding outlet cannot earn a routing reinforcement.
4. Predict equivalence at the same margin when the prior strongly favors only the bound outlet in the early fixture. Receipt-based learning can have a cold-start limit.
5. With the downward channel cut, or with the incoming seam request delivered to the extent lane while only the seam actuator is enabled, predict zero useful seam settlements in the early fixture, and no positive credit to the machine→useful seam link. This exact control fails on any contrary row.
6. Predict at least 0.08 more late useful share under live early than under live synchronous with matched uniform priors in at least 8/12 bases. This tests sensitivity to the delay between physical action and the next local observation. The observation schedule can also affect fixed routes, so report both.

For directional contrasts eight parents meeting the positive margin support and eight meeting the negative margin contradict. Equivalence requires eight strictly within the band; eight outside in a common direction contradict it. Other cases are inconclusive. Preserve conditional outcomes across assignments and all failures. These 12 bases share the same intentionally constructed topology and are not independent replications of natural motifs. Any supported outcome establishes a mechanism on this fixed apparatus, not general learned optimization.
