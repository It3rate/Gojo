# Can available height open another construction?

A wider frame alone lets a pen reach farther. It gives no pen a reason to change a short drawing that already fits. This study connects the unary vertical-slot surface to editable local path gates and asks what that extra information can buy. All three machines are explicitly authored for this question. They are compared as mechanisms, with no learned finalists or human preference ratings.

The same tensored path runtime that preserves site identities executes all rows. Three gated compounds are registered before opening drawings: a running key with real optional side limbs at named sites; a pitch spine with two terminating inward spirals in opposed registers; and a pitch spine with a crest that mirrors itself in alternate bays, plus a second pen starting in the reflected frame. Each program owns three binding bytes in front of its serialized native path. One local owner reads the first width slot, the second reads the fourth and the third reads the eighth. Gate laws compile from ordinary Gojo positive-frame bit relations. The availability input is a contiguous stream of occupied slots. Each offer can independently be refused for crossing the actual height capacity. A blocked earlier row may leave the next row starting from a different site.

Use six distinct A/B ratios with pitch A+2B, six actual bays and an aligned period-eight clock. The six normalized triples must be distinct from the preceding tension tournament. Register them and the three byte strings before generating any output. Each program executes on the identical extents, initial pulse, phase and pitch at two, five and nine vertical slots. Recalibrating the slots does not move or rescale the already supplied engine.

**W1.** On all six bases, the ladder will deposit with one, two and three distinct owners in order from narrow through wide. **W2.** On all six bases, the spine will make every native end-to-next-start handoff in that arm. **W3.** At intermediate width, capacity only will deposit some optional ink from the third owner that the ladder withholds, on at least four bases per machine. **W4.** Both opposed spirals in the wide frame will end in a witnessed, one-ray terminal and will preserve more horizontal-reflection correspondence across the whole motif than the single intermediate spiral, gaining at least 0.15 on four bases. **W5.** In the intermediate crest family, adjacent bays will agree more after horizontal reflection than after simple translation by at least 0.15 on four bases. In the wide family the two correspondences will agree within 0.02 on four bases. **W6.** Across all three machines and six bases, the wide ladder will preserve zero positive-length overdraw in at least two thirds of the 18 realizations. Record contrary and inconclusive outcomes, physical refusals, eligible connected forks, terminals, auxiliary strokes and seam continuity. Crossing a line in another direction is admissible. Also report pattern-level symmetry for the key; it has no promised mirror.

W1 and W2 are diagnosed per machine; an incomplete transition is a failed prediction for that template. The registered mechanism scale is 3 engines × 6 bases × 3 widths × 4 arms = 216 runs. The resulting correlations describe the three supplied machines. They do not demonstrate spontaneous invention of a width relation. No further rounds of search will be selected on these scores.
