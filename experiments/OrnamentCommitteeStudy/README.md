# Keeping continuity on a small committee

The unconstrained selectors gave course a seat on only 19 of 96 panels. We retained the same nominations but required material to participate and reserved at least six of sixteen shares for the course of a through-running engine. For the inward spiral we reserved at least four for joints instead, because its inside stop can be intentional. Every panel still had two to four readers. We trained 32 new winners under the restricted committees, charging 15,360 program trials and 92,160 training drawings. Originals, comparisons, and the new finalists were locked before evaluating 6,912 reserve drawings.

The restricted committees improved the mean broad score by 0.0248 over unrestricted selectors. The directional prediction was supported in six families, inconclusive in one and contrary in one. The course or joint constituent rose by 0.109 on average. Comparison with the fixed declared panel was much less decisive. Across families the mean broad gain there was 0.0112. Requiring a seat lowered historical similarity by 0.0404 on average, including a 0.230 loss for opposed chevrons. Each result is a consequence of how a new population was selected, rather than a re-score of the old winner.
