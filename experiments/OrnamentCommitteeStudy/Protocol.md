# Does a sparse panel need a guaranteed seat for continuity?

The primary panel selection admitted course on 19 of its 96 panels. A fixed four-reader panel with seven shares assigned to course exceeded the unconstrained panel search on the mean broad score. We will test one remedy in a second local competition without seeing reserve results.

Keep all original pilots, reference packets, training banks, nominee calibration scores, search operations, seeds, 480-proposal budget and validation rules. For through-running briefs require at least six of sixteen shares on course, and at least one on material. For the inward spiral, which can legitimately terminate, require at least four shares on joints and at least one on material. In each family/replicate/width use the highest calibration-scoring existing eligible panel. Break ties by panel bytes; retain its other readers. If no eligible spiral panel exists, use the declared panel as an explicit recorded fallback. In all cases the sum remains sixteen and two to four readers participate. An explicitly identical committee or program stays in the study. The old coupled and declared winners are already locked, and the new 32 winners must be locked before accessing reserve outcomes.

Read the twelve reserve bases, two views and three widths under identical live execution. Average paired differences across widths, views and four replicates within each basis. Predict (1) the restricted winners exceed the original unrestricted winners by .012 on broad utility; (2) they exceed the unrestricted winners by .04 on the reserved constituent (course or joints); (3) their broad utility is equivalent to the declared winners within .025; (4) their historical similarity is equivalent to the original coupled winners within .04. Require eight of twelve bases beyond a margin, or within the open margin for equivalence. Keep failures and ties. These thresholds and read choices are experimental preferences. Retaining a seat for a constituent does not establish improvement on every brief.
