# An ornament must draw its lines

This local competition starts with a blank strip. A program executes strokes, can leave one path to start a branch, and can attach to a published site. A drawing and its history survive together. The question is whether judging these machines from several perspectives helps them produce better borders than optimizing spacing alone. A further question is whether that advantage is visible to someone who sees the drawing without its laboratory scores.

## Before opening any competition results

Freeze the executable, the actual Gojo kernel it loads, the machine grammar, the judge descriptions, the goals, every physical input, and the conditional predictions. Keep reserve scores unopened until a separate follow-up question is registered. Use 8 local CPU workers by default. Worker timing must never decide ties. Operate locally, separately from the Atlas.

The historical precedents include meanders, lozenges, changes of register and repeated diagonal hatching documented on Greek pottery. They motivate measuring courses, joint sequences, contrast and space in a band. Studies of contour integration motivate a separate read of path continuity. Historical examples justify asking particular questions. They supply neither training labels for these drawings nor a universal weighting of beauty. [Metropolitan Museum, examples on pottery](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/The_Passas_Painter_A_Protoattic_Realist_The_Metropolitan_Museum_Journal_v_38_2003.pdf) · [Field, Hayes and Hess](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/004269899390156Q).

Five goals emphasize different readings of the same 28 observations. The quiet border seeks open space and a repeat. The turning key seeks a winding course that continues through its seam. The countermarch seeks opposing rhythmic figures. The branched spray seeks meaningful attachments to a main stroke. The stepped lace seeks openings, contrasts and returns. The exact weights are recorded in the design manifest. Each perspective starts with equal total influence, then gains two units for each specially requested reading. Within each perspective, emphasized criteria receive five times their default weight. Requested evidence that is unavailable contributes zero to utility and stays visibly unavailable in the per-case records. A drawing needs two nonparallel stroke directions to enter the utility comparison. This floor intentionally excludes a successful single straight rule.

## What competes

Every goal uses four reproducible proposal streams and fourteen equally funded arms. The full evolutionary search competes against full random sampling, evolution starting from unrestricted random bodies, all six single-perspective omissions, field alone, and four two-perspective combinations. In the histories the combinations are craft/field, seam/rhythm, topology/craft and symmetry/rhythm. Every other search starts with mutations of six authored structural foundations. A pure-foundation control chooses the strongest of those six unmodified bodies on validation for each brief. Distinguish that supplied construction knowledge from discovered mutations or exchanges.

Each of 280 populations receives 18 generations of 24 paid proposals. Generation zero has equal representation of the six foundations in the seeded searches. Immigrants, single-parent edits and owner exchanges retain their proportional opportunities even when proposals repeat. All proposals, parent positions, operators, bytes and mean criterion results are retained. The top twelve distinct bytes compete to produce later generations. The six strongest go to validation. Selection compares the arm's own training objective, never the final blind utility. Validation breaks ties by training utility, program length and original trial position. A local machine can modify its writable frame bit during a run. Offered mutations between generations belong to the external trainer.

All 120,960 candidate trials see four training realizations each. The 483,840 training executions exclude validation and replay. Inputs bind extent A, extent B, repeat pitch, number of executed cells, initial public pulse and clock phase. The full ordered set of inputs is in the manifest. The two blind views of one parent share a physical basis and change cell count, phase and initial pulse together. Changes between these bundled views have multiple possible causes. The holdout tests new combinations of the same finite path grammar. Each physical length is an input to a construction, and no candidate encodes pixel coordinates.

## What will count as support

Lock the 280 validation finalists and five foundation choices before the blind assay. Compare all under the full brief on twelve new physical bases, two views apiece. Test matched wire cut, frozen writable frame and forced-open gates on each full finalist. For each comparison, average full minus comparator across views and search replicates within each parent. The directional margin is 0.02 on the 0–1 brief score. Eight of twelve parents in the predicted direction supports the comparison; eight in the reverse direction contradicts it. Equivalence requires eight within the open margin. Remaining results are inconclusive. The four secondary strata are consecutive blocks of three bases from the manifest. For each, all three parents must agree. The resulting 85 overall and 340 stratified predictions share underlying programs and tests. Their counts do not constitute independent studies or statistical significance.

Predict a full advantage over random, unrestricted starts, unmodified foundations, field-only and paired perspectives. An omitted perspective is predicted to help when reinstated if its group includes an emphasized criterion, otherwise predict equivalence. A live wire or gate should help branched spray and stepped lace. The writable frame should help countermarch and branched spray. Predict equivalence in the remaining intervention cells. For ablation comparisons, a high full score is evidence under the registered brief. It cannot independently validate taste.

Record how often each criterion is available and when it reverses a measured parent–child preference in full search. Recompute the preference with its contribution removed, including candidates ultimately rejected. Count positive and negative reversals separately. Retrain thirteen other search arms, then test a foundation and three runtime interventions for seventeen contrasts against full search. Distinguish evidence that a criterion influenced selection from evidence its selected output proved useful on new inputs.

## Visual examination and limits

Show the validation-selected representative under the first blind basis, its best and worst blind realization, simpler comparisons and other replicates. Render the actual executed cells. Any further illustrative copies must be identified as such. Run hand-constructed judge challenges separately, including equal ink with different site identity.

For a small additional blind visual read, fix 20 pairs, one per goal and replicate, comparing full against field for replicates 0 and 2 and random for 1 and 3. Use blind basis at index (3 × goal index + replicate) modulo 12, ordinary view. Shuffle side assignment independently. Reviewers get the brief and the drawings only and can choose either or tie. With 20 pairs, treat this as a diagnostic, never a human taste benchmark. Record votes before revealing machine provenance. The local gallery can collect a user's own preferences in a separate file. No human ratings are assumed.
