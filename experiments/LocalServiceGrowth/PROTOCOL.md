# Local service growth: protocol before trials

This is an exploratory construction, not an autonomous market or a general
complexity estimator. It keeps previous experiments and their results unchanged.

## Question

Can a structure buy a real upstream service, pay to try a narrower independent
construction, use it to replace that dependency, and sell the resulting service?
Can a small public signal of successful connections guide bounded local discovery
without becoming a substitute for the buyer's own later outcome?

## Intended native structure

```
input -> fine supplier -> paid live input -> intermediary -> coarse outlet
   |                                         ^                |
   +-> supplied candidate -> paid trial -> later outcome       +-> consumers
                                             |
                                      support for substitution

service outlet -> public active count / qualified count
consumer -> local neighborhood + traversal cursor -> at most two advertisements
connection -> actual paid use -> later interpretation -> qualified contribution
payment -> supplier wallet -> operating expenditure
```

Ports, dependencies, money, candidate support, count contributions and comparison
must exist in Gojo relation/carrier structure. Host code supplies the environment,
admissions, finite fixture population and reports; it must not score candidates
with detached ordinary-code fitness calculations.

## Supplied footholds

The fine supplier predicts the next difference from the latest observed difference.
Its price is 4 and its modeled operating cost is 2. The intermediary buys that
input and performs a direction clamp, costing another 1; its initial offer is 6.
The candidate is an independently instantiated last-direction construction using
the exposed raw observations. Construction costs 12, each shadow calculation costs
1, and later successful coarse outcomes plus lower input cost justify promotion
after four net supporting observations. Failed observations can oppose support;
unknown observations do not supply a verdict. The promoted offer costs 2 to buy
and 1 to produce. Its old paid supplier must actually cease being a dependency.

The candidate recipe is supplied. This tests paid construction, delayed validation,
dependency replacement and public use, not the invention of the recipe. Its coarse
function intentionally overlaps the original service. We are not discovering the
supplier's private computational complexity or asserting novelty from a copied
result. Public success history and spendable money are not copied into new work.

Operating costs, prices, service capacities and construction support threshold are
authored. Shared publication for one current input may serve multiple consumers,
but a previous input's result must not silently become a current paid calculation.
Two downstream consumers ask for coarse output initially; the second then requires
fine output and later returns to coarse. Fine service can satisfy coarse requests;
coarse service cannot claim finer detail. Coarse/fine meaning is expressed through
actual reading surfaces and their allowed omission, not a symbolic service tag.
All supplied increments fit the declared source interval [-8,8].

The first consumer initially sees only the intermediary. The second can see both
services. A direct-access control also lets the first see both. This makes the
intermediary's initial access advantage explicit rather than secretly making the
cheaper fine supplier incompatible with a coarse consumer.

Use a finite initial 65,536-credit pool with explicit bounded funding inlets. No
earned issuance is added in this round: the prior minting law must not confound
the comparison of real supply-chain costs. Supplier fees are internal transfers;
whole-system expenditure includes operating, contact, construction and trial work.

## Public connection signal and locality

Each distinct consumer/service connection contributes at most one active count and
one qualified count. Qualified means its last evaluated use was useful in that
consumer's own basis. Detachment retracts contributions; missing outcomes preserve
their unresolved status. Counts are hints, not probabilities or proof of independent
evidence. No recursive global ranking or payment for popularity is introduced.

Counters are updated by native local deltas. Reading an advertisement must not
enumerate all clients. Duplicate registration/update must not inflate the counts.
Native connected local neighborhoods contain at most eight supplied neighbors;
one search reads at most two advertisements and advances its local cursor. Traversal
of this bounded neighborhood is allowed; no population-wide scan, sort, or inspection
of every private recipe is allowed. A periodic exploration opportunity must remain
available to offers without an established usage count.

Actual compatibility, affordability and capacity apply before a public-use hint.
A bounded public success contribution can affect local preference; subsequent own
outcomes determine support. The exact executable local policy will be frozen with
the implementation before first campaign outcomes, and any deviations from this
proposed shape will be disclosed.

## First campaign

For seeds 11, 23 and 47, run 360 arrivals under each combination of:

- Restricted or direct supplier access for the first consumer.
- Buy-only without popularity; construction without popularity; construction with
  a public-success hint.

That gives eighteen paired runs. Add three direct-access, seed-11 unpredictable-sign
runs under the same three policies, for twenty-one runs / 7,560 arrivals total.

The main fixture has three 120-arrival phases: both coarse, second consumer fine,
both coarse again. Signal direction reverses every 18 arrivals, magnitude is a
seeded 2..4, and each phase contains a 12-arrival flat interval at offsets 72..83.
Arrival 180 is explicitly missing. Labels, seed and future input never enter the
interpreting network. The unpredictable control changes sign by an external hash,
with magnitude 2..6, and uses the same demand phases and missing arrival.

No tuning after outcomes. Bugs that invalidate a trial must be recorded before an
affected rerun; negative findings remain findings. A fixed supplied population and
bounded-neighborhood check cannot establish scaling to millions of actors.

## Observation and checks

Record sparse windows, actual consumer errors at each requested resolution,
resolved/unresolved and paid/unpaid opportunities, input payments, resale receipts,
operating/contact/build/trial costs, construction/promotions, supplier dependence,
active/qualified counts, inspected advertisements, money accounting and retained
native graph size. Reports never feed back into choice. Preserve only live state,
pending promises and explicitly needed recent support; release completed frames.

Check the paid live dependency directly, then perturb the old source after
substitution to establish independence. Check no premature outcome reward, no
duplicated capacity/money/reputation, coarse/fine compatibility, local inspection
bound, missing input, native restore/continuation, and release of finished witnesses.
Do not assert a particular trial winner. Run focused checks and the repository suite.

Promising behavior includes lower cost for equally useful coarse output, continuing
demand for finer service, a real consumer-to-producer transition and locally useful
discovery. Failure includes expensive imitation, reliance on the supposedly replaced
input, popularity preventing a useful newcomer, stale endorsements, or a reseller
surviving solely because of restricted access. Merge, split, autonomous recipe
synthesis, endogenous pricing and learned global value remain future work.

## Executable policy frozen before the campaign

The implementation uses the following supplied laws. The contract checks passed
before the campaign; these settings will not be selected against its outcomes.

- Two provider and two consumer wallets start at 32, drawn from the pool.
  Each arrival can fund a consumer by up to 8, capped at a balance of 64.
- A ready, affordable candidate is constructed at the first opportunity. There
  is no learned investment trigger. Before promotion it receives one paid shadow
  trial per ready arrival while its provider can afford it. Helpful coarse outcomes
  add one support; other resolved outcomes subtract one, bounded between 0 and 4.
  Promotion requires support 4 and an input saving. It is irreversible in this
  experiment, although consumers can stop buying. The offer changes from 6 to 2
  by the supplied law, not by endogenous price negotiation.
- Each service can deliver to two clients per arrival. Production is paid once
  for the current arrival and can serve both; all current presence flags reset
  on the next arrival. Consumer 0 is scheduled first. This fixed admission order
  can bias capacity allocation and is not an emergent market rule.
- Each search inspects at most two neighboring advertisements and costs 1 per
  inspection. An incumbent is reconsidered after four completed services, or
  when it is no longer eligible. Ordinary preference is the last own gross
  benefit minus price, plus `hintWeight * min(qualifiedConnections, 2)`.
  Its private margin must be positive independently of the popularity term.
  A previously untested eligible offer, or a non-incumbent on every fourth search,
  receives a reserved paid trial. This is an authored exploration policy.
- Initial local benefit expectation is 8. Changing requested detail resets that
  consumer's local service evidence to this prior, and disconnects its old route.
  Earlier promises keep their captured interpretation. Latest local benefit is
  a deliberately small current trace, not an average over historical samples.
- Outcome benefit is avoided absolute error relative to a zero-change baseline
  at the requested read: direction clamped to [-1,1], or detail in [-8,8].
  Coarse avoided error is calibrated at 8 credits per unit; fine at 4. These
  utility calibrations are supplied. Positive gross benefit qualifies a still
  attached connection; nonpositive net benefit releases it. Neither evaluation
  nor a public connection count creates spendable money.
- Missing or out-of-domain observations remain unresolved. Current publication
  closes when input is missing. A removed connection contributes neither active
  nor qualified use; the public signal has no accumulated transaction votes.

The native arithmetic, payment, dependency, support and count structures are
reusable and mutable. Host scheduling still executes the authored policy and
constructs local comparison/read witnesses. This is not a self-scheduling soup
of actors, nor proof that all policy has crystallized into autonomous equations.
Report windows and full current checkpoints are observers only; completed frames
are released, while pending predictions and live support remain. Maintained public
counts avoid client enumeration when reading an offer, but registration can inspect
local incidence and kernel settlement can traverse connected accounting structure.
Bounded offer discovery does not make those operations constant-cost.
