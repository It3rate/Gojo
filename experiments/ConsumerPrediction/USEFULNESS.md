# Usefulness as connected, revisable relationships

Status: exploratory design discussion. The first experiment in PROTOCOL.md tests
one small combination of these ideas. The alternatives below are not new kernel
axioms, implemented evolutionary laws, or a claim about how biological memory
works.

## Recalculation and memory

Dynamic structure is the source of current answers. A recent result, a short
trace and a historical commitment have different jobs:

- Current input drives a reusable structure and produces a new read.
- A changing trace supplies context that the instantaneous input cannot supply,
  such as whether a value increased or how far a run has progressed.
- A pending forecast preserves what was promised before its outcome. Replacing
  it with a forecast recalculated using an adapted model would rewrite history.
- An optional archive supports an explicitly requested historical inspection.
  It need not participate in ordinary interpretation.

For the present probe, three live level sites and one pending issue are enough.
There is no table of historical situations and their answers. Support is a
coarse evolving trace of experience, not a list of previous successful values.
The observer may save results without granting the learner access to that file.

Longer-term memory could retain a reusable mechanism plus a starting condition.
Driving that mechanism again would construct a replay. If exact historical
detail has been forgotten, the generated replay is model-conditioned and must
not claim to recover that detail. Forgotten context may leave several possible
replays; keeping those alternatives is more honest than silently selecting one.
An input-driven trajectory also needs its external forcing retained, supplied
again, or replaced by an exposed model. A mechanism and seed alone cannot
reconstruct inputs it never retained.

Pressure to recalculate should discourage persistent answers detached from
their generating dependencies. It should not forbid every useful state. A
short-lived shared result may be cheaper than repeating an expensive read for
several consumers at the same instant. A changing phase site may be the actual
state of an oscillator, not a disposable cache.

A future local tradeoff could expose three competing routes:

```text
current source -> recalculated read ---------------------> consumer
source/dependency witness -> recent retained read ------> consumer
retained mechanism + seed -> reconstructed replay ------> consumer
```

Each route would expose its basis, freshness/applicability, unresolved detail,
latency and use of limited capacity. A result becoming stale would lose its
claim to be current. Under capacity contention, retaining a rarely used result
could lose to retaining the smaller mechanism that recreates useful responses.
This is a proposed pressure, not something already implemented in the probe.

## Do not conflate the questions

| Read | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Predictive ability | What subsequent participation does this structure account for, under which circumstances? |
| Current demand | Does a consumer actually need that distinction now? |
| Marginal contribution | Does this contribution change the consumer's result beyond what its other inputs already provide? |
| Opportunity | Did the relevant context occur, and was a promise issued or an eligible opportunity declined? |
| Resource cost | What occupied capacity, activity or delay did the contribution require? |
| Retention value | What is worth keeping available for future use or replay? |

These can influence one another through explicit equations. Initially combining
them into one number would hide important disagreements. Incompatible units do
not become addable merely because both numbers seem relevant to usefulness.

## Mechanisms to explore

### 1. Relief of one consumer's actual obligation

For an allowed error interval I chosen by a consumer, let D_I(e) be the qualified native
violation of prediction error e. For the same later observation:

```text
U = D_I(error without contribution) - D_I(error with contribution)
```

Positive means relief, negative means harm, and zero can mean either no marginal
change or that both results already satisfy the obligation. Preserve the two
demands as well as their difference. Open input remains an open read.

This is directly expressible with current interval-response reads. The
predictions, candidate correction, observation and comparison must be connected
to the same issue/outcome. A changing environment between two measurements would
not, on its own, establish that a helper caused improvement.

**Risk:** a producer could earn support by creating the unmet obligation or
widening its own tolerance. Keep those surfaces consumer-owned, fix them before
the outcome, and expose the origin of demand.

### 2. Separate useful aspects

A forecast of -6 when the next change is +6 preserves magnitude and fails
direction. Give direction and scale their own connected read surfaces. An
amplitude-sensitive consumer may use that output directly; a directional
consumer needs an explicit reversing transformation that succeeds prospectively.

This preserves partial competence without declaring a harmful prediction useful
to every consumer. Timing and stability can be other aspects, with their own
bases and requested resolutions.

**Risk:** a blended score can hide a critical failure behind irrelevant accuracy.
The consumer must select which aspects are acceptable or consequential.

### 3. Residual-specific help

A coarse interpreter can export its accounted component and expose the remainder
through another port. A second structure competes to predict or handle that
specific remainder. It can be inaccurate about the whole signal yet valuable
for what the first structure cannot handle.

```text
source -> coarse read -> consumer
                  residual -> helper -> consumer's remaining obligation
```

**Risk:** the remainder changes when the upstream model adapts. Its source/basis
and issue context must remain connected; old corrections cannot be silently
reused in a changed residual frame. Observation residue is not automatically a
new injection of physical tension.

### 4. Opportunity-qualified persistence

A turn helper receives predictive feedback when its declared context occurs
and a subsequent outcome is available. Ordinary intervening silence need not
damage its ability evidence. If it promises a turn during that silence, however,
an observed flat signal can be a real failed opportunity.

**Risk:** a candidate could evade every failure by narrowing its scope after the
fact. Eligibility must be fixed when the issue is made. Expose coverage and
cost separately so a candidate that never contributes does not win by abstaining.

Opportunity counts can be useful coarse history. More repeated reads of one
event do not create more evidence; several correlated routes do not automatically
make independent observations. Keep opportunity count separate from output
resolution and from the amount of unresolved context.

### 5. Demand through bounded receiving capacity

An unmet consumer obligation opens a compatible receiving surface. Useful
producers compete for its actual capacity or a bounded support budget. Once the
need is satisfied, additional identical offers get no additional intake.

This gives an accurate but unused leaf counter a reason to become inactive
without saying that it predicts badly. It also lets useful downstream structures
support lower-level features they depend on.

**Risk:** popularity and circular agreement can manufacture apparent importance.
Feedback returning through A -> B -> A is not fresh independent evidence. A
bounded budget should not multiply merely by taking several routes; externally
grounded outcomes or explicit internal tasks must justify its renewal.

### 6. Complementarity and substitution

Some parts become useful only together. Read the same consumer with neither,
with A, with B, and with the connected pair. A turn event and a run structure
may form a meaningful ordered composition even when neither alone supplies a
forecast. Shared group benefit should not be paid in full to every member.

The converse is equally important: if A and B each satisfy a need completely,
removing either alone shows zero loss. Fading both simultaneously would destroy
useful service. Limited intake, prospective alternating trials, or a comparison
of the set against neither can expose which service must remain.

**Risk:** exhaustive subset comparisons become expensive. Start with small local
compositions suggested by shared unresolved obligations, not every possible set.

### 7. Useful refinement at the requested resolution

A cheap structure can satisfy a coarse read, while a finer branch handles only
the distinction still requested. A zigzag approximation might therefore remain
useful even when a more accurate curve structure is available.

**Risk:** more digits are not automatically more benefit. If both results meet
the current consumer's need, fine accuracy may add no current utility. Preserve
the distinction between requested resolution, allowed error and uncertainty
about the actual signal.

### 8. Earlier information and avoided future pressure

A coarser prediction arriving before an action deadline may be more useful than
an exact description arriving afterward. A prediction could open a route or
reserve capacity that prevents a later blocked transfer or unmet draw.

**Risk:** claimed future savings are not observed savings. Connect the issued
forecast, allowed action, later transition and actual remaining pressure. Keep
simulated action outcomes distinct from an intervention in the environment.

### 9. Exploration paid from a limited allowance

An unresolved obligation can fund a temporary probe connection. The probe is an
expense and a question, not a success. Later useful outcomes can justify its
continued participation; unhelpful outcomes release its capacity.

**Risk:** surprise is cheap to manufacture. Exploring noise can remain endlessly
novel without helping any consumer. Limit exploration by the obligation and
available capacity, and keep the exploratory allowance separate from earned
predictive support.

### 10. Maintenance, activity and remembered ability

Keep separate routes for the compact retained structure, current activation and
its ability evidence. A rare useful structure can cease running between its
opportunities, then take current input and recalculate when relevant again.

**Risk:** dormancy is not free storage. Real capacity contention may eventually
force forgetting or coarsening. Such loss should not masquerade as evidence that
the structure was wrong. Wake-up also needs a trigger; a dormant predictor cannot
magically recognize its own relevance without some active receiving structure.

## Where local usefulness ultimately comes from

There is no demonstrated Gojo law that makes every local improvement converge
to one globally good outcome. Different consumers can legitimately disagree.
One might care about accurate temperature; another about a timely coarse alarm;
another about energy consumption. Their connections, allowed ranges, capacities
and priorities must make those differences visible.

For the original conceptual work, removing all ambiguity is not always the
goal. A useful poetic interpretation may preserve two interacting readings,
expose a contrast, or route an unresolved part to another local space. The
obligation there is not automatically 'make every residual zero'. A structure
that erases the interesting ambiguity could settle a narrow claim and still
fail the wider task.

The shared pattern is: expose the need, make a bounded contribution, inspect
what changed for that need, and preserve what the comparison cannot establish.
Arithmetic can evaluate a specified relation of usefulness. It cannot conjure
the consumer's purpose from an otherwise unspecified number.

## What this first probe chooses

The fixed probe combines prospective consumer-relative relief, opportunity-
qualified support, explicit negative feedback, and a small bounded shadow
candidate. It preserves direction and scale evidence separately. It does not
yet charge for retained structure or repeated shadow computation.

Before adding a large population, the next useful alternatives are to expose
real downstream demand, give comparable candidates a limited receiving budget,
and compare the cost of a current reread against a short retained result. Those
can test pressure toward dynamic recomputation without rebuilding the timing
stack or declaring a universal score for usefulness.
