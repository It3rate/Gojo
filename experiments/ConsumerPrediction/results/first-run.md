## Retained structure and limits

The learner retains three current levels/presence spans, one frozen pending issue, bounded support, persistent equations and one latest outcome. Historical CSV rows are observer output and never enter the learner. Pure forecasts are recalculated; there is no stored successful-value lookup. Capturing the pending issue is a causal commitment, not a long-term memory model. The latest outcome is diagnostic and does not drive the next prediction.

Allocation is cumulative construction during admissions, not simultaneous retained memory or a controlled benchmark. Selected `.gojo` files preserve native witnesses and final live worlds; their references to operational identities are live at capture time, while numeric issue/outcome premises are copied.

There is one fixed supplied shadow probe per run, no population search and no learned timing/segmentation. Eligibility is observed reversal, not prediction of a future reversal. The support law values signed error reduction at one supplied consumer tolerance. It does not price computation, trace retention, exploration, route maintenance or competing consumers. With no external demand, ability/support is retained and the route is silent; adverse no-demand observations therefore cannot penalize it under this protocol. Flat padding supplies valid no-turn data. Unknown separators explicitly reset readiness.

A retained route is not always an active publication: the demand/eligibility gate is zero when it has no present task. A withdrawn route retains its recipe and can be recruited again. These distinctions do not establish autonomous evolution or globally useful memory allocation.

All ready arrivals still calculate counterfactual diagnostics, including ineligible and undemanded ones. The gate filters learning and publication, not the cost of inspecting a quiet signal. The long quiet control therefore exposes extra allocation even while its eligible response trace and retained footprint remain unchanged. Restricting those reads to demanded opportunities would be a later attention/cost experiment, not a result already established here.

## Focused checks

- Known zero, absent input and bounded warmup remain distinct native reads.
- A prediction is issued before its outcome; recruitment affects a later issue only.
- Signed negative contributions include receiver tension and close without accumulating stale output.
- Demand and tolerance belong to the issued obligation, not the later observation.
- A missing immediate outcome remains unresolved and is never scored against a later known sample.
- Harm reduces support even when the baseline already satisfies the consumer.
- Identical candidate and baseline have zero native marginal response.
- Direction disagreement remains distinct from equal absolute scale.
- Repeated inspection cannot refresh support, change attachments or retarget input sites.
- Current forecasts recalculate from existing retargetable native sites.
- Captured issue numbers survive subsequent changes to the live predictor.
- Persistent equations and one pending issue do not accumulate an observation archive.
- Longer flat gaps add neither eligible reinforcement nor missed turn opportunities.
- Stale callers and invalid host tolerance cannot partially change the live world.
