# Can learning one printed fret help with the next?

Franz Sales Meyer’s *Handbuch der Ornamentik*, plate 81 figures 1 and 2, contains a construction grid followed by completed fret bands. Both figures were inspected to define the observer. Only figure 1 is available to train and select machines. Figure 2 is a held-out figure on the **same printed plate**, not a previously unseen object or a blind transcription. The test asks whether any features transfer to its more involved course.

## Transcription and calibration

We read the center between two banks printed for the broad fret. The two fine horizontal rules enclosing each band are excluded. Put the origin at the lower end of a long upward and read toward the right. Figure 1 gives N4, E4, S2, W2, S2, E4 and pitch 6. Figure 2 gives N6, E6, S4, W2, N2, W2, S4, E6 and pitch 8. Count divisions on the left construction grid and confirm both banks on the completed right-hand repeats. These are our selected orders through the surviving image. The original tool, chronological drawing order, absolute physical width and registration to a vessel are unknown.

Compute overlap on the exact vector segments. Align the selected origin and fit the printed period to the input pitch with one uniform scale on both axes. Do not independently stretch height, rotate, reflect or rephase to maximize a match. The historical score weights axial/diagonal unique-line intersection over union at 0.55, the best direction-sequence match on one uninterrupted owner in each bay at 0.20, realized common-site seams at 0.15, and unique/deposited length at 0.10. Course order uses longest common subsequence divided by mean sequence length. A distinct-site pen break begins a new sequence. Actual junctions, proper interior Xs and overlapping pairs are reported separately.

## Competition

Freeze this design and executable before training. Each of four replicates tries 32 generations of 16 proposals in three arms: Fig.1-informed start with the historical score, named library start with the historical score, and the same library start with the generic seven-criterion score. First-generation positions and immigrant opportunities are matched. Generic selection equally weights no-overdraw, connected-run, turn-variety, period-match, boundary-match, register-balance and gap-evenness. The Figure 1 control is legitimate supplied knowledge in the first arm. The Figure 2 reconstruction is excluded from all candidate definitions and search targets in every arm. Record every proposed byte string, its parent, and both scores.

Train on four A/B/pitch bindings (2/1/3, 3/1/5, 4/2/6, 4/3/7), with 3 and 9 vertical slots on alternating cases. Four training replicates shift initial clock phase. Select the best validation winner from six distinct finalists using four new A/B/pitch triples (6/3/9, 2/3/6, 5/2/8, 3/2/5). Charge all 6,144 proposals and 24,576 training drawings. Lock twelve finalist programs and their tension policies before any transfer scene is evaluated.

The external reference reads never act on a pen. Each winner runs under both its live and quiet local tension policy. Render figure 1 and figure 2 behavior for every finalist on twelve paired-case calibrations: four matched-shape (A=2B, pitch equal to the corresponding printed period scaled by B/2), four with A/B perturbed, and four with pitch perturbed. Compare narrow and wide slot affordances separately. All twelve combinations per figure are generated using a larger B than was used in training. These test lengths can be uniformly scaled versions of an earlier ratio; they do not prove generalization to additional design traditions. Compare the two exactly authored reconstructions as feasibility controls with a quiet actuator. Include an ink-identical reversed-chronology diagnostic and an added overlapping stroke.

**Predictions.** On figure 1 the printed-start historical arm will have higher mean historical utility than the generic arm (P1). On figure 2, without adaptation or refitting to that figure, we predict an advantage of the printed-start historical arm over generic (P2). We predict that the printed-start full equality of figure 1 will exceed its full equality of figure 2 (P3). We predict that the two authored controls will attain exact ink in their matched 4/4 cases under quiet execution (P4). We predict that live vs quiet will often change a line but will not consistently improve untrained transfer (P5). Average per parent across both slot settings and four search replicates. P1–P2 require signed margin 0.03 on at least 8/12 parents to support or contradict. P3 requires a positive difference in proportion exact in at least three matched-ratio parents. P4 requires all matched cases. P5 requires a real difference in material on at least six parents and fewer than eight parents improving *or* declining by the same margin when all finalist machines are pooled. Outcomes outside these rules are inconclusive, and a tie on exactness is reported explicitly.

The magnified plate figures are historical references, not generated output. Score and render the complete run. Show one figure-1 and one figure-2 nominal-basis comparison at a common scale, with the native action histories retained in the detailed records.
