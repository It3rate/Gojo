# Independent event supplier: first protocol

Exploratory, written before the trials. Continue the bounded native-structure
work without introducing a trolley or a general event taxonomy.

```text
public-port = group(event-basis [0,1], source-coordinate-axis, sequence-axis)
packet = group(public-port, copied sequence, directed source-support span,
               copied event reading or open slot, exposed residue)
subscription = group(public-port, source-to-receiver-clock correspondence,
                     event-to-receiver-basis correspondence)
consumer = group(existing continuation, subscription, conditional equation,
                 support, demand, policy, proposed route, participating route,
                 consumed sequence, latest local evidence)
receipt = group(packet or open input, receiver availability, subscription,
                freshness evidence, projected reading or unresolved read)
issue = group(existing issue, receipt, frozen counterfactual,
              frozen candidate or open slot, frozen export, participation)
```

The producer observes only its own input. An authored native predicate detects
opposite signs in two consecutive differences. The first two levels cannot
supply that predicate and publish an open reading. A complete zero reports that
the selected predicate did not fire; it is different from a missing packet.
The private calculation remains with the producer. The public packet has no
path to its private sensor, model or retained input trace.

Source support identifies the observations used, not an exact instant of
inflection. Source coordinate, publication sequence and receiver availability
have distinct carrier identities. The initial fixture explicitly calibrates
one source observation to one receiver observation, with zero offset. It claims
no physical sampling rate. A future observer could publish from a different
structural traversal or perspective through the same interface, provided its
basis and availability correspondence are supplied.

The consumer's first failed exported prediction opens a persistent opportunity
to test the supplied connection. It cannot reward a candidate for the failure
that caused recruitment. Subsequent fresh events can propose reversing the
primitive continuation. Native equation: `delta * (1 - 2*event)`. This is a
replacement continuation; its additive correction at event one is `-2*delta`.
Compare it with the receiver's **existing exported prediction**, including any
internal timing help, before assigning credit.

Utility in this pass is a declared demand for a better next-step direction.
Each fresh nonzero event costs one support tick to examine/maintain. A strictly
better future directional prediction earns two, so a useful opportunity nets
one; incorrect or redundant events net minus one. Support stays in [0,4] with
no hidden tension bank. Two support ticks permit an actual participation edge;
less removes it. Explicit zero and unavailable packets do not create reward or
event cost. Demand zero disables export and reward; fresh events then gradually
withdraw an unused connection. This is a supplied local cost and demand law,
not learned global utility or a universal decay constant.

Every baseline and candidate is frozen before the target observation. One
source sequence can be consumed at most once. Missing, stale, future or foreign
packets leave delivery unresolved while the independent base forecast remains
usable; these do not count as failed event predictions. Delayed physical signals
with fresh publications are different: their predictions are graded normally.
A packet supplied after issuance cannot rewrite the earlier issue.

The environment generates a driver process sequentially. In the leading case,
its current change drives the consumer's next change; the supplier sees only
the current driver observation. The consumer gets neither the driver value nor
its generating state. Controls use a physically lagging or independent observed
process through the exact same producer. No learner receives future arrays,
condition labels, seeds, target change, or hidden phase.

Before inspecting results, fix the same support policy and test three seeds
for: irregular leading input; one-step lagging input; unrelated input; a useful
channel becoming lagging; deterministic publication dropouts; and removal then
restoration of consumer demand. Also check regular input, where existing timing
may already explain turns and the extra supplier can be redundant.

Report all arrivals: existing versus combined directional agreement, improved
and harmed opportunities, event cost, additions/withdrawals, coverage, and final
support. Preserve the producer and consumer graph-size samples. Test boundaries,
missing versus zero, duplicate/late delivery, frozen forecasts, independent
private state, bounded reachable structure, and joint checkpoint continuation.
Do not tune support parameters to win these trials.
