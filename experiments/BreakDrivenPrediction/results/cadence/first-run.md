Regular and unknown-coverage use five-tick alternating runs; jitter draws each length from 4–6. Restart uses five through 180, flat through 360, eight through 540 and four thereafter, resetting a positive run at 361 and 541. Doubling uses five through 240, ten through 480 and twenty thereafter, resetting at 241 and 481. Long-period uses twenty throughout. Alternating-intervals repeats run lengths five and thirteen. Only jitter depends on its seed; other repeated rows are deterministic duplicates, not independent evidence. Event times are strict reversal detector observations.

Demand is width 3 through 240, zero at 241–360, width 12 at 361–480, width 1 at 481–600, then width 3. Both readers receive the same demand before admission. Unknown-coverage masks reports 252, 264, 276, 288 and 300 using an open occurrence slot and open coverage evidence; port, directed coverage span, timestamp and scalar endpoint remain exactly the published objects. Hidden fine events never enter either reader or its score. Startup known events remain visible with incomplete coverage and are conservatively unused.

New issues follow the current report's grading and are strictly later than its availability. Native selected-rung verdicts supply hits and misses; different widths are different promises. An absence miss has no invented event coordinate or zero timing error. Withholding a forecast is not a correct no-event prediction. The scope policy can reject a legitimate longer interval; its fixed allowance is not inferred uncertainty and has not been tuned from these results. Alternating intervals remain a challenge for a one-interval continuation.

Issued and assessed rung counts are structural work proxies, not total calculation or runtime. Scope checks, validation, absence evidence and attention control also cost work. Managed allocated bytes are the same-thread counter change around each native admission, all sixty calls including startup, in fixed scoped-then-attentive order. Construction, source acquisition/publication, demand assignment, reporting and graph capture are excluded. Positive/zero columns use current demand, so grading an older wanted promise during withdrawal remains included in zero-demand cost. This is a diagnostic, not a benchmark, CPU measurement or retained-memory estimate. Acquisition is fixed and unchanged.

`traces.csv` exposes native before/after fresh trace, remembered interval, expected coordinate and window, plus current scope break counts and prospective outcomes. Blank scope columns mean the attentive reference has no scope, not an inactive known scope. `boundaries.csv` lists the first issue strictly after each fixed inspection boundary, with its target and width; it does not assert those boundaries were visible to the learner. `graph-sizes.csv` samples retained source and reader structure every 120 ticks. Report history and pairing records are host apparatus; neither reader retains this archive.
