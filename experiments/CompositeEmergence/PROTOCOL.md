# Composite emergence: protocol and interpretation rules

Status: three exploratory pilots complete. Source and confirmation settings
were frozen on 2026-10-11 before launching fresh seed identities 200..207.
Development seeds are not confirmation evidence.

## Questions

1. Does later behavioral evidence select a native relational equation from
   initially inadequate single-channel alternatives?
2. Is the resulting publication informative about behavior while each
   primitive's complete permitted observation history is individually
   uninformative about the hidden apparatus rule?
3. Can a frozen learned service help a fresh consumer learn an opposite task
   wiring through its exposed port, without raw sensor ancestry?
4. Does intervention on that publication cause the predicted consumer change?
5. Can a second native learner choose paid refresh versus reuse over time,
   including after an unannounced reversal?
6. Do those advantages survive fair fixed, flat, shuffled, memory-removal and
   outcome-history comparisons? Which claim remains unsupported?

## World and information restrictions

- A fair direction `a` produces a later response `b=a*h`, with hidden
  convention `h=-1` or `+1`. Primitive A sees A alone; primitive B sees B alone.
- Neither primitive receives the task goal, action, global resource outcome,
  hidden convention, or the other channel. Its own local predictor may learn
  from its own channel only.
- Source learning receives the two advertised events, a genuinely issued
  exploratory action and its later physical direction consequence. It
  predicts before admitting that consequence.
- A consumer receives the feature publication and public goal, then its bound
  physical resource consequence. It cannot traverse raw A/B ancestry through
  the published boundary. The source retains that derivation privately.
- A manager receives current availability, coarse age and previous observed
  outcome class. It receives no hidden reversal announcement.
- Shared time/order, port contracts, candidate-building operations and reward
  laws are authored. A newly discovered pairing convention or arbitrary
  hierarchy is not claimed.

Correct actions produce `+8`, incorrect actions `-12`, and explicit holds `0`.
The isolated source/transfer stages count fresh acquisitions without charging a
probe fee. Refresh/reuse debits every attempted probe, including a failed
acquisition. Source pretraining and consumer training actions are reported
separately from deployment return.

## Stage A: useful native relation

Initially supplied candidates read current A, current B, previous A or previous
B. When their prequential prediction evidence is inadequate, one bounded
native expansion supplies pairwise product, sum and difference, including
current/prior source variations. Learned candidate prediction rows and
prequential correctness ledgers choose a retained relation with an explicit
complexity cost.

Controls train/evaluate on paired tapes:

- no expansion;
- unchanged initial parameters;
- independently shuffled consequence feedback;
- independently shuffled communication during training and evaluation;
- temporal retention withdrawn;
- previous-trial A paired with the current response.

Report prediction coverage, all-trial correctness and conditional correctness
when answered separately. An open prediction is not correct. Report candidate
count, selected native equation, feature-state count and update/receipt costs.
The same-trial sum and difference are informative too; product selection is
not the only valid success.

## Stage B: composition, intervention and transfer

Train a task consumer on the frozen source's coarse feature and goal. Compare
with a flat market reading A, B and goal, and with an authored fixed-sum
publication followed by the same consumer law. Neutral actions are a separate
untrained baseline.

Deploy the learned source under:

- longer delays, with ordered events still intact;
- independently unavailable signals;
- a same-state donor publication;
- an opposite-state donor publication;
- a missing publication;
- inverted B orientation.

Donors are valid feature coordinates previously produced by the same frozen
service. These publication interventions measure causal use and sensitivity.
The reversed/missing cases may be unfamiliar inputs. The retrained shuffled
communication arm is the complementary test of useful information with
preserved channel marginals.

For transfer, reverse the task wiring, freeze the reused source and train a
fresh consumer. Evaluate frozen consumers at 0, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 and the final
consumer-training milestone when those budgets fit the configured run.
Compare with:

- a fresh source and consumer learning jointly;
- a frozen inadequate/no-expansion source;
- a source trained with shuffled communication;
- a fresh flat A/B/goal consumer;
- a fixed-sum publication and fresh consumer.

The target action budget is paired. Source pretraining is an additional cost
for reused-service; the fresh-service arm changes both source and consumer
parameters on its target actions. That arm also experiences a changing feature
representation. Target sample-efficiency gains do not imply a total-budget
gain or an absolute representational advantage over the flat model.

## Stage C: refresh, reuse and first success after reversals

Calibrate an independent task consumer, then freeze it and the source. Train a
probe/reuse manager through its availability/age/previous-outcome contexts.
Evaluate frozen learned parameters on paired persistent-rule tapes.
Operational feature/outcome memory can continue changing during evaluation.

Compare learned refresh, always probe, never refresh after initialization,
an authored outcome-only retrodiction policy, probe after failure, and fixed
interval refresh. The outcome-only policy uses its permitted issued action,
goal and observed gross resource sign through a native product; it does not
receive `h` from the world and should not depend on the learned feature's sign
or state encoding.

Use immediate-history and delayed-history arms. In the delayed-history arm,
the operational prior-outcome context/retrodiction receives an older
consequence. Manager utility credit remains immediate during training. This
does not simulate every feedback route being delayed.

Frozen test conditions are baseline hazard, faster unannounced reversals,
more expensive probes and unavailable signals. A frozen changed-price test
measures robustness; it does not establish price-sensitive relearning.

Measure correctness, action coverage, probe rate, net return, and the time to
the first successful action after each reversal. Report intervals interrupted
by another reversal or ending without success separately. First success is
weaker than sustained recovery, and the conditional delay mean must not hide
censored intervals.
The runner stores a zero delay sentinel when no interval completed. Analysis
excludes that sentinel whenever `switches - unrecovered` is zero and reports
completed interval counts and omitted seed identities.

At immediate observation and hazard `q`, an ideal previous-outcome rule has
accuracy `1-q` and expected gross return `8-20q`; always probing at price `c`
has return `8-c`. The simple one-step cost boundary is `q=c/20`. This benchmark
is analyst arithmetic, not a learner input. With delayed outcome history the
benchmark changes; no immediate-history value is claimed for that arm.

## Analysis and verification

- Group result rows by stage, controller, condition, training milestone and
  seed. A seed appearing at several checkpoints is not several replicates.
- Report seed means and paired seed differences with descriptive 95% Student-t
  intervals. List all comparisons; exploratory multiplicity is not corrected.
- Check rate/count correspondence and the physical resource accounting
  identities, including explicit holds and every priced probe.
- Save learned native models and verify signatures, real post-restore
  publications/decisions, shared interface aliases and independent continued
  learning where applicable. A description or row-count match alone is not
  behavioral replay.
- Guard frozen learned projections while allowing declared operational memory
  and latest-read witnesses to evolve.
- Verify by graph reachability that a consumer's advertised service boundary
  withholds the raw A/B carriers and private producer ancestry.
- Measure retained graph sites and transport bytes separately. Model footprint,
  historical audit cost and run time are different quantities.
- Run focused structural tests and the repository solution test gate.

## Claim boundary

Evidence can support learned relational selection, reusable publication and
particular temporal resource decisions. The individual primitive interfaces
lack the joint information required by the task. A flat model given both
channels can express the same solution. The finite supplied grammar and
authored hierarchy do not establish subjective comprehension, invented goals,
unrestricted recursive synthesis or open-ended higher-order intelligence.

## Pilot findings and confirmation freeze

| Run | Seeds | Budgets: source / consumer / source test / manager train / temporal test | Probe price | Finding used for the next decision |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| pilot-01 | 0 | 320 / 96 / 128 / 384 / 256 | 1 | Relational discovery and publication intervention worked. The delayed-history manager failed to acquire a useful refresh policy with the short training budget. |
| pilot-02 | 1, 2 | 384 / 192 / 256 / 2,048 / 768 | 1 | Longer training resolved the delayed-history failure by largely choosing to probe every time. Immediate reuse had only a 0.2-unit ideal advantage at hazard 0.04, which was a weak distinction for finite aged evidence. |
| pilot-03 | 3, 4 | 384 / 192 / 256 / 2,048 / 768 | 2 | Both immediate-history managers mostly reused, with about 3% queries and 97% correctness. Delayed-history policies differed: one queried about 25%, the other almost always. This variability remains part of the question. |

The primary probe price was set to 2 to make the investigate/reuse consequence
more distinguishable without changing the learner law or its coarse age
buckets. The confirmation increases independent seeds and source/test budgets;
it retains the 2,048-action manager-training budget. All earlier pilots remain
available, including their failures and historical source hashes.

Transport verification was strengthened after pilot-02: actual restored
feature/prediction/action/query behavior and shared pipeline aliases replace
the earlier shape checks. Pilot-03 has six passing replay checks. An archive
does not reproduce the external world or the discarded historical audit.
