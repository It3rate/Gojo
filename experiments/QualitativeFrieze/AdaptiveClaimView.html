When a local control needs more

A useful question can stop being readable. A legal step can keep overshooting. A control may not yet know what its own motion does. These experiments give each problem a small, explicit response—and then connect the responses.

1 · Ask a different questionRetain a landmark-separation read when the section-gap claim cannot answer.
2 · Try a finer siblingA response reversal can permit smaller steps, while preserving the coarse state and its read.
3 · Observe a local effectCompare candidate motion words with a controlled before/after change.
