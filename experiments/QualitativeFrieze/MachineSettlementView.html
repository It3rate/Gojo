    What the seams mean

    The two paths have independent native sites. The study explicitly asks for a shared view at their starting and ending sites. A formal position read admits or blocks each declaration. An admitted declaration produces a connected quotient view while preserving the original paths.

    That is stronger than noticing that two pixels coincide, and weaker than claiming a material weld or a new flow passage. No other coincident sites are silently merged.

    A flat extent of zero still has a phase and two distinct sites. The chevron pair can therefore look like a diamond while retaining all six original sections.

    Where we supplied the guidance

    • The seed phases and their reflected frame are authored.
    • A shared step unit, capacities, goals, and observation contracts are supplied.
    • The hold, clock, and feedback response laws are supplied alternatives.
    • The disturbance schedule and outcome checks belong to the experiment.
    • General shape discovery, automatic topology changes, and arbitrary geometric clearance remain outside this pass.

    The value of this study is being able to see exactly which supplied connections helped, which could not respond, and which kept changing without settling.
