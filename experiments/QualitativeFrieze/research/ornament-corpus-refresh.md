# Interior silhouette and detail: corpus review

This is an authored composition experiment. Native structure retains the
scaffold, actual source ports, introduced words, transform images, attachment
witnesses and curved-display selection. The choices below do not establish a
learned aesthetic law or automatic conceptual decomposition.

The review distinguishes a small decorative mark from a detailed motif. A
large coherent silhouette can occupy most of a compartment and still serve as
detail of the larger lattice. Detail needs internal articulation and a useful
relationship to its parent; sparse narrow leaves do not become finer ornament
merely by having less visual mass.

The other modes of those sources retain their previous constructions.

## Native fit and limitations

The new focused tests check all introduced vertices against their actual
convex parent compartment in B03, B04, B06, B06.B, B16, B19, B22, B23, B24,
B28 and B29. This guards both enlargement and attachment to the correct
sibling. The four B30 flowers use broad 80-by-56-reach silhouettes fitted under
the actual main-lobe profiles, with separated occupied bounding boxes across
the authored staggered courses. The initial 44-reach flowers still looked weak
at field scale; widening the same shape to 80 reaches improved their relation
to the 192-reach arch interval without adding conceptual parts.
B30's finer native unit contains 224 finite chords, up from the earlier
200-chord catalogue budget; the additional work is explicit structure.

B32/B33/B34 tests retain each source path and source port by identity. The
overlaid ornament creates no new synthetic port on a source crossing. A broad
flower may visually hide a course, while the angular construction view still
exposes it. Rendering prominence is not a topological join.

The eight-lobed flower is a single shaped contour with an eight-way vein
family and a seed. The six-petal flower uses actual transformed leaf images.
These are deliberately different native recipes for related visual roles.
One useful conceptual read can therefore be much smaller than a count of its
paths, chords or repeated petal instances. Conceptual cost estimates belong
to an explicitly selected grammar and are not claims about discovered human
perceptual units.

Curved display remains an interpretation of selected finite guides. It does
not introduce a native analytic curvature law, filled-face inference,
automatic collision avoidance or over/under knot topology.
