Growth · cadence · counterform

Attachment is a beginning.

A valid offset witness says where an addition comes from. The authored recipe must also give it a coherent direction, parent, repetition and room to breathe. These studies use shared finite prototypes and explicit mirror, translation, rotation or angled substitutions. Each plate executes after native archive import; the close-up and repeated field inspect the same acknowledged paths.

Scaffold, ornament and finer internal marks remain separate, connected native selections. The woven and knot-like studies preserve actual interrupted strands; a visible gap does not prove unseen continuity or knot topology. All contours are finite chords. Colour, filled silhouettes and outlines remain display choices.
