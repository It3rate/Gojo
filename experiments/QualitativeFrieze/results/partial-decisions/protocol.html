Partial decisions across complete passes: prospective protocol

The question

Can a small controller keep working without first knowing which construction explains its input, then use the result of a complete pass to improve its next choice? This extends the three-adaptation experiment. It does not replace uncertainty with an unqualified guess or treat every useful guess as discovery of a physical law.

We distinguish three kinds of evidence:

  1. Shared consequence: every remaining, supported candidate admits the same response in the selected read. Their identities remain different.
  2. Provisional action: a visible choice selects one offered continuation; the competing explanations remain. A choice is an operation, not a new fact.
  3. Whole-pass utility: a selected local discrepancy improved, stalled or worsened during a bounded pass. This may change a route preference, but does not isolate causal credit or eliminate motion candidates.

                                                       |

Each height/width control has its own goal read, candidate scope, choice memory and trial allowance. The host supplies clock opportunities, the finite candidate bank, geometry and experimental conditions. It does not inspect a private correct motion word to select the next response.

The quiet grant covers only the other control's responses routed through its advertised gate. It does not prove that the entire outside world was still. Blocked and confounded trials cannot identify a motion word.

Whole passes

Carry the actual control values and pending tension from one pass into the next. Do not reset a failed attempt or silently undo its effects. Complete a bounded sequence before evaluating that controller's local discrepancy. A supplied finite policy may keep an option after improvement or achievement, and advance through the declared option order after a stall or regression. Unavailable evaluation remains unresolved.

Keep two recent pass evaluations operationally, with explicit evidence that older detail was dropped. A separate report may retain more for inspection; the controller must not consult that diagnostic archive. Motion identification and pass utility remain separate memories with different truth conditions.

Changing a selected goal/context creates an explicit new evaluation context. Old evidence is retained as old-context evidence, not silently compared with a different target. Movement may also change which section reads are available. That is a change in accessible perspective; it is not automatically a rewrite of the drawing's topology. A changed selected response route must remain visible.

Comparisons and predicted limits

The campaign crosses nine supplied situations, five modes (unique explanation, shared consequence, fixed provisional choice, pass-revised choice, and combined choice/trials), and two construction families: 90 runs. Each run carries its state through six passes of six beats. Equivalent-word cases receive the same two actual calibration interventions before the passes so that response agreement can be isolated from initial motion identification.

  • Unique-model control versus a shared-response read, retaining two indistinguishable constructions.
  • A fixed provisional choice versus pass-level revision, starting with a poor offered option and retaining all physical consequences.
  • Finite local probes versus no probes, including positive capacity blockage, delayed or refused quiet grants, and a deliberately confounded trial.
  • Repeated passes after a goal changes, and after the primary section reader becomes unavailable while a landmark comparison remains possible.
  • The same controller grammar on inset and chevron constructions. Their calibrations and candidate words remain supplied; cross-family success does not establish automatic metaphor discovery.

Success is not only final settlement. Record provisional versus shared acts, useful versus uninformative trials, acknowledgements, remaining candidates, accepted movement, held tension, changes in reader availability, pass outcomes and the size of retained operational memory. A negative result is useful when the connected reason is visible.

No global aesthetic score, arbitrary program synthesis, continuous material deformation, universal causal isolation or autonomous sensor discovery is claimed. The target is a small, inspectable decision mechanism that can make and revise bounded choices while its world continues to change.
