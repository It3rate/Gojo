Keep the questions separate: does it share a site, continue as a strand, sit in front of another, belong to one object, or permit transfer? A useful cue for one question does not answer all the others.

Open read evidence counts missing or unresolved cue reads. A completely supported cue can still have several open interpretations.

What this round establishes and leaves open

    Inputs expose native sites, strokes, formal unmeasured words and actual incidence. No renderer coordinates or image parsing feed the reader. The source is never rewritten when a candidate is selected.

    Junction cues can support completion, while context can change the interpretation. See Tse and Albert (1998). Our proposal rules are a bounded engineering experiment rather than a model of human perception.

    Connection counts name their basis: sites with multiple incident strokes, incident stroke pairs, connected source components, or components under an explicitly selected observational continuation. None supplies a material transfer law.
