04 / What the encoding carries

Keep the evidence connected

The glyph name is not an instruction. Native section endpoints, ordered visits, ports and laws carry the distinctions. A reader can inspect one level while leaving another open.

ODV still describes the selected magnitude read. V = 0 means undetectable at that resolution. It does not mean a missing section or an unselected stroke. Here a joint needs connected sites and laws; it is not compressed into one new joint bit.

A bend and an attachment may have identical final ink and connectivity. Their difference survives only if the construction history or different response laws survive. The same applies to a bridge that was drawn in one direction but is later usable in both.

Pen-stroke groups record drawing history. They do not supply material stiffness or bolt, hinge or weld laws.

What this pass does not establish

It does not discover handwriting plans, learn meanings from images, certify mechanical balance, or supply a universal workspace or force solver. It gives concrete native structures, executed finite route probes and explicit counterexamples that a later learner can compare.

Uses and interactions can broaden a concept beyond its isolated outline. That is a useful direction for Gojo; a complete concept is not guaranteed by collecting a list of associations.
