02 · REFINING THE OBSERVATION

The same motifs can occupy different places

The original motif and grid readers see exactly the same segment observation in every state below. A supplemental reader receives only ordered projection buckets and explicit endpoint-box constraints. It asks whether each known line motif lies strictly inside each axis-aligned rectangular interval chamber.

The arrangement question supplies one motif slot per chamber. Empty and competing slots describe that chosen goal relative to the selected finite motif family; they do not declare an ornament universally defective.

Ordinal equality is equal projection, never an automatic join. Strict placement concerns line support in a product of two intervals, conditional on the supplied passage constraints. It does not infer filled regions, arbitrary polygon interiors or a metric shape. Endpoint order alone cannot constrain an unseen bowing passage.

03 · A CLAIM AND ITS DEPENDENCIES

Keep the pattern, test its bindings

The original native motif definitions and occurrence bindings remain fixed. One local heading or passage observation changes; each obligation is reapplied without rediscovering or relabelling the whole field. Missing from a closed inventory and unavailable in an open inventory yield different results.

The ring marks the probed segment. The old geometric diagram remains a guide; a changed heading does not borrow its old direction from the drawing. Refutation belongs to this occurrence at these bindings. It does not erase the reusable motif, and unrelated matches remain available.
