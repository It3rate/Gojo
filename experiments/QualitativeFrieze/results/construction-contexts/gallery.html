C05 · observation becomes construction

One field, three useful organizations

The source has 48 actual triangular chambers. Courts, overlapping vertex diamonds and row parents select different groups from that same source. Each view's exposed boundary-contact network is consumed by new acknowledged drawing flow. The same eight-chord ornament program is then placed at the published group landmarks.

Stripes mark shared chamber participation. Grey is the original field, shown as a guide; teal is accepted construction. Dots mark actual constructed endpoint roles. A bridge gap marks a projected crossing without a joined site. Shared observation alone never creates a passage.

What to look for: useful context can be a current editing surface, a correspondence to a coarser construction, or a remembered route. It can also be unnecessary for the present drawing. Changing the chosen view changes the units available to the next construction.

Interpretation and limits

    The square-like drawings use a chosen equal-axis display. Formal shared reaches and cancellation are native evidence; perpendicular screen axes and Euclidean appearance are supplied by this renderer. Grouping proposals are authored in this round.

    Native structure is relations and placeholders. Numerical screen coordinates and colours are added only for display. Structural counts are observations of these constructions, rather than a claim about general recognition speed or human salience.
