What this establishes

Execution and correspondence use native relations and shared unmeasured reaches, with no numeric carriers or numeric locations. The request laws and embedding are authored premises. Screen geometry is an optional interpretation, used for display and bridge gaps only.

Neighbour requests are small structural signals with visible held demand, not continuous calibrated tension. Recursive placements retain dependencies and detect stale sources; automatic propagation after later source revisions is still open. Chamber queries validate an explicitly supplied finite embedding; they do not discover all faces in an arbitrary picture. Withdrawal changes a derived connection view and retains original history.
