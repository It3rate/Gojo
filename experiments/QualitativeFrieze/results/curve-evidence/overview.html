What must a curve description preserve?

Straight passages, bows, tangent joins, cusps and missing spans are separate pieces of information. A coarse overall reading can anchor a fine local exception without replacing it. These cases compare several concrete receiver interpretations of native evidence; the drawings do not feed a pixel classifier.

Premises and limits

schema: curve-evidence-study-v1

scope: Native polynomial curve mechanics and authored reconstruction on supplied anonymous endpoints; no dataset import, bitmap recognition, learned likelihood or measured curve parameters.

dependence: One pooled estimated compatibility event per source; macro envelope, tangent, bend, join and micro reads are not independent copies of the source evidence.

archive: Separate evaluator source/correspondence branch and public receiver branch. Private handles are retained for audit, never supplied to reconstruction or assessment.

interior: Explicit oriented-boundary premise; closure does not imply convexity or establish interior.

C001 · Straight despite a flowing context

A weak preference for flowing forms is revised by explicit straightness evidence; the object label never supplies geometry.

C002 · A bow without a measured bulge

Curved is communicated, but tangent directions and bend side are withheld. Different bows remain allowed.

C003 · Endpoints alone leave line and curve open

The source is bowed, but the receiver gets endpoints without trace or tangent evidence. Its sampled curve is a hypothesis.

C004 · An inward curved notch

The top section bends opposite to the selected interior orientation. Closure is not a convexity rule.

C005 · Four smooth bows make an oval

Four polynomial sections share tangent joins and a closed site cycle. Their circular appearance does not establish an exact circle.

C006 · Capsule: straight runs and curved ends

The line/curve transitions can be tangent without equal handle lengths or curvature continuity.

C007 · Rounded rectangle

Eight explicit sections keep straight intervals separate from curved corners; a smooth join does not erase the section identities.

C008 · Pointed lens: corners at curved tips

Two curved pieces close with finite-angle corners. A point is not automatically a tangent-reversal cusp.

C009 · A true tangent-reversal cusp

At the right tip, the outgoing forward tangent reverses the incoming one. This differs from both smooth continuation and a finite-angle corner.

C010 · S connection with an inflection

One span has opposed bend signs and a regular derivative certificate. A single convex/concave label would discard its important structure.

C011 · Arch meeting a straight base

The closed contour mixes a bow with a straight section; its two joins are corners, not accidental smoothing instructions.

C012 · Two curves share a forward tangent

The middle join is smooth even though curvature and interpolation speed are not promised to agree.

C013 · Same middle site, different tangent

The next curve leaves downwards instead of continuing rightwards, producing a corner at the shared landmark.

C014 · An observed gap remains open

A closing continuation is offered as an explicitly assumed passage. The source still has an observed gap and is never silently rewritten.

C015 · A requested closed-parent reading

The same kind of open evidence is read with a separate completion goal. A preferred bridge is an action proposal, not newly observed truth.

C016 · Coincident endpoints are separate sites

The two endpoints occupy the same word position but retain distinct identities. Coordinate coincidence does not establish closure.

C017 · Crossing paths without a joint

Two independent line paths cross in the drawing. No shared midpoint site or branch is invented.

C018 · Closed, with interior orientation withheld

The oval closes, but convex/concave readings remain unbound because its interior-side premise is not communicated.

C019 · A vanishing first handle

A tangent-limit read is possible, while the current sufficient regularity certificate remains open. Unknown is not singularity proof.

C020 · Collinear support can still retrace

The controls lie on one line, but the trace can reverse within the parameter interval. Straight support alone is too coarse to promise no backtracking.

C021 · Mirrored bows and interior side

Reflection reverses signed turning and the oriented interior together, retaining the same physical convexity read.

C022 · Reversed traversal, same oval boundary

Section order, endpoint roles and tangent directions reverse; the interior side also reverses, preserving local convexity.

C023 · R095: diamond envelope with stepped detail

Four outer tip landmarks support a weak diamond-like envelope. The original stepped/notched boundary remains a separate detailed read, not a failed diamond.

C024 · Mixed flowing stem and angular lobe

A small ornamental scaffold combines a curved stem with an angular branch. Its multiway join leaves the continuation choice explicit.
