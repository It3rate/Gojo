G01Continuous key

A compact target for ordered turns, repeat continuity and legitimate returns.

S01S03
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • The key path continues across the repeat boundary.
  • Returning paths use distinct occupied edges.

Can vary

  • Stretch the key's proportions.
  • Vary the band and passage widths.

A miss: A convincing isolated key whose next repeat breaks the path or retraces an edge.

G02Facing meanders

Two related path systems frame a shared interior passage.

S03S09
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • The two meanders retain distinct path identities.
  • Their inward-facing returns bound a recurring central passage.

Can vary

  • Change the gap between the meanders.
  • Use longer or shallower returns.

A miss: Two unrelated rows that happen to sit near each other without a recurring facing relationship.

G03Glide staircase

A repeated stepped path exposes how reflection and displacement cooperate.

S03S04
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Neighbouring steps follow a declared reflection-and-displacement correspondence.
  • The resulting passages continue through the repeat.

Can vary

  • Change the staircase's rise and run.
  • Change the separation of opposing steps.

A miss: An alternating staircase without a consistent glide correspondence between its repeated parts.

G04Half-drop diamonds

A sparse field makes phase, neighbourhood and repeat closure easy to inspect.

S02S04
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Alternate rows follow one shared half-period promise.
  • Both repeat directions preserve the declared neighbour correspondence.

Can vary

  • Stretch the diamond proportions.
  • Change the gaps without changing neighbour order.

A miss: Rows appear staggered locally but drift out of phase when the field repeats.

G05Nested diamond courts

Nested courts combine enclosing cycles, radial correspondence and intervening passages.

S07S09
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Each court retains nested boundary cycles.
  • The intervening bands preserve their declared connections.

Can vary

  • Vary court proportions and band widths.
  • Change the number of nested boundaries.

A miss: Nested outlines that collapse a required passage or lose their shared court correspondence.

G06Triangular chambers

A repeated triangular subdivision tests compound cells and subordinate enclosure.

S05S09
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • The intended triangular chambers have closed boundaries.
  • Shared edges and enclosing bands retain their declared incidence.

Can vary

  • Stretch triangles without changing incidence.
  • Change the depth of the enclosing bands.

A miss: A triangular-looking fragment whose chamber opens unintentionally at a neighbouring repeat.

G07Octagon and square field

The gap shapes become indispensable members of the repeated structure.

S02S06
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Eight-edge principal cells alternate with the intended four-edge gap cells.
  • Shared boundaries close across both repeat directions.

Can vary

  • Vary edge lengths within compatible cells.
  • Exchange which cell family receives visual emphasis.

A miss: Octagons are repeated while the intervening gaps are omitted or left structurally unresolved.

G08Counterchanged steps

One stepped partition supports useful motif and field readings.

S03S10
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Both roles derive from the same bounded partition.
  • A supplied role-exchange correspondence preserves the intended adjacency.

Can vary

  • Change the two roles' physical areas.
  • Stretch the steps while preserving their order.

A miss: Similar ink totals conceal different connectivity, or an empty complement is mistaken for a corresponding pattern.

G09Angular woven band

Independent continuity and local crossing order create complexity from a small path vocabulary.

S03S08
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Every thread remains traceable through each crossing.
  • The declared over/under sequence agrees at the repeat boundary.

Can vary

  • Vary thread spacing and bend lengths.
  • Exchange the threads' visual emphasis.

A miss: A crossing silently merges two threads, or the crossing sequence contradicts itself on repetition.

G10Herringbone passages

Alternating slants produce a directional field with recurring angular passages.

S02S03
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Opposing slants recur in a consistent chevron sequence.
  • Passages and selected edges continue through neighbouring repeats.

Can vary

  • Change the apparent lean.
  • Stretch the branches and intervening passages.

A miss: Locally attractive elbows accumulate inconsistent direction or disconnected passages across the field.

G11Sixfold star corridors

A shared radial organization can coordinate star centres and the corridors between them.

S05S07
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Each centre has six ordered corresponding sectors.
  • Neighbouring sector boundaries retain the intended corridor connections.

Can vary

  • Vary the star's inner and outer reach.
  • Change corridor width under a compatible interpretation.

A miss: Six rays look balanced around isolated centres while their inter-centre corridors fail to connect.

G12One scaffold two readings

Alternative selections expose how one scaffold can support different coherent readings.

S02S05S10
Target and allowed variation

Required

  • Both readings retain correspondence to one shared scaffold.
  • Their selected paths and complementary cells remain separately inspectable.

Can vary

  • Choose different supported edge selections.
  • Change which reading is emphasized visually.

A miss: Two similar pictures are asserted to share a scaffold without a participant correspondence.
