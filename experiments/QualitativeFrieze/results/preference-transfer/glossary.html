Machines in the preference-transfer experiment

The short version

The source learns an option preference. A correspondence connects that option to an option in a new machine. The new machine tries it provisionally, acts through a complete pass, evaluates its own discrepancy, and keeps or changes its preference. Source evidence, target evidence and the analogy between them remain separate structures.

Reading the replay

H and W are the two local controls. The viewer numbers options from one; the source code indexes them from zero. An option number has meaning only in its local option domain. The arrow between source and target option numbers shows the actual supplied correspondence, not a claim that matching numbers are equivalent.

Preference after evaluation is the option that the next pass will try first. It is not necessarily the option just used, and after a failed pass it may not yet have useful evidence of its own.

Two candidates means two possible motion descriptions remain. It does not mean two physical commands were sent. A provisional choice selects one allowed response without deleting the competing explanation.

Gap is the primary geometric section read. Landmark gap can survive when a section disappears. The viewer's diagnostic value is not automatically available to the controller; the withheld case makes that separation explicit.

Tension is unabsorbed change, not a score. In the reversed experiment, height 12 with tension +1 must receive one negative step before its visible height can decrease. Pass utility is a separate comparison of before/after discrepancy in one context.

The preference cursor has its own wrapped carrier. Crossed complete cycles remain visible there as whole-cycle tension; they are not actuator debt and are not included in the height/width tension plot. Restoring that valid wrapped state preserves the cycles instead of clearing them or rejecting the memory.

Jointly settled means the report found the desired primary gap and endpoint coincidence with zero pending height and width tension. It is a diagnostic read of both goals, not a new global controller.

What this vocabulary does not imply

There is no new hexagon, diamond or general analogy-learning machine here. The shape templates, options, mappings and keep/advance law are supplied. The new capability is accountable preference transfer and target-local revision. A mechanically valid mapping can fail in practice, and a useful pass does not identify a unique motion law.
