Machine composition and settlement: prospective protocol

What this experiment is for

Two small drawing structures can expose the same outline while responding differently to change. This experiment asks whether a narrow feedback connection helps two separately owned extent machines agree, and what their changing extents do to several connected drawing templates. The machine glossary explains the parts rather than using the final shape's name as an explanation.

This protocol is written before running the new campaign. It is a bounded mechanism study, not a test of human aesthetic preference or general learning.

The connected proposal

desired extent -> exposed comparison sensor <- first owner's published extent
                                 |
                     less / equal / greater / open
                                 |
                      first owner's finite response law
                                 |
                      signed unit input -> local extent

first owner's published extent -> comparison sensor <- second owner's extent
                                           |
                               second owner's response law
                                           |
                               signed unit input -> local extent

each owner's exposed extent -> a bound repeat in its drawing template
paired drawings -> seam, symmetry and opening reads

The observer may compare advertised values. The receiving controller gets a coarse comparison, not a hidden target coordinate. Observation does not consume the source. A signed command is an explicitly supplied adjustment request, not conserved ink or space. Capacity and unmet requests remain separate from the comparison residual.

The paired drawing uses actual ordered phases and repeated common-unit participation. A geometric realization interprets those words. Equal endpoint coordinates alone do not weld two independently identified ends.

Declared comparisons

The same controlled inputs will be applied to disconnected/hold, fixed-clock, and feedback laws. A second comparison will withhold or delay observations. The drawing templates will include a diagonal cap, a cardinal cap, and an inward-turning hook. Their names are report labels; the executor must use their connected instructions.

The planned disturbances are a changed desired span, unequal initial extents, an unavailable observation, an ambiguous observation, a delayed comparison, and a desired extent outside a receiver's capacity. A change from a positive flat span to zero must retain the participating phase and native endpoint identities. A diamond-like read at that limit must not claim that the six-phase construction became a four-edge topology.

Predictions and failure criteria

  1. With a reachable constant target and fresh comparisons, a bidirectional one-unit feedback law should reach the selected extent. Its cooperating follower should subsequently agree. Fixed-clock activity is not expected to stop reliably at arbitrary targets.
  2. Missing or ambiguous comparisons must not be replaced by target values. They should stop correction or retain an unresolved support, depending on the encoded law.
  3. Delayed comparisons may cause overshoot or oscillation. This is a useful failure, not permission to refit the target after every step.
  4. Capacity-limited requests must leave an unsatisfied goal and visible blocked change. A visually acceptable result cannot erase these failures.
  5. A native law and its supported compact compilation must produce the same admitted transitions. Save/restore must preserve independent owner state, phase, unresolved pressure and subsequent behavior.
  6. One retained feedback connection may be justified by repeatable local observations in its tested scope. Reuse on fresh disturbances is separate evidence; neither the candidate law nor its discovery grammar is learned from nothing.

How much guidance is supplied

The experiment supplies the seed instructions, public sensors, step unit, response-law candidates, local capacity, goals, event schedule and correction outlets. It does not supply intermediate shape coordinates to the controller. The renderer, target silhouette and global quality scores must be inaccessible to the controller. This supplied-guidance inventory is reported with the results; additional rules needed to cure a failure must be named explicitly.

An interpolating animation by itself is not evidence of settlement. Every displayed intermediate state must come from an actual admitted local response. The target is expressed through selected exposed extents, not inferred from an arbitrary picture. General correspondence discovery, curved clearance, topology editing and spontaneous growth of new routes remain outside this pass.

Evidence to retain

Record inputs, publication supports, phase, selected law, requested change, accepted change, carrier tension, remaining mismatch and the actual ordered drawing output. Report agreements and failures separately from appearance. Measure transition count, changed states, transmitted comparison symbols, stored representation size and diagnostic history cost separately. A saved history is not all the live machine's operational state.

Keep representative runs with full diagrams and step inspection, and a compact summary of the full bounded sweep. The Markdown account and the HTML report must explain the same scope and limitations.
