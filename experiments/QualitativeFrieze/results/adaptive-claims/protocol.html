Three local adaptations: prospective protocol

What we are testing

The last study found three useful limits. A section can disappear from a gap reader's view. A legal step can repeatedly cross a goal without reaching it. And each control was given its movement law in advance. This pass tests a small response to each limit, then connects them in the same construction.

The apparatus, candidate laws, connections and test conditions are specified before the campaign. This is not autonomous discovery of arbitrary machines or learned ornament taste.

1. Keep a different question available

Connect a section-gap claim and a landmark-separation claim to an explicit first-preference selector. The second asks about projected separation between actual advertised landmarks; it does not certify uniform clearance between nonzero sections. Retain both claims and their witnesses. Withhold or obscure both publications together in the unavailable/ambiguous controls.

Prediction: the alternative can continue after a section collapses, and can sometimes bring disjoint sections back into the first reader's domain. It cannot make the original premise true by renaming its own result.

2. Try a finer sibling after a response reversal

Connect successive comparison publications and the previous accepted actuator step to a finite sign law. An opposed response after a real accepted move permits a trial of a supplied finer rung. This is a reason to try, not proof that resolution caused the reversal. Changing goals can also reverse responses.

The fine control has an independently identified carrier with three smaller ticks per coarse tick. Copy the exposed coarse value by an explicit observation import, retaining its calibration and source snapshot. This is non-draining observation, not duplicated conserved material. Refuse activation when pending coarse tension or insufficient fine capacity would be lost.

Width and height must earn their own refinement trigger. Keep the original coarse state and provide a coarse read of the fine state with exact residue. Use a fixed common fine drawing basis from the beginning, so changing rungs cannot visually resize the object or quietly change the desired gap.

Prediction: the inset's gap between coarse steps becomes reachable on the supplied finer rung. A capacity refusal stays visible. Refinement is not a universal fix for incompatible claims or lost observations.

3. Read motion from a controlled change

Give each actuator a finite scope of candidate positive-motion words. Compare their predicted displacement with actual before/after exposed landmark words and the actuator's accepted extent change. Retain matches, contradictions and open evidence. Candidate elimination acts on connected observations, not a shape-name label or rendered pixels.

The host supplies one positive trial per actuator/rung, with an explicit local trial allowance. During an ordinary isolated trial the other extent is held. A deliberately confounded trial changes both inputs and exposes both in its changed-input scope. A blocked trial changes no value. Neither establishes a motion law. Distinct candidate words with the same displacement stay distinct; the apparatus does not choose the first one merely to continue.

Only a unique surviving supported candidate can supply the later control's motion word. Each finer actuator needs new evidence in its own tick basis. Isolation and landmark correspondence remain supplied experimental premises.

Campaign and comparisons

  • Alternative read: four situations, two modes, two seeds (16 runs).
  • Refinement: three situations, two modes, two seeds (12 runs).
  • Motion evidence: four situations, two modes, two seeds (16 runs).
  • Composition: four situations, five ablations, two seeds (40 runs).

The combined campaign has 84 runs of 48 supplied beats, with an initial frame. Record original and alternative claim truth, selected support, both extents, pending tension, active rung, retained coarse residue, candidate support and trial/activation witnesses. Report each local result separately. A geometric match never welds sites, and a finite run is not a convergence theorem.

Controls include missing observations, collapsed/disjoint sections, a blocked trial, confounded evidence, equal-displacement candidates and insufficient fine capacity. The combined ablations compare baseline, alternative only, refinement only, observed motion only and all three.

Native and compiled laws must agree on representative complete traces. Saved candidate memories and rung activations must restore their actual behavior, not merely their scalar summaries. Interactive HTML and repository Markdown will show the goal and mechanism before the detailed results.
