Changing the question, the resolution, and the motion assumption

What this experiment does

Our two controls can now try three small adaptations. If a section-gap read fails, a separate landmark reader can remain useful. If legal steps keep crossing the goal, a supplied finer control can be admitted. If a motion rule is not supplied to the controller, a controlled trial can filter a finite set of candidate rules.

The three mechanisms work separately and together. They also fail differently, which matters: an alternative question does not fix inadequate resolution; a finer control does not make a confounded observation informative; and a successful landmark comparison does not make a missing section reappear.

Open the interactive replay, or see the machine glossary and predictions written before the campaign.

The campaign has 84 conditions and 4,116 recorded frames. These are supplied small constructions and test interventions. The bounded learning component selects among supplied motion words; it does not discover arbitrary programs, learn ornamental taste, or decide where to attach its own sensors.

Three good places to start

Alternative read → Sections do not overlap → Allow landmark alternative. The original section-gap reader cannot answer. The landmark reader supplies a correction which brings the sections back into overlap. Once the original question becomes readable, its response takes precedence again.

Refinement → Goal between coarse steps → Permit finer sibling. The inset path keeps stepping across its requested gap. Height gets a finer sibling, then width earns its own refinement. Both can eventually settle. The old coarse values remain visible next to current coarse reads of the finer values.

All together → Goal between coarse steps → All three mechanisms. Begin with no selected motion correspondence. Each control receives a supplied isolated trial, then uses its surviving candidate. When a finer rung activates, that new control needs another trial in its own tick basis. Its old motion word is not inherited by assumption.

The unchanged drawing basis

This study draws everything in one common fine reach basis from the start. One coarse actuator tick contributes three fine reaches; a fine actuator tick contributes one. The inset construction uses its height role three times, so one coarse height step moves the selected boundary by nine fine reaches.

The previous gap example asked for 10 coarse reaches and could only alternate between 9 and 12. Here the same relationship is displayed as target 30 fine reaches, with coarse outcomes 27 and 36. Refinement keeps the target fixed. It changes the available step, not the meaning of the goal or the display scale.

Four explicit affine maps connect actual upper/lower width/height ports to the common drawing basis. Each observation retains the live port, captured source sample, projection and any residue. Only exact whole-reach projections can supply this bounded path renderer; a fractional or unsupported projection remains open. Reading does not mutate the controls.

The height and width controls have separate memories, gates and rungs. Both observe the same construction before feedback responses are applied. During an isolated trial, the other control is held. A separate negative control changes both and exposes both inputs in the trial's changed-input scope.

The host supplies the trial protocol and one native allowance per actuator per rung. It also supplies the correspondence between successive observations of the selected landmark. These are experimental premises, not discovered causal guarantees. The finite laws and retained trial evidence supply the subsequent response or unresolved state.

Experiment 1: a useful alternative is a different claim

The original reader needs nonzero parallel sections with a positive overlapping interval. The alternative constructs a hypothetical target from the first landmark plus the desired displacement along the selected normal. An endpoint claim compares this reference with the actual second landmark.

The target construction is observable native structure, not a physical vertex which has been moved into place. Both claims retain their own evidence and truth conditions. The preference law selects a response; it does not merge their meanings.

Width is held in this stage to isolate the observation mechanism. Consequently gap agreement does not generally imply endpoint agreement. In one ordinary starting case the endpoint happened to agree already.

The displayed alternative selected flag describes the chosen response read. An actuator pulse can still be held by the trial schedule or by another local condition. Selection is not proof that a geometric change occurred.

Experiment 2: a finer sibling, not a rewritten coarse tick

A nine-row law reads consecutive ternary responses. Opposed singleton signs after an actual accepted step can permit trying a finer rung. The detector checks that the earlier event and support match the consumed transition, that the current event is fresh, and that the previous after-state is still current. Trial pulses are not recycled as evidence of a feedback oscillation.

The finer carrier has SubdivisionOf(coarse unit, 3). Activation projects a captured source value into the new state through an explicit calibration and imports it as a delta. This is a non-draining observed copy of a parameter, not duplicated conserved material. Source tension, inadequate fine capacity, a nonempty target or stale evidence prevents admission.

In the successful inset case, final height is seven fine ticks and width is sixteen. Their current coarse reads are two and five whole ticks, each with one fine tick of residue. Those reads do not replace the independently retained coarse source values. Switching a rung preserves that extent's physical read; the other actuator may still move on the same beat.

The reversal rule is deliberately weak. It may also fire after a changed goal or another control's movement. This experiment shows that the supplied trial can help these cases, not that every reversal diagnoses a resolution problem.

Experiment 3: learn a local motion within a finite candidate scope

Each active control starts with 16 candidate words: positive and negative horizontal/vertical displacements, selected diagonal displacements, and zero. The scope is supplied and contains the relevant motion. The ambiguity control adds a distinct word which has the same displacement as the expected motion.

For every trial, candidate prediction uses the actual accepted extent change, not the requested step. Native before/after landmark words supply the observed displacement. Their difference from each prediction remains in the witness. Only a nonzero change under the supplied single-input isolation premise can eliminate candidates.

The authored-motion comparison settles the ordinary, confounded-label and ambiguity-label goals without needing those trials. That is the cost of withholding supplied knowledge: the observed controls stop when their evidence cannot select a rule. One ambiguity trial happens to reach the gap goal, but the endpoint goal remains unmet and motion remains unresolved. A favorable geometry by itself is not evidence that learning succeeded.

The current study records controlled trials, then uses the retained candidate. It does not continuously relearn from every coupled feedback step, where causal attribution would be more difficult. Candidate counts are neither probabilities nor independent votes. The component tests separately cover later contradictory evidence, replay, missing samples and restoration.

This does not show that all three additions are necessary for every task. Refinement with supplied motion already solves both compatible geometric probes. In the collapsed combined case, the width trial or endpoint control can make the original section readable before the landmark response needs to act. The isolated alternative-reader stage is the cleaner evidence for that mechanism's contribution.

The meaningful composition result is that a new finer control can learn its own local effect and continue using the same claim and response machinery. In the inset example, coarse height learns (0, −9) fine reaches per tick; fine height learns (0, −3). Coarse width learns (3, 0); fine width learns (1, 0). The active candidate count expands again when a new rung begins.

Evidence, size and verification

Across all conditions, 26 runs end at the joint goal, 31 at the original gap goal, and 35 at the landmark goal without height debt. These are different claims, not a combined success percentage or an ornament quality metric.

Four representative complete traces agree between native and compiled finite laws. The four saved native graphs decode successfully. Component tests cover resuming motion candidate memory and finer-rung behavior from actual decoded structure. This is not a claim that the whole host scheduler can be resumed from the report JSON.

The measured apparatus archives are 129,807–327,388 bytes, including actuator and candidate histories. The selected full witness exports are roughly 3–7.5 MB. Repeated native diagnostic and candidate evidence accounts for much of this size. These are inspectable experimental archives, not compressed minimal machines or a benchmark of production memory use.

All 82 focused component and integration checks passed. Browser checks covered all 84 run selections, desktop and narrow-screen layouts, keyboard selection, and local report links without script errors. The complete dotnet test Gojo.slnx run passed in all 18 test projects, including 3,260 qualitative execution tests. No tests failed or were skipped.

What this leaves open

  • Learning which landmark or alternative reader deserves attention.
  • Choosing informative trials, retrying after an uninformative one, or trading immediate goal progress against information gain.
  • Revalidating a motion model when coupled inputs or context change.
  • Admitting a useful equivalence class when several different words have the same relevant behavior, instead of requiring one unique structure.
  • Choosing a subdivision or tolerance from evidence, rather than offering one fixed finer rung.
  • Compacting histories while preserving the promised coarse evidence and the difference between observed copies and conserved flow.

These results strengthen the local mechanics. They do not yet establish an autonomous learning architecture, a general interpolator, persistent material deformation, or a universal aesthetic evaluator.
