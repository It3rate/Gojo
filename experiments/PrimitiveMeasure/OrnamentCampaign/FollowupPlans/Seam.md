# Does an actual seam landmark change what can be learned?

This is a new prospective question chosen after the primary report. In the
primary assay the evolved seam specialist
averaged +4.7 utility points versus doing nothing. It regressed on 114 of
576 paired specialist cases. Other briefs did much better on the same machine
grammar. One possible missing observation is the location of the repeat seam.

Full primary report hash: `1320D2322AB10060B67E879F975C51A3390CCB1DCB4D1DB97E1423018EA1FBAC`.
Primary frozen artifact file hash: `BFA2E5AEF551148B0B468914373C85740042C52A0D3CF2C1B165EEE3399E47D8`.

Only the published sample read by sensor port 10 changes. In one arm the
stored original seed is published. In the second arm a single bound marker
names site 0 beside the actual seam, the boundary between the last site and
site 0. In the third arm a single interior site names a decoy. Its placement
is deterministic per parent and the sibling reflects it. The opening material,
private judge, permissions, available stock and disturbance stay the same.
The marker reports where the boundary is. It does not identify a needed edit
or provide the desired finished rail. The comparison with the original arm
also changes the density and availability of stored seed evidence.

Use the same uniform full-grammar proposals in all three arms. Four independent
seeds, 4,096 proposals per replicate and 32 training examples per candidate
give 1,572,864 charged evaluations across the arms. Include idle, retain eight
distinct training candidates per arm for validation and lock twelve winners.
Evaluate all combinations of trained and supplied cues on **seam reserve**
only after all follow-up winner locks have been verified.

Predict that learning with the real seam cue improves on the matched sparse
decoy and improves on the original information at test time, and that switching
the same real-cue champion to the decoy hurts. Require a 20-point increase
in at least two thirds of parent groups after averaging sibling views and
search replicates. Otherwise preserve contrary or inconclusive outcomes. Also
predict the real-marker winners will beat making no edits by the same threshold.
Keep the interior-preservation cost and all cases getting worse in the assessment.
