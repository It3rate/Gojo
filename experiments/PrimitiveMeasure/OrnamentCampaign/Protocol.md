# Local ornament tournament · protocol 1

This campaign asks whether search can discover small executable controllers that
adapt a seeded pattern under a brief. It is separate from the public Atlas. The
unit of competition is a stored machine. It must act through local ports when
reloaded without a trainer, candidate population, stored target picture, or
judge. The machine that emits a signal and the machine that spends an edit
have separate local roles. Their incidence and laws live in the encoded program.

## Decisions made before the run

- Freeze the full experimental manifest, seed grouping, goals, score, competition
  budgets, interventions, and executable fingerprint before opening any campaign
  outcomes. Unit tests may use separate small fixtures to check the instrument.
- The primary grid crosses four briefs, six seed morphologies, and ten planned
  contrasts. Each of its 240 cells gets an identifier, comparator, predicted
  direction or equivalence, threshold and outcome. Count those separately from
  seed episodes, trained programs and repeated renders. Cells sharing a brief,
  recipe or champion are dependent.
- Train five leagues: the four single briefs and a mixed-brief league. Compare
  a parameter-only restriction, an open-loop restriction, full-grammar random
  sampling, and structural evolution. Each receives four independent search
  replicates, 64 generations, 64 paid proposals per generation, and 32 training
  episodes per proposal. That gives 327,680 paid proposals and 10,485,760
  charged training episodes. Report wall time and completed work separately.
- Bounded CPU concurrency defaults to eight workers. Schedule independent
  populations through a shared pool. Worker count may change elapsed time,
  never the examples, budgets, seeds or tie order. Duplicate proposals still
  cost a slot. Keep competing variants, parentage and unsuccessful candidates.
- Variation can change signal sources, laws, delays, endpoint bindings and
  passages within the declared grammar. The teacher may select candidates
  during training. The evaluator may measure output in ordinary code. At
  execution the artifact runs its stored local laws through a fixed Gojo
  interpreter or its equivalent lookup tables. Neither teacher nor evaluator
  supplies edits at inference.
- Choose finalists from training and validation only. Lock every league,
  method and replicate before primary blind assessment. Include weak champions
  and every blind case in the record. Blind results do not revise the primary
  campaign. Reserve parents stay unassayed until a separately frozen follow-up.

## Success and failure

The strongest evidence would be a reusable, connected controller beating equally
funded simpler searches on unfamiliar seeds and surviving perturbation. A
matched wire cut, delay or polarity change should then selectively damage the
capability attributed to that wire. An open-loop win, an equivalent simple
machine, an uninformed tie, or no improvement over keeping the original seed
are all publishable outcomes of the local report.

The primary world is a cyclic ordered rail. Counts measure equal uncalibrated
participations in its chosen resolution. The target, initial seed, protected
sites, available material, allowed edits and realized output each have their own
roles. A protected empty corridor matters as much as a protected stroke.
Ambiguous or absent reads may leave an obligation open. Keep bounds and
failed attempts visible. An offline score is evidence about this chosen task.

## Secondary transfer

Freeze a separate two-axis fabric probe before training. A horizontal owner
and a vertical owner execute stored rail programs on separate profiles. A
third owner receives one participation bit from each at a bound crossing. Its
packed two-input law chooses where to mark. This tests portability and the
consequence of thin communication when the same rail structure participates
in another construction. The probe does not establish general drawing or a
learned grammar for two-dimensional glyphs.

Compare a connected fabric with holding one strip at its initial state and
with disconnecting its contribution. Assess agreement with the requested
crossing law, preservation of protected seed marks, and output differences
under seed perturbations. Include cases where the two choices are expected to
look alike. An indistinguishable output is not evidence for a causal wire.

## After the first wave

Write the primary report and lock it before formulating the follow-up. Select
one consequential failure, unexpected equivalence, or apparent reuse. Record
the new question, its prospective prediction, the control, budget and exact
reserve cohort before seeing reserve outputs. Use the already frozen runner,
or identify any new runner explicitly. A second-stage claim stands on its new
case groups. Retesting the primary cases is diagnostic only.

Keep the experiment, records and reports local. No Atlas layer or external
publication is part of this campaign.
