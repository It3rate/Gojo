# Three independent posters: paired faces and retained resolution

This second series adds three independent compositions, C, D, and E. All use
the same event facts: September 29, 2006, 2:00PM, and $10 entrance fee. Every
logo consists entirely of geometry. The designs were generated and frozen at
2026-09-27T16:25:08Z, before the assessment was written. Assessment verifies
both the PNG hashes and the complete source snapshots; none of the designs
was revised to fit its evaluation.

## Design comparison

| Quality | C: open rings | D: colored blades | E: square labyrinth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balance | The upper-left title and lower-right logo create a diagonal counterweight. Quiet but deliberately asymmetric. | The tilted blue field creates movement; the large title and rectangular footer stabilize it. | Unequal light and dark columns balance by contrast. The date and logo compete for first attention. |
| Whitespace | Long, generous gaps separate the title, logo, and details. | Large colored areas and tightly grouped information give an energetic, denser impression. | Broad dark space isolates the emblem. Empty space need not be white. |
| Eye motion hypothesis | Title → red accent → rings → lower details. | Title or blades → time tile → fee tile. | Date or labyrinth → opposite column → lower information. The first fixation remains ambiguous. |
| Color harmony | Restrained navy and paper with a small warm accent. | Yellow, blue, pink, and green create cheerful tension rather than quiet unity. | A narrow neutral palette is consistent and severe. |
| Form and type | Open circular lines and a large serif suggest an editorial composition. | Solid irregular blades and heavy capitals make a forceful, playful composition. | Repeated right angles and condensed capitals emphasize structure. |
| Reduction | Fine rings and small footer text are vulnerable as the poster shrinks. | Broad blades and bold information survive the inspected reduced preview best. | The silhouette remains strong; inner turns need enough room to stay distinct. |

These are readings of the frozen designs. Eye motion, welcome, and emotional
tone remain observer hypotheses, not measured audience responses. The meaning
of the event copy is excluded from the suitability comparison; typography and
information hierarchy remain part of the visual design.

## What the paired faces preserve

The user's confirmed interpretation is **paired faces**. A pair is built from
a carrier and its `Inverse()` face, connected to the selected poster structure.
Signed movement stays on its selected face. This retains four distinct cases:

| Native reading | Contextual emphasis | Explicit comparison read |
|---|---|---:|
| +strong 4/5 | Affirming graphic strength | +0.8 |
| −strong 4/5 | Rejecting graphic strength | −0.8 |
| +weak 4/5 | Affirming graphic weakness | −0.8 |
| −weak 4/5 | Rejecting graphic weakness | +0.8 |

The right column requires a declared alignment. Equality there does not erase
which face was examined or which claim was rejected. An unaligned native
comparison between +strong and −weak remains unresolved. A negative location
on the strong carrier is directly visible from that carrier; a location on
its inverse face is not. The inverse is not a reciprocal.

The numerical distinction is executable; the difference between affirmation
and rejection is supplied by the observer's focus and supporting explanation.
The kernel has not independently inferred a psychological effect from the
word "weak." It preserves the material needed to make those different reads.

The full experiment uses twelve pairs: strong/weak, big/small, happy/sad,
colorful/muted, balanced/unbalanced, spacious/crowded, ordered/disordered,
harmonious/clashing, clear/obscure, resilient/fragile at small size,
welcoming/distant, and expressive/anonymous. Each reading points to its
selected document or logo. The positive direction is a convention, not a
universal preference. Thin lines can be exactly right for a particular design.

## Equal proportions, different resolution

Each observation retains its original numerator, denominator span, selected
face, calibration relation, source structure, and read witness. Fractions are
not reduced to a floating-point value. For this experiment a source tick is
interpreted through a declared half-cell envelope:

| Source reading | Proportion | Represented cell |
|---|---:|---|
| 1/2 | 0.5 | [0.25, 0.75] |
| 100/200 | 0.5 | [0.4975, 0.5025] |
| Coarse 1/2 merely rewritten as 100/200 | 0.5 | Original [0.25, 0.75] retained |

Gojo equations calculate the doubled denominator and both half-cell endpoints.
Extrema reads preserve their order when an opposed face reverses the numeric
alignment. A finer display does not create a finer observation. The executable
checks verify that the rewritten coarse reading keeps the original envelope.

This supplies an explicit resolution interpretation for the user's certainty
distinction. It is an experimental reading convention, not a claim that the
kernel already has a general statistical confidence calculus. Confidence in
an observer or an audience association remains an open part of the witness.

Examples of the actual authored readings:

| Pair | C | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong / weak | weak 3/5 | strong 9/10 | strong 4/5 |
| Happy / sad | neutral 0/2 on happy face | happy 2/3 | sad 1/3 |
| Colorful / muted | muted 4/5 | colorful 9/10 | muted 19/20 |
| Ordered / disordered | ordered 4/5 | ordered 1/2 | ordered 9/10 |
| Resilient / fragile at small size | resilient 1/2 | resilient 4/5 | resilient 3/5 |

Mood is deliberately coarse. C's calmness does not establish sadness. E's
near-monochrome appearance supports a sharper colorfulness read than its
emotional associations support. Those denominator choices are still authored
judgments, with inspectable explanations, not calibrated empirical evidence.

A second caution comes from the measured RGB channel-spread proxy:

| C | D | E |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2080/51000 | 30715/51000 | 1434/51000 |

Here 51000 is **200 spatial samples × 255 channel steps**. The graph retains
that factorization and the sample traces. It does not mean 51000 independent
observations or extraordinarily reliable aesthetic judgment. This proxy
supports the colorfulness comparison; it does not calculate harmony.

## Suitability and unresolved ordering

All contexts refer to the same twelve observation pairs. Weights and their
signs are declared Gojo relations. The oil-meeting context, for example,
prefers restraint through a negative colorfulness coefficient. Weighted
equation surfaces compose central reads and endpoints; extrema reads handle
the reversal caused by negative coefficients. Rounding occurs only for display.

| Use | C /10 | D /10 | E /10 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Children's event | 5.4 [4.5–6.3] | 8.9 [8.4–9.4] | 4.6 [4.0–5.2] |
| Tech trade show | 6.8 [6.2–7.4] | 8.4 [7.8–9.0] | 7.9 [7.3–8.4] |
| Oil industry meeting | 7.3 [6.8–7.8] | 7.9 [7.3–8.5] | 8.5 [8.0–9.0] |
| Art show | 8.6 [8.1–9.0] | 7.7 [7.2–8.3] | 8.4 [7.9–8.8] |
| Small-town bake sale | 7.3 [6.4–8.2] | 8.8 [8.3–9.3] | 5.3 [4.7–5.8] |

Brackets propagate represented reading cells, not all cultural or modeling
uncertainty. They are not statistical confidence intervals. Context means a
welcoming family event, energetic public tech showcase, restrained engineering
meeting, independent art show, or neighborly bake sale, respectively. Different
audiences can bind different preferences to the same lower structure.

Gojo also compares the nominal leader's lower endpoint against every rival's
upper endpoint, before display rounding, and retains the smallest separation:

| Use | Nominal leader | Smallest separation /10 | Conclusion within these cells |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Children | D | +2.188 | Clear separation |
| Tech | D | −0.625 | Ordering remains open |
| Oil | E | −0.469 | Ordering remains open |
| Art | C | −0.663 | Ordering remains open |
| Bake sale | D | +0.113 | Narrow separation; sensitive to assumptions |

The minus signs in the last table indicate overlapping alternatives, not a
failed read. C's nominal art-show lead should not be presented as a settled
preference over E. Numerical projection residue and the unbound audience
evidence remain attached to the underlying witnesses.

## Experience and recommended adjustments

What worked well:

- Equal proportions can carry unequal source resolution. Reframing can preserve
  that distinction instead of silently creating confidence.
- Opposed faces and negative movement remain separate. An explicit comparison
  can combine them without deleting their original context.
- The same artifact supports multiple audience reads. A positive direction
  need not have a positive preference coefficient in every context.
- Gojo performed the arithmetic, endpoint propagation, extrema selection, and
  ordering comparisons. The report follows connected witnesses back to source
  logos, source fractions, and the declared context.
- Overlap becomes an inspectable result. The system can retain a nominal leader
  and an unresolved ordering together.

Weaknesses exposed:

- The choice of aesthetic association and denominator is still supplied by the
  observer. A larger denominator alone cannot justify stronger confidence.
- Half-cell propagation and face calibration required a local experimental
  surface. They are not automatic semantics of every existing Gojo fraction.
- Weighted sums permit compensation and omit interactions. A severe palette
  plus a particular typeface can matter differently from their separate scores.
- Many scaffolding reads still depend on fixed relation-slot arrangements.
  A reusable surface should expose these parts locally without becoming a
  code-owned concept object.
- Detailed inspection is expensive. The three poster snapshots are roughly
  284–344 KB each; the fully traced assessment is about 115 MB. That is the
  observed verbose export cost, not a minimum graph size or a demonstrated
  restored workspace. The known compact-export issues from the earlier
  experiment were not repaired here.
- The existing local color/type adapter and background-colored cutouts remain
  limitations of these poster constructions. Reusable transparent logos need
  proper boundary/area treatment.

The strongest next code candidate is a **reusable qualified-read surface**
built from existing carriers, basis relations, and read witnesses. It should
retain the selected face, signed value, original grain, frame conversion,
evidence, and unresolved context through composition. It should discover these
parts from connected structure, with no confidence enum or detached score DTO.
Promote only the repeatedly useful structure, not these particular aesthetics.

Next, make comparison return the nominal ordering together with separation,
overlap, and residue; add explicit contextual face alignments that never
silently replace the native source; and investigate local/lazy evidence reads
before expanding diagnostic snapshots further. Evidence calibration and
interactions should be separate experiments, not implied by longer numbers.

No shared kernel or renderer behavior was changed. This series adds local
experiment code and reports only.
