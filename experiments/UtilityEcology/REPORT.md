# Utility ecology: confirmation results

Completed October 11, 2026, local time. Eight fresh paired seeds, ten variants,
163,840 world tasks. Four developmental pilots were preserved separately. The
confirmation took 343 seconds including output and saved-graph checks; solution
tests overlapped part of it. This is not a speed benchmark. No paid Jev calls.

## Short summary

The consolidated mechanism works for acquiring useful interpretations and using
them through a narrow communication boundary. Services start without a supported
answer, learn a sufficient presence-conditioned response, and can pay to add
ordinal distinctions when the response stops working. The apparatus does not
announce its changing conditions or supply desired-action labels to learners.

The strongest findings are:

- Learned condensed answers matter: withdrawing their delivery reduced group
  net return by **3.913 resource units per task**, across all eight seeds.
- Error gating helped: compared with always learning under the same contract,
  it reduced updates by **85.7%**, burned work by **19.8%**, and improved net
  return by **0.488 per task**.
- Useful dormant knowledge should survive this demand drought. Retention beat
  the tested inactivity-reset rule by **0.540 per world task**, across all seeds.
- The extra opportunity gate **hurt slightly** when isolated from buyer probing
  and renewal: **−0.046 per task**, negative in all eight seeds.
- Success-contingent fees, repeated ordinal renewal and the longer cold-start
  allowance have **no clear overall advantage** in this small confirmation.
- Newly useful information remains difficult to discover reliably. Error-driven
  work can also spend resources on irreducible noise. These are remaining gaps.

This supports a modular, primitive-first learning mechanism under a supplied
grammar. It does not establish open-ended invention, an optimal market or new
higher-order emergence beyond the earlier composite experiments.

## What was implemented

The native objects are connected carriers, relations, unit-shape coordinates,
equations, claims, evidence ledgers, choice surfaces and settlement routes.
`InterpretationProvider`, `LearningGate` and `ServiceEconomy` are local
construction/read helpers for those graphs. Their operational state is retained
in the native roots, including mutable wallet and attention aliases.

The host supplies a small physical world, an action-independent tape, a fixed
scheduler and evaluation projections. It holds the private measurements. Every
physical sample publishes presence with **open magnitude**; raw class numbers,
metric scale, absolute zero and phase labels do not appear at the learner port.
Purchased comparison exposes only order between acquired same-source samples.

One buyer and four supplied service scaffolds form the economy. The buyer uses
native observed-utility claims and a local cycle to choose buy or hold. This is
not an auction among independently discovered competing suppliers. Prices,
consumer routing, binary action calibration and the construction grammar are
authored. Native body replacement is actual variation within that grammar;
arbitrary new service ports and deep hierarchies are not generated in this pass.

Actual physical consequences are bound to the exact issued command. Under this
world's explicit two-opposed-actions premise, the provider derives:

```text
successful direction = issued direction × sign(actual physical consequence)
```

That native product supplies a direction, not a hidden desired-action label.
Holds and misbound outcomes cannot train direction. The gate compares the
pre-update advertised prediction with the later independent consequence sign.
Physical monetary magnitude remains separate from this deliberately coarse
directional observation.

### A concrete trace

In the success-paid variant, seed 600:

1. The common service's first four observations give open advice. Fallback
   commands are tested; successes and failures both support the same direction
   through the declared retrodiction law.
2. At world tick **11**, it publishes `+1` without ordinal landmarks or paid
   magnitude comparisons. Presence alone is sufficient for the initial law.
3. The physical law changes after tick 511, without an announcement to learners.
   At tick **520**, accumulated mismatch admits a **16-unit** ordinal proposal.
   The action on that step still fails; construction is not immediate success.
4. Tick **522** adds an acquired anchor. Tick **524** supplies a supported `−1`
   answer for another sample. Later evidence produces two useful ordered regions.
5. In phase two, all common requests are answered correctly using the ordinal
   interpretation. Occasional learning continues while ordinary error is low.

Across the eight full-variant runs, the initial common phase had **2,178
supported presence-only publications in 2,210 requests**, no ordinal proposals
and no comparisons. Initial action accuracy was **99.3%**, including startup
fallback errors. The later stable common phase answered **2,651/2,651 correctly**
using ordinal models, with no further proposals in that phase. This demonstrates
useful primitive stages without reconstructing full numeric magnitudes.

For general trace inspection, `landmarks` is recorded after the step, while
advice is read before feedback. A step that renews or resets its model cannot be
classified reliably by the post-step landmark count alone. The stable phases
above avoid that ambiguity. Unknown presence interpretation is distinct from
physical signal absence, which this apparatus does not try to learn.

## Protocol and controls

Seeds **600–607**, four phases of **512** tasks each. All variants for a seed
receive the same external event tape regardless of actions. The source laws are:

| Source | Initial condition | Later condition |
|---|---|---|
| Common | One response works regardless of magnitude | Ordering becomes necessary after phase zero |
| Rare valuable | Two ordered regions | Absent for two phases, then returns unchanged |
| Noise | Fair consequence independent of sample | Remains uninformative |
| Future opportunity | Initially noise | Becomes ordinally useful in the final phase |

Source identities identify requests, not these analyst descriptions. The world
cannot write model state. Separate deterministic generators supply requests,
sample classes and noisy consequences. An enumerated fair-noise-law test checks
absence of sample information; a reproducible finite tape alone is not a proof
of independence.

The primary endpoint is **signed physical gross minus all burned work, divided
by all world tasks**. Holds, failed investigations and startup costs stay in the
denominator. Starting endowment is excluded from return. Internal service fees
are transfers, not additional group costs.

Each paired contrast uses eight independent seed replicates. Reported intervals
are descriptive, unadjusted Student t intervals on seed means or paired changes.
They are not multiplicity-corrected significance claims across ten variants.
No parameters were changed after fresh confirmation began.

The exact prices, cycles, pressure and proposal rules are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md)
and [README.md](README.md). Costs are abstract units; they do not price every
native operation or measure CPU time.

## Group results

Resource units per world task. Accuracy is pooled among non-hold actions;
coverage reports what fraction of all tasks received such an action.

| Variant | Net/task | Burn/task | Answered accuracy | Coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No interpretation learning | −0.351 | 0.140 | 47.2% | 6.2% |
| Always, upfront | 1.880 | 2.612 | 96.1% | 60.9% |
| Error, upfront | 2.368 | 2.094 | 95.9% | 60.9% |
| Matched error, upfront | **2.877** | 2.531 | 94.6% | 70.1% |
| Opportunity, upfront | 2.831 | 2.570 | 94.6% | 70.1% |
| Opportunity, success paid | 2.861 | 2.485 | 94.4% | 69.6% |
| Success paid, inactivity reset | 2.321 | 2.482 | 93.2% | 67.6% |
| Success paid, communication withdrawn | −1.051 | 0.640 | 48.8% | 15.3% |
| Success paid, no ordinal renewal | 2.840 | 2.464 | 94.4% | 69.1% |
| Success paid, early buyer cutoff | 2.699 | 2.418 | 94.9% | 67.5% |

The highest observed group mean does not prove a universally best policy. The
matched error variant keeps the same eight-request buyer probing period and
renewal permission as opportunity/upfront, but removes its opportunity gate.
The original error and always variants probe every 32 requests and disallow
renewal. Their comparison with the opportunity package changes several things.

The no-learning control has empty interpretation models and zero model updates.
Its supplied alternating fallback can act on purchased tasks. It is therefore
an initialization/learning control, not a strict never-act population. Removing
communication also changes later physical experience, utility and purchasing;
it is a causal intervention on the running system, not fixed-tape action replay.

### Paired changes

| Change, left minus control | Mean net/task change | 95% descriptive interval | Positive seeds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Delivered advice vs withdrawn advice | +3.913 | [+3.679, +4.147] | 8/8 |
| Full learning package vs empty interpretation | +3.213 | [+2.981, +3.445] | 8/8 |
| Error gate vs always learning | +0.488 | [+0.449, +0.527] | 8/8 |
| Opportunity gate vs matched error gate | **−0.046** | [−0.063, −0.028] | 0/8 |
| Success paid vs upfront | +0.030 | [−0.021, +0.082] | 6/8 |
| Retention vs inactivity reset | +0.540 | [+0.358, +0.723] | 8/8 |
| Ordinal renewal vs no renewal | +0.022 | [−0.045, +0.089] | 4/8 |
| Sixteen vs eight initial paid trials | +0.162 | [−0.069, +0.393] | 5/8 |

Error-gated updates total **1,422**, versus **9,950** for always learning.
Accuracy changes slightly while work falls substantially. This directly supports
the principle that low observed error can slow learning without stopping action.
The supplied gate remains fixed; it does not itself learn the best permission
law or forecast value of information.

## Retention and occasional usefulness

Rare service has no requests for 1,024 world tasks. Parked storage is deliberately
free in this comparison. The inactivity-reset variant additionally discards its
learned interpretation after a gap and burns 16 units. It also retains gate and
economic evidence, so this is model forgetting, not complete entity death.

After return:

| Rare-source result | Retained | Inactivity reset |
|---|---:|---:|
| Correct/answered, pooled | **922/927 (99.46%)** | 586/679 (86.30%) |
| Answered/all rare requests | **92.15%** | 67.50% |
| Correct in first 16 requests of each seed | **103/128** | 44/128 |
| Mean allocated net per rare request | **11.002** | 4.307 |

The first-return comparison includes holds, avoiding an apparently high accuracy
produced by answering only easy requests. Retention's whole-world benefit is
positive in every seed. This supports preserving an occasional service under
this resource contract; it does not establish that all unused knowledge should
be stored without cost indefinitely.

## Opportunity, noise and currency: useful failures

### The extra gate is not yet a useful agency mechanism

Opportunity/upfront lost 0.046 net/task to matched error/upfront in all seeds.
It made **2,757** updates versus **2,122**, with little change in task behavior.
The rule periodically grants work when there is traffic and buyer capital.
Traffic means a bought request, including an already interpreted request;
funded demand means sufficient reserve, not independently discovered demand.
These proxies add work where existing interpretations are already sufficient.

The larger opportunity package beats original error/upfront, but that comparison
also changes buyer probing and renewal. The matched control prevents attributing
that package improvement to the extra gate.

### New utility is not reliably captured

For the final-phase newly useful source:

| Variant | Mean allocated net/request | Profitable seeds | Answered/all requests |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Matched error, upfront | +0.328 | 3/8 | 26.9% |
| Opportunity, upfront | +0.323 | 3/8 | 26.9% |
| Opportunity, success paid | −0.217 | 2/8 | 19.6% |
| Success paid, no renewal | −0.785 | 1/8 | 12.3% |

The full success-paid mean's interval spans **[−1.007, +0.573]**. Some seeds
learn the new distinction; most fail to turn it into profitable participation.
Seed 603 is a useful success case: its last 64 new-source requests have 62
correct actions and allocated net +3.406/request. Seed 600 has no final-phase
new-source learning grants, empty ordinal anchors and negative return. Both
are retained rather than selecting the success story alone.

In seven of eight full-variant runs, that service ends with zero attention.
Earlier noisy work, low historical utility, infrequent paid re-entry and scarce
supplier funding can block repair even while the buyer has ample resources.
This is observed funding failure, not a formal causal decomposition of those
factors. A permission to learn cannot help if its attention account cannot pay.

### Noise is bounded by spending, not understood

On truly noisy source 2, the full variant still makes **296 paid learning grants**
and **22 ordinal proposals**, across eight seeds. Early noisy history of source
3 consumes additional work. Exhausting attention reduces activity but does not
establish that a module has recognized irreducible noise.

The next useful distinction for a gate is likely **error versus achievable
learning progress**, measured within comparable coarse contexts. A high-error
stream with no progress should get a small recurring retest allowance rather
than repeated full reconstruction. Novel opportunity may need a separately
funded exploration reserve so old noise does not permanently close its route.
These are proposed next experiments, not implemented results of this pass.

### Currency is a useful constraint, not yet a superior allocation policy

Payment changes where attention can be funded, so it can change behavior.
Its transfers remain conserved. Success payment can reward fallback success
even when advice is open; it is not causal credit for supplied information.
No overall superiority over upfront payment was established here.

Utility quotes use observed gross resource, not a prediction of every future
cost. Source-local net numbers allocate shared upkeep and startup burns to the
world step when incurred; they are not causal marginal component utility.
Whole-world net avoids that allocation ambiguity. Future causal pricing would
need a justified counterfactual or another explicit contribution contract.

## Pilots and adjustments retained

| Pilot | Protocol | What changed afterward |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Seed 500, phase 128 | Equation-result witness format prevented learner admission; corrected the native read bridge and added regression coverage |
| 02 | Seeds 500–501, phase 256 | Buyers rejected weak services before enough paid evidence; extended bounded initial trials and admitted earlier funded proposals |
| 03 | Seeds 500–501, phase 256 | Initial missing information accumulated into needless refinement; added generic success-pressure relief |
| 04 | Seeds 502–503, phase 512 | Longer phases and stronger final opportunity traffic tested re-entry; retention looked useful, new opportunity remained weak |

After pilot 04, the matched gate control was added and native service bodies
were refreshed immediately after idle reset, including rejected requests. Saved
artifact checks were extended before confirmation. Current source does not
retroactively describe every pilot; each pilot retains its original manifest
and source hashes. Pilot 01 is an implementation failure, not learning evidence.

## Verification and scaling boundaries

- **2,269 full-solution tests passed**, zero failed or skipped, including **69**
  UtilityEcology cases. The full test command returned zero.
- Focused cases verify withheld raw values, fair-noise law, bound outcomes,
  unknown versus zero, paid proposals, stable coarse behavior, rare retention,
  refund/escrow accounting and transport.
- Three nonlinear monotone private encodings give identical small-run decisions,
  costs and native graph counts. The experiment uses order, not a recovered raw
  magnitude scale. This test uses phase 64, not all campaign seeds.
- Independent raw-data review confirmed **163,840 per-step monetary identities**,
  aggregate totals and paired event tapes. Internal fees were not double charged.
- All **10 actual saved first-seed graphs** preserve signatures, money and aliases,
  reproduce retained-anchor/presence publications and continue paid gate decisions
  through an explicitly reconnected ordered comparison fixture.

Saved-model replay does not restore the world's private metric dictionary for
arbitrary novel samples. Host scheduling and source configuration remain part
of a runnable experiment. Provider-level duplicate-feedback exclusion is not
guaranteed; the synchronous study admits each event once. Larger asynchronous
training needs a native receipt admission law first.

At most four ordinal landmarks and eight proposals are allowed per service,
and recent ledgers/receipts have explicit finite windows. This does not prove a
fixed bound on the entire reachable witness graph. The representative full
seed-600 archive is about **2.57 MB**, with **65,468 relations** and **7,763
carriers** in the whole graph. Historical provenance, coordination and economic
witnesses are substantial compared with the tiny learned decision rule. No
compactness, CPU-efficiency or indefinite-run memory guarantee is claimed.

The solution's shared TRX filename overwrote earlier projects' result files.
[verification/solution-summary.json](verification/solution-summary.json) retains
the verified per-project console counts and that limitation; focused TRX files
remain separate. No failing test was omitted from the reported gate.

## What I would keep and try next

Keep **open initial interpretation, narrow public answers, consequence-bound
learning, selective error gates, explicit funding and protected dormant useful
knowledge**. Keep payment optional until it beats simpler sponsorship.

Revise the current opportunity rule. It needs evidence that work can improve a
decision, not just traffic plus money. A next study should compare:

1. A coarse local progress signal against raw error, retaining a small retest
   budget for apparently unlearnable contexts.
2. A buyer-sponsored re-entry reserve against success-only supplier funding.
   The reserve should be bounded, paid and independent of hidden phase labels.
3. A proposal market among two or three genuinely alternative native parts,
   admitting replacements only after measured decision benefit exceeds cost.
4. A second-level module that combines earned lower-level services on a held-out
   relational task, preserving the earlier emergence study's information
   boundaries and communication-removal controls.

The current data justify those tests. They do not require keeping every market
rule or letting every idle structure disappear.

## Research and reproducibility

[Oudeyer, Kaplan and Hafner](https://www.pyoudeyer.com/ims.pdf) motivate separating
raw error from learning progress; the current gate does not implement their
complete mechanism. [Hay and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.2048) motivate
valuing computations by their benefit to a later decision rather than presumed
precision. [Wolpert, Tumer and Swanson](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20010071848)
motivate explicit local utility contracts; the experiment does not compute their
counterfactual contribution utility.
