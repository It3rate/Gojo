# Protected structural trials: protocol

## Status and question

The production law is frozen for fresh confirmation after the repaired
`seed-pilot-02` passes its strict artifact audit. This is a separate, opt-in
UtilityEcology experiment: an exploratory spending and replacement law, not a
new default for Gojo. Existing ecology and learning-progress runners retain
their original behavior. Parameters and source hashes are retained in each
run's manifest. The first development run is explicitly excluded below.

The question is whether a small, finite allowance can let a local structural
hypothesis become useful before it must support further investigation from
service income. We also ask whether comparing a candidate with a working
incumbent on the same retained experiences helps avoid harmful replacement.

The preceding [progress study](PROGRESS-REPORT.md) found that its signed-progress
gate reduced updates but also reduced physical benefit. The new trial protects
one candidate's work while retaining the current service. It does not presume
that the new law will perform better.

## Supplied world and boundaries

Reuse the existing four-source world, prices, request tape and opaque sensor
contracts. Four phases of 512 tasks give 2,048 world requests per seed and arm.
Every arm in a seed receives the same action-independent `WorldTape`.

Only the evaluator may use phase, raw measurement, source quality or desired
action. A learner receives a source port and an acquired opaque sample with a
presence read and open magnitude. A paid comparison of acquired samples from
that same source supplies ordered evidence. It does not expose a calibrated
zero, metric distance or the private six-level measurement.

The four source laws remain useful controls:

- The common source first admits a coarse constant direction, then requires an
  ordinal distinction.
- The rare source remains useful across a long demand gap.
- The independent-noise source never contains a predictive input distinction.
- The initially noisy source becomes useful later, without an announcement to
  the service.

Actual issued action and a bound physical consequence support the existing
native retrodiction under the supplied binary opposed-action law. An unknown
advice read remains open. It is not a numerical zero, wrong answer or extra
physical observation. Unsupported or misbound outcomes cannot furnish a
training label.

## Native trial structure

The intended local shape is:

```text
G(
  source contract,
  incumbent structure,
  candidate or open slot,
  finite seed reserve and earned-work reserve,
  local proposal claims and paid work receipts,
  retained opaque experiences with bound consequence witnesses,
  separate training and evaluation participation,
  paired reads and decision-benefit/cost equations,
  admission or refusal witness,
  replacement correspondence and timeline
)
```

The helper class and JSON traces inspect this connected structure. They do not
own candidate status, money, evidence membership, admission or model meaning.
The host schedules requests, supplies opaque world witnesses and produces
diagnostic artifacts. A read-only replay comparison cannot grant money or
change a physical outcome.

Use a separate seeded provider and runner. Keep the legacy provider, gate and
runner intact. Program selection and documentation can expose the new mode;
the saved baseline under `backout/seed-trial-20261012/` provides the entry-point
rollback reference.

## Trial law

### A working incumbent and a finite hypothesis

Begin with the same empty presence-conditioned predictor. It can acquire a
coarse useful response before adding ordinal distinctions. Supply a bounded
ordinal candidate grammar; this pass does not discover arbitrary programs.
The incumbent remains the published service while a candidate is being tried.
Failure to train, evaluate or afford a candidate preserves the incumbent.

The initial candidate has a work allowance of **320 abstract resource units per
source**. That reserve is transferred from conserved initial buyer capital; it
is not a new external grant. It is available for at most one initial trial.
Unused startup allowance cannot pay for subsequent trials. Later trials use
earned service funds, with no repeated sponsorship or automatic rescue.

The allowance is a spending cap, not a guarantee of completion. Construction,
training events, paired inspections and comparison queries require paid work.
If construction or the next required event cannot be funded, the trial is
refused. A paid paired inspection can yield an open prediction when a finer
comparison query cannot be funded. That valid physical event still counts
toward the 32-event validation block, without adding known candidate coverage
or unsupported benefit. A completed block with at least 24 known candidate
reads can pass the admission law despite other open reads. Do not manufacture
certainty or benefit from an unpaid query.

After the initial trial, space attempts by at least **64 bought requests** to
that local service, and permit no more than **eight total trials per source**.
Use local request counts and error/tension evidence; private world phases must
not open a trial. The first proposal requires at least 16 bought requests and
pressure of at least 4. Subsequent proposals require that pressure and the
request spacing above. Each new-mode service begins with 64 conserved units in
its base attention wallet. Its ordinary service wallet starts at zero after
attention and the candidate seed reserve are funded. Later fee receipts can
fund ongoing work.

### Separate training from evaluation

Use **16 paid physical experiences for candidate training**, then freeze both
the candidate and the incumbent for **32 new purchased physical experiences**.
Establish membership without consulting the outcomes. On each of those 32
events, obtain both frozen predictions before executing the actual command and
receiving its consequence. Neither model learns from the validation events
before admission. The incumbent remains the actual service during this block.

Retain those same 32 samples, pre-feedback reads and bound outcomes for the
paired comparison. Reading that fixed batch controls the data used to compare
the two models; it does not make a second physical observation. Fresh
post-admission prediction before feedback then tests the admitted structure
outside the batch used for selection. Do not silently claim that selection on
the validation block alone establishes future generalization.

Training and evaluation records retain sample identities and bound physical
consequence witnesses. The supplied runner connects each acquisition with its
actual event; the native trial does not independently authenticate membership
in the world's private instrument map. Replaying one experience twice does not
create independent evidence. A sample is used in the evaluation denominator
only once within a trial. A new trial needs a new declared evaluation block;
repeatedly optimizing against the same small block would select lucky models.

### Paired decision benefit and paid cost

Both frozen models are read on the **same 32 evaluation samples**. Both reads and
their necessary ordered queries are paid. A query receipt may be reused only
under its declared identity and payment contract. Reusing a paid observation
does not establish an independent observation.

For each sample, compare the directions the two decision paths would actually
issue. When the candidate is open, it reuses the incumbent's actual fallback or
decision for this comparison, producing no artificial improvement. Coverage is
reported separately from correctness. At least **24 of the 32 candidate reads**
must establish a direction.

Under the supplied binary action law, the bound consequence can support a
conditional estimate of the resource consequence of each opposed decision. The
native calibration is the supplied opposed-action payout correspondence:
positive actual resource maps at a ratio of −3/2; negative actual resource maps
at −2/3. This is the same ratio for all four sources. The actual bound consequence
grounds its sample's resource scale. Retain that premise and its affine
correspondence; unsupported amounts cannot establish this read. The evaluator's
desired-action label or source-specific private reward class does not fill it.

The paired admission law is deliberately conservative:

```text
paired benefit = sum(candidate conditional resource
                   - incumbent conditional resource)
trial net      = paired benefit - all paid trial work
admit          = completed comparison
                AND candidate known coverage >= 24/32
                AND trial net > 0
```

Count all actual candidate construction, training and evaluation burn, including
failed or open work, once. Fees transferred between buyer and service are not
burn. Do not amortize cost over an invented future horizon. Ties and an
unsupported benefit comparison retain the incumbent. Successful admission
retains the proposal's prior-root connection, replacement correspondence and
paid evidence. There is no separate permanent snapshot of the incumbent taken
at validation start.

This is a fixed authored admission rule. It is neither an optimal value of
computation calculation nor evidence that the service has discovered this rule.

## Five predeclared arms

| Arm | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `seed/error-only` | Existing `progress/error-only/upfront` behavior under a study name: passive observer, error learning, buyer probes every 8 requests, renewal enabled. |
| `seed/progress` | Existing `progress/signed/upfront` behavior with retest 8, under a study name. |
| `seed/paired` | Finite seed trial, separate incumbent/candidate, paid same-experience admission law. |
| `seed/unfiltered` | Same seed, work and evaluation procedure; withdraw only the admission comparison's influence, promoting a completed candidate unconditionally. |
| `seed/no-seed` | Same candidate and paired admission mechanism with zero initial candidate allowance; the first and later trials require earned service funds. |

The paired-versus-unfiltered contrast is the main test of the admission law. Compare
paired versus no-seed to test startup support. Comparisons with the two legacy
runners concern the full package: provider lifecycle, trial cost and funding
also differ. They cannot isolate the replay rule alone.

The unfiltered arm must still perform and pay the evaluation. It may promote
only a structurally completed trial; insufficient funds do not fabricate a
candidate. It should retain its withheld comparison witness for audit. Later
actions can diverge because the arm changed its model, so later trials need not
incur identical costs. This is an intervention, not perpetual trace matching.

Keep declared whole-group initial capital equal. Account for any different
initial reserve placement and report it. Do not interpret the no-seed arm's
larger unallocated buyer balance as new income. Buyer admission, fees, ongoing
model-work rules and idle retention must be fixed in the final manifest.

## Measures and claim boundaries

### Primary physical result

For each complete run, report **actual physical gross minus all paid burn per
world request**, including startup and rejected trials. Report paired seed
differences for paired versus unfiltered and paired versus no-seed. Show the
legacy contrasts as package comparisons. Internal fee receipts are not added
to group benefit.

Use eight independent seed differences for descriptive 95% t intervals with
seven degrees of freedom. The observations within a source, phase or trial are
not additional independent campaign replicates. Report all contrasts, without
choosing the most favorable one after seeing results. Pilot values have no
confirmation interval.

### Startup versus ongoing support

Record startup reserve received, paid startup work, unused startup locked or
retired, and the transition to earned-only funding for each service. Record
later trial cost and its actual funding origin. Any later buyer rescue or
unaccounted refill invalidates the earned-only claim.

Report whole-group gross-minus-burn after startup closes, per-source actual
physical contribution and work allocation, and service reserve cashflow. Also
report the final evaluator-only phase as a fixed late interval for all arms.
Services close startup at different observed times; a post-closure comparison
alone would have a selection bias. Include services that exhaust, fail to
finish or never begin a trial.

A supplier can pay its bills from fees while the group loses physical resource.
That is service financing, not environmentally sustainable usefulness. A
positive finite late interval is evidence within this world and price law, not
proof of indefinite survival.

### Does replay transfer to new experience?

For every trial, retain training/evaluation sample membership, known coverage,
paired gross gain, cost and admission read. Captured pre-feedback validation
publications preserve the evaluated decisions. The models are frozen while the
validation block runs; after promotion the accepted candidate can learn as the
current model. A historical trial root may therefore retain live model carrier
state, not a permanently frozen model snapshot. Training count records paid
physical experiences, not necessarily successful ordinal updates.

For admitted candidates, report actual fresh pre-update prediction coverage,
accuracy and gross-minus-work on the next 16 bought experiences, or on every
remaining experience if fewer are available. Keep the denominator and partial
window visible. Count physical outcomes once. The service may learn after a
fresh prediction; record that timing. If a later trial replaces it, stop the
window and label the shorter interval. Entire-future and all-task resource
results remain separate from this descriptive 16-event window.

Report accepted candidates whose replay benefit disappears or reverses on fresh
events. Noise-source admission rate, wasted trial burn and false favorable
replay blocks are central negative controls. A better replay result alone is
not generalized learning.

### Useful coarse structure, adaptation and rare retention

Separate common coarse-phase coverage from later common ordinal adaptation.
Show paid work and number of retained landmarks so extra complexity is not
treated as success itself. Record the first 16 common requests after the world
change and any later correct-response window as descriptive adaptation measures.

For rare return, report the first 16 world requests and the first 16 bought
experiences separately; missed demand must not disappear from the metric.
For the newly useful source, report final-phase net contribution, coverage,
completed trials and profitability count across seeds. Keep its earlier noisy
phases separate from the permanently independent-noise source.

## Pilots, freeze and confirmation

1. Implement the separate mode and the focused structural tests below.
2. Run **two pilot seeds, 900 and 901**, with all five arms and phase length 512.
   Preserve the first pilot's sources, manifest, traces and results.
3. Fix correctness, payment and protocol failures. If an experimental law or
   threshold changes, explain the reason and preserve a separately named new
   pilot. Do not silently rewrite the first pilot.
4. Verify this document's contracts against code and tests. Freeze
   settings, source hashes, artifact schema and primary comparisons.
5. Run **fresh seeds 1000 through 1007**, all five arms, phase length 512. This is
   81,920 world requests. Confirmation must use a fresh result directory.
6. Analyze every scheduled run, including poor outcomes. Keep raw traces, native
   archives, accounting/behavior checks, source snapshot and a phone-readable
   offline review. Final results belong in `SEED-REPORT.md`.

Fresh confirmation seeds are not available for tuning. If a mechanism fails,
report it before designing a further experiment. End the finite run rather than
extending it until a favorable segment appears. No Jev requests or paid external
model calls are part of this study.

## Required verification

- Preserve existing runner behavior with unchanged modes and a rollback check.
- Open sample magnitude and initial unknown prediction remain open through the
  full learner graph; no private phase, desired direction or raw metric enters.
- Grounded action/consequence retrodiction rejects unsupported and misbound
  outcomes. Native replay estimates cannot mint world publications or currency.
- Training and evaluation sample membership are disjoint, retained by identity,
  and correctly transported. Evaluation cannot mutate either frozen model.
- Open candidate reads reuse the recorded incumbent decision; open does not
  count as known coverage or a zero prediction.
- Cost receipts appear exactly once in total burn; all money paths conserve
  against declared initial capital and actual world changes. Trial startup and
  earned funding are distinguishable and no repeated sponsor is available.
- Failure to fund a required event, an incomplete validation block, tie, low
  known coverage and unfavorable trial net retain the incumbent. A paid open
  prediction is permitted within a completed block. The unfiltered control changes only the
  intended admission connection after an otherwise complete paid trial.
- Retry spacing and finite proposal limits survive save/restore. A native saved
  trial graph can reproduce at least one retained paid evaluation/admission
  decision, including its aliases and unresolved cases.
- Same-seed world tapes match across arms. Monotone private sensor re-encodings
  preserve behavior when the exposed ordered comparisons are unchanged.
- Run focused tests while developing and the full repository solution before
  the fresh confirmation. Independently reconstruct trace sums, trial membership,
  paired decision calculations, funding origins and archive behavior in analysis.

## Research used as design guidance

[Schmidhuber's same-history progress comparison](https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/driven2008.pdf)
motivates reading old and new models against identical retained data. Our test
uses a small bounded record and paid decision benefit, rather than an entire
history compressor or its curiosity objective.

[Dawid's prequential approach](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/147/2/278/7106293)
motivates evaluating a prediction before its future outcome is available. We
borrow that timing discipline for fresh physical events; this is not a
probabilistic forecasting system or a transfer of its statistical guarantees.

[Hulten, Spencer and Domingos' CVFDT](https://ai.cs.washington.edu/www/media/papers/kdd01b.pdf)
provides precedent for maintaining an incumbent while an alternate grows, then
replacing it after a comparison. We do not implement CVFDT, decision-tree split
tests or its complexity claims. The Gojo candidate remains a supplied local
structure with its own costs, witnesses and unresolved reads.

None of these sources establishes that the particular seed allowance, replay
size, threshold or monetary law above is effective. That is what this experiment
will test.
