# Utility ecology: consolidated exploratory study

Status: completed. Four developmental pilots preserved; confirmation protocol
frozen after pilot 04 and executed on eight fresh seeds. Fresh results did not
guide further parameter changes in this pass. See REPORT.md for conclusions.

## Stable requirements

- Modular native elements expose only condensed information and local requests.
- Interpretations start open, with no preassigned magnitude estimate or accuracy.
- Later actual physical consequences admit useful distinctions and utility.
- Error, uninterpreted traffic and funded demand can request investigation.
- Low error slows ordinary learning; a bounded opportunity route can still act.
- Currency rules, upkeep and renewal are experimental alternatives.
- Rarely used useful services are tested after a long demand drought.
- All steps, failed experiments, construction and burned work remain accounted.

## Shape

```text
producer = G(opaque source port, adaptive ordinal landmarks,
             advertised interpretation port, local gate, reserve,
             bound consequence receipts, construction correspondence)
consumer = G(request port, exposed interpretation, action calibration,
             actual command, independent physical resource witness)
market = G(buyer reserve, producer reserves, payment/escrow routes,
           paid-work sinks, candidate funding, utility claims)
```

Raw physical measures exist only in the external world. Samples expose presence
with open magnitude; requested pairwise order is separately billed. A producer
learns which coarse distinction supports action from actual action/outcome
correspondence. Unknown is not encoded as a zero estimate. Internal payments
redistribute currency; external outcomes and explicit burned work determine
group resource. Error does not mint money.

## Frozen confirmation protocol

Eight fresh paired seeds 600 through 607; four phases of 512 world tasks each;
ten arms, 163,840 total task evaluations. Each arm receives the same external
tape for each seed. Common service starts constant, then requires ordering;
rare service disappears for two phases and returns; noise has independently
fair consequences; a previously unhelpful service becomes useful in phase four.
The external world does not announce these conditions or write learner state.

Primary endpoint: group physical gross less construction, interpretation,
comparison, learning and upkeep burns, divided by all world tasks. Internal fees
are transfers. Compare paired seed means and report their variation, including
small-sample uncertainty. Secondary endpoints: primitive-stage use, accuracy
among answered tasks, rare return after inactivity, newly useful service,
noise spending, gates and ordinal refinements. Per-source cost rows attribute
costs to the world step when incurred; they are not causal marginal utility.

Controls: no interpretation learning; always versus error gated work;
communication withdrawn; ordinal renewal forbidden; use-based model reset;
early cutoff at eight versus sixteen paid cold-start trials. Upfront versus
success-contingent fees tests a payment hypothesis. A matched error/upfront arm
uses the same eight-request buyer probe period and permits renewal as
opportunity/upfront, isolating the opportunity gate toggle. Other error versus
opportunity comparisons are explicitly package comparisons.

Initial buyer capital 16,384; construction 8 per supplied service; four
supplied service scaffolds; sponsorship 64 each (32 initially in attention).
Prices: fee 2, interpretation 1, acquired comparison 1, gate inspection 1,
granted learning 1, ordinal construction/reset 16. Upkeep 1 per active service
per 32 world tasks; parked retention free. Low-error learning period 16,
opportunity period 8. Refinement requires at least 16 bought observations since
the last proposal, pressure at least 4 and an actual learning grant; at most
eight proposals and four retained ordinal landmarks per service. Successful
matches relieve one pressure unit per four matches. These are fixed authored
rules; no optimality or open-ended invention is claimed.

Pilots are exploratory and separate from confirmation:

- Pilot 01: 1 seed, phase 128; a witness-format integration fault blocked
  interpretation learning. Fixed and regression tested; not scientific evidence.
- Pilot 02: 2 seeds, phase 256; early rejection prevented cold-start refinement.
- Pilot 03: 2 seeds, phase 256; extended trial allowance and earlier proposals,
  but cumulative initial missing information provoked needless refinement.
- Pilot 04: 2 seeds, phase 512; generic success-pressure relief, longer phases
  and stronger final opportunity traffic. Retention helped; opportunity discovery
  still struggled. No parameter change based on fresh confirmation is allowed.

Last protocol changes before confirmation: add the matched gate control; update
the supplied service's native body immediately after idle reset, including on a
subsequently rejected request; extend actual saved-file checks to known-anchor
publication replay and paid gate continuation. Novel-sample instrument replay
requires separately reconnecting the external apparatus and is outside this pass.
