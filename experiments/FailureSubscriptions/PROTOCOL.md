# Failure-triggered subscriptions: protocol before trials

This is an exploratory extension of local service discovery. It leaves prior
experiments unchanged. The question is whether short, paid future subscriptions
help a structure choose assistance at its own failure points, rather than reward
unrelated historical success. We seek useful and negative signals, not a required
winner or a complete market, attention trainer or genetic algorithm.

## Native construction

```
observations -> reusable self-prediction -> frozen delivered promise
                                              |
later observation -> error/tension -> bounded local search
                                     |
source recipe -> live port -> paid subscription gate -> receipt
                     |              |                  |
                     |       fixed future window       +-> next delivered promise
                     |       trigger + counters
                     +-> conditional prospective surface
                               ^
                later relief relative to frozen self-prediction

resource inlet -> seeker wallet -> source fee -> provider wallet
                        |                         |
                search/activation/cancel       actual work
                        +-------------> spent <---+
```

The connected Gojo structure must hold the recipes, publication dependencies,
subscription interval, triggering failure, conditional evidence, resource accounts
and local selection comparisons. C# supplies observation scheduling, this finite
population, and reports. It must not supply a detached numerical fitness function.
Pending forecasts are retained only as needed for their immediate next outcome;
finished frames are released. Current structure is mutable, not a history table.

## Supplied footholds and limits

The seeker predicts the next difference using the latest observed difference.
Four neighboring sources expose: that same difference (a deliberately redundant
control), its signed inverse, twice that difference minus the preceding difference,
and zero change. They are native numeric recipes, with no symbolic pattern labels.
All can use admitted raw input; none sees phase names, seeds or future samples.
An unavailable input remains unresolved. Actual input differences lie in [-8,8];
the extrapolation may predict outside that interval. No synthetic clipping may
make an incorrect prediction correct.

Two sources can be subscribed simultaneously. One source or the seeker's own
prediction is selected as the actual exported answer BEFORE its target arrives.
The other subscription can supply a paid trial. Individual counterfactual relief
is diagnostic/learning evidence; it must not be summed into actual system benefit.

Search is triggered by a miss of the actual exported prediction, not merely by
the seeker's private self-prediction while assistance is already working. A search
inspects at most two advertisements in a rotating local neighborhood of four.
Previously unseen sources remain eligible for a paid trial. There is no search
of private recipes or a population-wide ranking.

A contract covers the next one or five SHARED INPUT OPPORTUNITIES. Missing input
consumes an opportunity but provides no accuracy verdict, and cannot be repaired
by a later observation. Current publication requires a fresh paid calculation.
Two resolved nonpositive marginal outcomes can cancel a still-running contract;
completed work and earlier payments remain. Natural expiry is not cancellation.
Useful contracts can renew from earned local evidence without shopping for new
help while the actual export is correct. Dormancy retains the connection recipe
and bounded evidence, not a supposedly current output.

Repeated useful terms may strengthen a native local trace for the corresponding
failure context; poor terms can oppose it. No promotion is irreversible. This is
strengthening and reactivation, not invention of a new primitive, copying private
internals, or making a permanent service free. Those are future possibilities.

## Prospective advertisements

Each source has two supplied local failure-context surfaces: signed actual-minus-
delivered error in [-32,-1] and [1,32]. A subscription binds to the surface selected
by its initiating failure. That context remains part of later outcome evidence.
These are coarse authored partitions, not discovered categories.

The surface exposes separately the number of helpful, resolved and unresolved
outcomes and accumulated signed relief under that context, together with the
requested future window. A prospective read can estimate aggregate future
usefulness from this evidence. It does not claim a probability of five consecutive
successes, independent samples, or a guaranteed useful duration. An empty surface
is unknown, not a proven bad source. The reader must retain the original evidence
grain and any projection remainder. Data can affect future offers only after the
corresponding promised outcome has arrived.

## Costs and controls

Start with a finite 65,536-credit pool, seeker wallet 128 and four provider wallets
32 each. Consumer funding is at most 8 per arrival, capped at 128. Inspection,
activation, early cancellation and each actual calculation each cost 1. A source
delivery transfers fee 2 from the seeker to its provider. Actual production costs
1 paid by that provider. The self-prediction also costs 1 when calculated.

The recurring delivery fee is identical across policies. A longer subscription
can reduce effective cost by amortizing discovery and activation; a discount is
not created by silently dropping work costs. Fees are internal transfers, while
actual work/contact expenditure is drained to the spent account. No reputation
or outcome mints resources. Error relief is reported separately, with a supplied
valuation of 4 credits per avoided absolute signal tick when a value comparison
is needed. That calibration is not general economic value.

Use four policies with the same streams and source orders:

1. Seeker alone, without paid assistance.
2. One-opportunity assistance after failures.
3. Five-opportunity subscriptions without the prospective advertisement term.
4. The same five-opportunity subscriptions with conditional prospective evidence.

The two subscription policies share cancellation, accounting and revival laws.
Comparisons therefore separate duration from the prospective evidence term.
All attention, prior and renewal laws are supplied footholds, not trained skills.
Precise implementation choices will be recorded before the campaign and not
selected afterward to obtain better results.

## Frozen first campaign

Run each policy for 241 arrivals on each of three scenarios, with seeds 11 and 47.
Use neighbor rotations 0 and 1 respectively: 24 runs / 5,784 arrivals.

- Mixed: 36 arrivals of mostly increasing input, 41 alternating increments, 36
  changing-slope increments, 30 flat, 41 alternating again, and 57 longer zigzags.
  Seeded modest magnitude variation prevents every non-flat phase being exact.
- Wandering: bounded changes in the difference, with externally generated
  unpredictable acceleration. This challenges the extrapolating source.
- Unpredictable: independently hashed signs and magnitudes. A negative control
  for claiming general predictive improvement from a favorable streak.

Arrivals 90 and 179 are missing. Boundaries are intentionally not aligned to the
five-opportunity contract. The source generator and seed stay outside the native
actors. No campaign tuning or replacement of unfavorable outcomes. If a bug
invalidates a run, record why before retaining an explicitly identified rerun.

## Evidence

Measure actual exported error versus the same frozen self-prediction, supplied
utility, direction agreement, individual subscription relief, resolved/unresolved
outcomes, source use, subscriptions/renewal/cancellation, local inspection counts,
fees and whole-system work costs. Keep sparse windows and one current native
continuation rather than every sample. Observe retained graph and allocation
costs; do not confuse a bounded lookup with a scalable global runtime.

Contract checks must cover causal forecasts and quote snapshots, missing input,
fixed expiry, cancellation preserving expenditure, no false reward for a duplicate,
bounded search/concurrency, real port/receipt dependence, native transport and
continuation, release of completed frames, and conserved currency. Do not assert
a campaign winner. Run the repository solution suite after focused checks.

## Executable choices frozen before the campaign

The implemented choice score is four times the positive part of the route's last
resolved marginal relief, plus four times the prospect weight times the positive
part of its quoted aggregate relief. A cold or unreadable quote adds no empirical
term and remains available for a trial. Negative evidence is retained in the
native counters; this supplied ranking clips its contribution to attention while
cancellation responds to nonpositive outcomes. Equal scores keep local order.
At most two discovered offers are compared. Both may be activated when slots are
available; with one slot the higher score gets the opportunity. The actual export
selects the highest-scored freshly delivered source, including a cold paid trial;
it uses the self-prediction if there is no current paid source output. This is a
deliberately simple supplied policy, not a learned optimal mixture.

An expiring term with positive accumulated marginal relief and last resolved
relief of at least one may renew, paying activation again. Unknown outcomes do
not overwrite that last resolved evidence; an unknown final outcome can therefore
be followed by renewal from older term evidence when current input is readable.
Renewal consumes capacity and still requires available funds. A completed term
with resolved evidence adds one to its contextual affinity if aggregate relief
is positive, or subtracts one otherwise. All-unknown terms supply no affinity
change. Affinity is exposed evidence, not an extra hidden score or crystallized
primitive. New terms reset their own counters, while last resolved route evidence
can persist through dormancy. That trace is not guaranteed to fit a new context.

Early cancellation requires funds for its stated fee. If unavailable, the request
leaves the contract active until funded or naturally expired; it does not receive
free cancellation. Source production is published and paid only when the provider
can cover its modeled work cost including the incoming service fee. No result is
silently supplied on credit.

The connected policy exposes horizon, prospect weight, capacity (capped at two by
the experiment), cancellation threshold, utility scale, funding/cap, fees and the
renewal threshold. Service costs and delivery prices are also native sites. The
runner's preset policies merely initialize these parameters. Local source order,
four supplied recipes and the scheduler remain authored. There is no training,
private-internals sale, autonomous port invention or permanent free primitive.

The observer's direction-agreement counts use the signs of already captured
native forecasts/actuals for display only. Native marginal relief and currency
accounting determine behavior; no report field feeds back into selection. Windows
flush at phase boundaries, every 48 arrivals and at checkpoints every 80 arrivals.
Final pending promises remain explicit; totals need not include their future
outcomes. Full current native checkpoints are retained for continuation.
