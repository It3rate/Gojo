# Local reviews and negotiated duration: protocol before trials

This extends the first campaign without replacing its results. The questions are
whether local adverse evidence prevents repeated harmful purchases, whether paid
exploration can remain separate from the actual answer, and whether a source can
offer a useful duration that a buyer can shorten. There is no required winner.

## Intended native shape

```
source port -> triggering-context review -> opportunity-offset evidence ladder
     |                  |                         |
     |          captured empirical offer -> offered / supported / trial extent
     |                                            |
     +-> connection -> buyer request cap -> accepted interval + captured tariff
             |
       recent local outcomes -> signed comparison / retry-after clock
             |                           |
       paid trial publication       next shopping eligibility
             |
       self or reviewed help -> precommitted actual answer
```

Keep the meaning in connected Gojo sites, equations, claims and settlement
witnesses. The host supplies this finite population, scheduling and external
observations. Reports do not feed back into choices. Existing recipes and the
two supplied error-sign contexts remain unchanged; those contexts do not claim
to distinguish all signal regimes.

## Reviews and attention

Use a bounded recent trace on each connection in each failure context, and public
conditional evidence attached to the service. A reader with its own resolved
experience uses that experience; the public estimate is a fallback, not a second
independent vote for the same observations. Connect the baseline against which
relief is measured. A different baseline cannot silently share an equivalent
quality rating.

The initial local window holds four resolved outcomes. Missing observations do
not become successes, failures or zero relief. When a term finishes with a
nonpositive current local review and new resolved evidence, another purchase in
that context is postponed for eight input arrivals. The
negative evidence remains available after this delay. A changed context or an
elapsed delay permits a bounded paid retrial, rather than permanent exclusion.
Shopping still examines at most two local neighbors and admits at most two
concurrent subscriptions.

The seeker's own prediction is an actual-export candidate with zero marginal
gain. Unproved and nonpositive paid help does not automatically displace it.
Trial outputs still cost money and receive prospective evaluation. Declining to
export them does not erase their outcomes. These are supplied attention rules,
not a trained policy or a general market equilibrium.

## Offers and requests

Each provider keeps a bounded ladder of eight opportunity positions under each
triggering context. Each position preserves resolved coverage, helpful coverage,
unknown coverage and signed relief. A supported prefix requires at least two
resolved outcomes and positive accumulated relief at every included position.
One further position may be offered explicitly as exploration. A previously
nonpositive frontier has an eight-arrival retry delay. A cold offer may therefore
be a single unproved opportunity; it is not advertised as confident success.

The provider's horizon and the buyer's request are distinct. The accepted horizon
is their minimum. A buyer can request two when five are offered. The accepted
window, offer evidence and tariff are captured when the contract forms. Later
changes to the source do not rewrite that contract. Shorter contracts have lower
quoted total cost under the supplied positive per-delivery fee and common
activation cost. Actual fees are paid only for delivered calculations; missing
input consumes time but does not earn a delivery fee.

An observation contributes only to the offset actually purchased and reached.
The unpurchased tail is censored, not evidence of either correctness or error.
The ladder describes past conditional usefulness by offset from acceptance; it
does not establish remaining useful life, discover cycle phase, or promise a
sequence of correct future values. Buying only short intervals limits what can
be learned about longer ones; the explicit frontier trial makes that limitation
inspectable.

Renewals retain the original triggering context. Position evidence therefore
includes continuation terms as well as fresh failure-triggered starts; it does
not isolate a particular phase of a cycle. Public duration history is cumulative
within these two contexts; only the connection's four-outcome review is recent.
An unavailable cold source may delay another source under tight capacity. No
fairness or universal opportunity to retrial is established.

## Longer-run runner

Keep deterministic input continuation, source/build/protocol fingerprints,
exclusive writers and native checkpoints. Expose requested duration, attention
and duration switches, resource budget, report-window cadence and checkpoint
cadence. Optional aggregate-only reporting avoids sample/window history while
preserving total outcome/accounting diagnostics. Resume retains all manifest
settings and checks report offsets. Interruption finishes the current arrival
and saves the continuation.

A larger declared finite resource pool supports long comparisons without an
unnoticed depletion confound. The initial trial uses 1,048,576 credits. This is
external experiment funding, not value minted by a favorable review. Exhaustion
must remain visible. Retained graph size and cumulative allocation are separate
measurements.

## Predeclared first comparison

Run 1,025 arrivals per case, four policies on three streams for seeds 101 and307:
24 runs / 24,600 arrivals. Source rotation is zero for both seeds, avoiding the
previous seed/order confound. Use identical inputs and funding within each paired
comparison. Future longer runs may use the same runner; they are separate
evidence, not replacements for this campaign.

Policies:

1. Seeker alone.
2. Original attention with fixed five-opportunity contracts and prospects.
3. Revised local attention with fixed five-opportunity contracts and prospects.
4. Revised local attention and adaptive provider offers, buyer cap eight.

Streams:

- Recurring regimes: mostly steady increase, alternating differences, flat,
  changing slope, and direction blocks of varied duration, with returns to
  earlier regimes. Include both short-lived and sustained spans. The exact
  deterministic generator is frozen before running the comparison.
- Wandering acceleration, as in the first campaign.
- Unpredictable differences, as in the first campaign.

Isolated missing observations and a missing block occur in the recurring stream.
No labels, seed, phase or future input enter the actors. Freeze implementation
and executable details before the campaign; do not tune against its results.

Measure actual exported error, directional diagnostics, costs, paid trial use,
nonpositive-source use, suppressed shopping, accepted/offered duration counts,
renewal/cancellation, unknown coverage and retained structure. Report adverse
results, resource limits and missing mechanisms alongside any improvement.
Provider prefix positivity and four-sample reviews are heuristics to test.

Accepted/offered/supported histograms describe formed contracts. The separate
advertised histogram also includes inspected zero-length offers. Reported graph
peaks are sampled at checkpoints, not on every arrival. These inspection counts
and sampled peaks may change if an otherwise identical run is stopped and resumed.

## Verification boundaries

Test causality, native state transport, expiry at the accepted length, immutable
contract terms, missing/censored evidence, cheaper shorter quotes, shadow trials,
duplicate non-improvement, same-context suppression with later retry, and
independence of contexts. Preserve accounting and bounded search checks. Run the
repository solution suite. A restart audit should cross active subscriptions and
new review/duration evidence. No test should assert that a campaign must improve.
