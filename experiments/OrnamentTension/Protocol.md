# Can an ornament feel its last stroke?

Start a pen on a named, editable path engine. A previous stroke can leave signed pressure in the pen. Its machine has a different view of the success of a repeat. A frieze sees a coarser publication again. Can a bounded signal moving between those owners change the next stroke usefully? Can a sparse group of judges suited to one strip pick better programs than the same weights everywhere? We will save the drawing, its joints, its pressure transfers, and the counterexamples.

## What is supplied

Eight named construction priors supply a running key, inward spiral, zigzag, opposed chevrons, crossing ribbons, branching spray, alternating pinline and counterstep. Direction, common extent references, pen ports, binding and termination have executable bit positions. A name identifies a prior and its external design brief. The online controller reads local observations, never an engine name, a judgement or a historical reference. Each pen offers one stroke opportunity per beat. A transverse crossing is permitted. Positive-length collinear deposition on existing ink is a separate observation. A spiral's designed stop has a different contract from losing the seam in a continuous band. ## What is trained

Twenty generations of 24 proposals compete for each family, arm and replicate. Four independent replicates and seven arms give 224 populations and 107,520 paid trials. Six training settings per trial give 645,120 native drawing executions. Generation zero includes the authored bytes; other founders receive one to four structural edits. Later generations retain up to ten distinct programs, compare immigrants, mutate path or feedback bytes, and exchange pen owners. The top six go to separate validation. Validation selects under the trained panel and the execution condition assigned to that arm.

## How the judges coordinate

There are eight perspectives. A panel distributes sixteen indivisible shares among two to four of them. The declared prior explicitly gives continuity the largest share. Its competition includes sparse alternatives. For each named family and search replicate, 96 independent pilot variants draw on six training settings and twelve separate panel-calibration settings. At each of three widths, 384 fixed alternative panels nominate their top four distinct pilots on the training readings. Choose the panel with the strongest mean broad score of those nominations on the separate calibration settings. Ties break by encoded panel bytes. This gives 36,864 panel evaluations, 96 selected panels, and 55,296 pilot drawings outside the main search budget. A committee that merely rewards a high value under its own measure has no special advantage at nomination time.

The judge training memory includes three common silhouettes: a closed box, a diamond and a slanted box, each realized at the same basis as the candidate. Similarity to this small archive addresses redundancy between designs. A rhythm repeated within a good individual design can still be valuable. The broad fixed read is separate from the selected committee but shares some of its ingredients. Holdout uses a separate set of source specimens. The historical comparison measures only the correspondence available in those reconstructions.

## What will be measured

Each physical basis is a triplet of A, B and pitch. The availability of vertical registers takes values 2, 5 and 9 in all comparisons. In this experiment the register limit is a bilateral envelope of the given half-height in units of B. The same genetic program experiences narrow and wide envelopes. Clock phase, initial pulse and number of actually executed repeats vary. Physical bases share no normalized triple across train, panel calibration, validation, blind and reserve partitions. The twelve blind bases each supply two views per width. The two views and four search replicates are averaged inside a parent, never counted as extra independent bases.

Within each family and width, predict coupled above every registered comparison at a margin of 0.012 on the broad 0–1 read, except that fixed-strength **execution of the same winner** is predicted equivalent. A separate comparison predicts the appropriate historical read exceeds a deliberately swapped reference by 0.04. Each cell needs eight of twelve parent means to meet its margin; eight in the opposite direction makes it contrary. For equivalence, eight within the open margin supports it and eight beyond one side contradicts it. All other outcomes remain inconclusive. This makes 312 related questions, not independent aesthetic laws.

Also measure realized vertical travel, range of ink, loose tips, actual crossings/overdraw, fork and merge counts, duty through each pressure route and comparison of equal plotted Xs with different sites, turns and pen histories. Treat these as diagnostics with the original records, not additional prespecified successes. Wider slots provide opportunity. Increased wandering, useful endings or larger-scale symmetry can remain absent from a given engine.
