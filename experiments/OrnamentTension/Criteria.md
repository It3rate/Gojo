# What the ornamental judges can see

A good through-running key ought to carry one pen through the seam. A good spiral can end in the middle of its motif. Two diagonal strokes may cross at one point; spending ink along the same interval twice is a different observation. This study therefore keeps eight distinct external readings. Each trained committee may consult only two to four and spends exactly sixteen shares among them. The pen sees its own pressure, routes and ports while drawing. It never sees these marks, the historical references, the portfolio or its resulting utility.

## The eight readings

| Reader | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Course | For a through-running brief, 42% is the extent of the best **uninterrupted chronological run of one owner**, bounded by both the horizontal span it reaches and the fraction of actual bays it visits. The rest comes from the owner's longest runs, offer-to-offer seam identity and adjacent shared endpoints. An inward spiral can end within its bay; its alternate read emphasizes within-owner continuity and its legitimate terminal. The branching spray still needs a through-running stem. A new site at the same coordinate genuinely interrupts this read. |
| Material | 82% unique/deposited line length and 18% support inside the horizontal frame. Splitting, reversing or partially overlapping a collinear deposited stroke is measured using exact linear subintervals along its axis. A transverse crossing has zero overlapping length. The crossing count is shown separately and gets no penalty. |
| Joints | Genuine witnessed forks are desired on the spray brief. Other briefs measure stray interior degree-one endpoints. The spiral exempts a real terminal only if a connected owner made at least three quarters of a revolution in a sustained sense and the ending leg was shorter than the leg at entry to that turn sequence. Adjacent material incidence and avoiding immediate reversals also contribute. Being legally terminal is distinct from being on the outer calibration boundary. The detailed X census is a separate history read. |
| Spacing | Fraction of available vertical slot count reached by distinct endpoint heights within each bay, moderate length density, clearance between occupied registers, and reach along the strip. Endpoint height count is an opportunity read; the external observer has no access to a privileged slot label for a realised site. Wider frames create an opportunity for additional heights. They never guarantee more desirable linework. |
| Motif | Finite geometric correspondence of one bay with its left–right reflection about its own occupied horizontal midpoint, together with vertical balance and extent contrast. The reflection includes the supporting direction of the segment, not simply the location of a mark. |
| Frieze | Translation agreement over adjacent actual repeats, plus left–right reflected agreement of neighboring bays about their *boundary*. Through-running work also rewards a proper shared seam. Detached motifs can repeat without an inter-bay material handoff. The motif and between-bay reflections can disagree. |
| Variety | Compare a bay with its translated predecessor, and compare the angular activity with an expectation that rises with available slots. A narrow legitimate border can repeat exactly. Separately compare the full drawing to the nearest member of a fixed external portfolio. The candidate cannot affect that memory during a search. Squared and slanted variants share turn/valence evidence, while supporting orientations still distinguish the read. |
| Reference | Compare the supplied style packet with an external geometric signature of the realised drawing. The generation, model of stroke ownership and manifold of aesthetic taste remain outside this comparison. A fixed wrong-style packet is measured alongside the intended one. |

Every committee has a byte per judge. Each nonzero byte allocates its integer number of shares out of sixteen. Moving one share or swapping an active reader with an inactive reader changes the committee without changing the total. A blank drawing or one with only one visible stroke orientation earns zero on the selection and broad utilities. This explicitly excludes a pure straight rule in this tournament. Underlying diagnostic reads such as overlap and reference distance are still available.

The broad assay uses weights 23%, 17%, 12%, 11%, 8%, 10%, 7% and 12% in the order above. It is fixed before search and is independent of the particular committee an arm selected. It shares the observable line features with committees, so success on it is a transfer test within this authored instrument. Historical similarity, native continuity/seams, interventions, and measured construction potential remain separate evidence. There are no human ratings in this study.

The packets in `Historical.cs` are published lists of ordered landmarks for the selected focus. A packet draws three actual normalized repeats. The lengths and aspect ratios of the catalogue-guided sketches are **our choices**. Only two rows were read against the construction grid of a printed plate. No candidate receives these paths as an output target.

All eight withheld source identities are distinct from the training sources. Both sides are nevertheless related through our choice of focus, structural families and the same angular approximation. The computed similarity is a distance between those declared readings. It supplies neither independent human validation nor a reconstruction of a textile's or vessel's real pen order.

## What enters each comparison

The historical signature uses unique orientation-weighted line lengths (28%), angle frequencies between distinct projected endpoint rays (20%), projected endpoint valences (15%), directional line mass across four within-bay registers (25%), relative height (6%) and line support per pitch (6%). The ray/valence read sees a projected contact only where strokes supply endpoints there. Lines crossing in their interiors remain separate strands. The measured distance is total variation for each normalized histogram and absolute difference for each scalar; one minus the weighted distance gives similarity. Crossing a curve into our eight-direction alphabet is an explicit approximation. No chronology or site identity from an object still enters historical similarity.

The finite portfolio uses a separate signature. Orientation contributes 16%, angles 35%, valences 22%, and a set of four shape/process measures 27% (height, branch-site share, actual turn activity, line support). Translation, reflection and rotation have distinct effects on these components. An exact duplicate of an archived signature has zero archive novelty. A single ideal repeated diamond can therefore be a successful rhythm but contribute little novelty to a repertoire populated with related loops. Nearly equal read signatures are not proof that two underlying machines or their future potential are identical. Within-band translational variation and different-repertoire novelty remain separate raw columns.

The empirical rationale for checking perceptual continuity and symmetry separately, including limits of transferring from a particular observer/brief, is in the [earlier source catalogue](../OrnamentPathCampaign/Criteria.md). The new test changes the treatment of crossings, gives continuous material considerably more influence, and separates motif symmetry from the larger reflected repeat.
