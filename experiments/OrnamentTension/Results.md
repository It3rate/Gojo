# What the pens learned from tension

**A request can travel from the frieze, through the machine, and change what the pen draws.** The clearest demonstration used two otherwise identical worlds, one asking for more ink and the other for less. Pens trained to answer the request deposited more *unique* line under positive than negative pressure in 1,059 of 1,152 unfamiliar comparisons. Cutting the downward channel removed that distinction. When the task was simply to make a generally good-looking border, turning feedback on was much less consistently useful.

## Where the tension lives

The pen has local signed accounts for reach/density, steering and ending or joining. An occupied collinear interval can ask for a turn. The machine owns accounts for discrepancies at the scale of the bay. The frieze owns accounts based on coarser repeat, reflection and seam observations. Local actions can change an extent binding, turn a stroke or admit an endpoint binding. Each port offers at most its capacity. Failed offers and unfinished transfers stay visible. A history of directions used successfully can influence which available direction is tried next.

These owners can send pressure up or down. A route changes strength only if its transferred unit reaches an actuator and the source later reads its own mismatch again. That is a temporal witness to possible relief. Receiving a packet alone earns no credit. The broad judge, declared engine family, portfolio and historical score remain outside the pen. [Bytes, read faces, capacities and ticket accounting](Runtime.md).

Routing adapts the strength of existing adjacent connections during a drawing. A new drawing starts from its encoded route preferences. The series owner below has its own memory between drawings. A new direct wire that bypasses a level would require a structural edit. This study tests the information that could justify that edit; it does not perform it automatically.

## The broad competition

Eight editable, named foundations competed on narrow, medium and wide bands. Four search replicates and seven arms gave 224 populations, 107,520 paid program trials and 645,120 training drawings. The prior panel-selection exercise used 3,072 pilot programs and 55,296 more drawings. The programs and sparse panels were locked before 29,952 blind case/intervention observations. Of 312 registered conditional comparisons, 107 were supported, 173 inconclusive and 32 contrary. Predictions are grouped by twelve physical bases. Their overlapping observations do not represent independent art traditions.

The coupled winners exceeded the authored default by **0.0714 broad utility on average**, supporting the directional prediction in 23 of 24 family/width comparisons. Relative to a fresh search that could change only the feedback policy, allowing path structure to evolve gained 0.0186. Equally funded random sampling, however, finished about 0.0003 below the coupled search on average. Keeping individual successful mutations is not yet a generally superior method for this grammar.

For the same coupled program, disabling pressure-driven actuation changed the material on **244/2,304** paired drawings. The mean broad gain from enabling it was **0.0015**. Against newly trained quiet machines the mean was **−0.0087**. Fixing the route strengths changed the deposited paths only **37/2,304** times. Across the coupled runs, 45,558 offered units were accounted for as 41,300 accepted, 2,185 blocked and returned, and 2,073 still in flight. There were 1,009 acts, **eight confirmed relief readings and 222 misses**. Pressure traffic was real. Evidence that it had reduced the *originating* mismatch was scarce.

The largest gain changed four disconnected single-direction strokes into a six-stroke construction. Its score rose from zero to 0.562, partly because the new drawing crossed the evaluator's two-orientation viability floor. In the largest loss, actuation broke a regular sequence of opposed chevrons and introduced an overlap, dropping the score from 0.912 to 0.639. These were selected extremes. [See both at the same scale](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/wave1/Pressure.png) · [All contrasts](Measurements.md).

One controlled test gave a machine two pen outlets and physically bound one pen's target so it could not do the same repair as the other. If the capable pen acted a beat before the source checked its need, live routing increased its late share of deliveries by **0.363** over fixed strengths. Swapping pen positions retained the effect. Both modes repaired 31.3% of seams, although they fixed different bays in 44 of 48 paired configurations. With the action and check aligned to the same beat, the effect reversed: the capable pen's late share fell from 0.553 to 0.206, and seam settlement fell from 0.313 to 0.179. A wrong starting preference could also starve the capable route of the very evidence needed to correct it. This gives us a concrete limit to routing by relief: the source must have a chance to observe the consequence at an informative time. [Routing protocol and actual matched results](../OrnamentRouteDiagnosis/README.md).

## Giving continuity a seat

Each committee assigned sixteen shares across two to four judges. Unconstrained nomination chose course on only **19 of 96** panels. A fixed panel that assigned seven shares to continuity beat the unconstrained choices by 0.0127 on average. Sparse coordination alone had supplied no reason to preserve a through-line.

In a separately locked follow-up we kept the nominees and budgets but required a minimum course weight for through-running engines, or joint weight for the intentionally terminal spiral. On reserve this improved broad utility by **0.0248** over unconstrained panel selection. The directional prediction held in six families. Historical similarity fell by **0.0404** on average. Opposed chevrons gained 0.070 in broad utility but lost 0.230 on the historical read. A better connected drawing can move away from the form we were trying to evoke. [Per-family gains, failures and thresholds](../OrnamentCommitteeStudy/README.md).

## Different pressures ask different questions

We then held four named path bodies fixed and trained only their eight-byte response policies. The response objective rewarded more unique deposited length under positive reach requests than negative ones while preserving broad form. Fourteen of sixteen directional/equivalence predictions were supported. Of 1,152 matched comparisons, 1,059 gave the requested sign, 61 reversed it and 32 had equal unique lengths. The no-request result lay in the expected order between opposite requests in 931 cases, with both inequalities strict in 686. The authored default key answered more strongly than its searched policies. Downward cuts and quiet actuation eliminated the signed response in this cohort. Fixed route strengths performed nearly as well as live learning. These results justify retaining the goal as an explicit signal separate from the exterior measure of ornament. [Raw measures and matched examples](../OrnamentDensityResponse/README.md).

## Width, terminations and joints

On the same coupled finalists, increasing capacity from two to nine B slots on each side increased mean occupied vertical range from **8.71 to 12.66 units** and vertical travel per band width from **1.74 to 2.30**. Measured turning variety increased from 0.492 to 0.574. Loose tips did not increase appreciably. Motif reflection went from 0.619 to 0.614, and mirrored agreement of adjacent bays from 0.400 to 0.384. More space afforded wandering. It did not by itself select larger-scale symmetry or make an ending purposeful. [Complete width and committee tables](Measurements.md).

Four fixtures deposit the same X-shaped material. Two opposed Vs preserve two turn histories. A direct crossing passes through two independent courses. A four-arm shared site permits a connected junction. A separate supplied construction has four directed arrivals. Their identical ink supplies insufficient evidence to choose among those histories. Positive-length collinear overlap has its own fixture. Among matched winners and their quiet controls, a straight segment followed by an eighth-turn toward the left was associated with +0.057 broad utility in crossing ribbons on 52 informative pairs. Only this one joint word cleared the joint audit's coverage rule. Its occurrence can accompany other changes in the same drawing. [Joint controls](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/wave1/Joints.png) · [All matched comparisons](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/wave1/joint-paired.csv).

To test the stronger version of the width hypothesis, we authored three compounds whose optional pens connect through an actual slot ladder. The *same* program opened one, two and three owners at widths 2, 5 and 9 respectively. The key added 0, 18 and 36 subsidiary strokes. The paired coils added 0, 42 and 84, preserving twelve intended interior terminals in the wide six-bay construction. All three compounds kept their spine handoffs on all six fresh physical bases. Capacity alone would already have admitted some third-owner ink at medium width. The explicitly connected rung accounted for its absence there. For the crest, medium width gave 0.233 mirror agreement inside a bay but 1.000 mirrored agreement across neighboring bays. Wide width admitted a second companion and raised within-bay bilateral agreement to 1.000. The key grew more intricate while its within-bay mirror read actually fell. **This is supplied machinery**, demonstrating what a trainable width-dependent connection can do. ## A fourth owner and the cost of boredom

A four-byte series owner remembers two masked frieze publications. It can switch between two stored machines and send a bounded signed request into the next one's frieze port. It sees no strokes, coordinates or external quality scores. In a sequence of eight drawings it produced **2.87 distinct material results**, versus **1.18** while holding one machine and **2.04** with simple clock alternation. Its gain in near-copy novelty over the clock missed the prespecified margin. Sending pressure as well as switching changed 1,328 of 2,304 collections relative to switching alone, but program choice supplied most of the diversity.

The upper owner has a genuine uncertainty of its own. In the primary run, we compared four independently trained machines of the same family on identical physical inputs. **1,344 pairs** published the same full byte. In **1,342 of those**, the drawn line support differed. A repeated publication warrants trying a change; it gives very little assurance that the world has repeated exactly. [Publication comparison](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/wave1/verification.json).

One running key makes the conflict visible. Holding the key gave it a strong within-band repeat score of 0.809 and zero collection novelty. Activating the series owner lowered its within-band repeat to 0.588 while raising collection novelty to 0.029. The historical profile score rose on that example. The same repetition can help the currently drawn border and bore the owner of a series. [Actual paired sequences](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/series/Tradeoff.png) · [Series results](../OrnamentTensionSeries/README.md).

## What historical resemblance can establish

The historical reader used sixteen source packets, with different objects or specimens on its training and held-out sides. It matched six of eight held-out sketches to their intended training family. Some packets are catalogue-guided abstractions and others come from the grid on a printed plate. The generated-source comparison exceeded a deliberately wrong comparison by 0.296 on average. Nevertheless, a blinded AI reader shown the sixteen predetermined drawings and eight labelled source sketches identified the intended family on only **4/16**, below the registered threshold of six. The reference scores can recognize some structure while missing what makes its family visually distinctive. No human judgments were collected. [Sources, refutations and all 64 pairings](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/history-reference/Index.html).

We also inspected the printed banks on Meyer's plate 81 and reconstructed a centerline for each of two actual figures. The supplied six-row and eight-row Gojo controls matched their own figures exactly on compatible, undisturbed bases. The best-seeded training arm matched its first figure exactly on 16/96 live drawings. **None of the 288 trained live drawings matched the second figure's registered ink exactly.** Both figures are from the same plate. The second figure was withheld from the trainer, not from the investigator who transcribed it. A printed ribbon supplies evidence for a route but does not recover the original artist's pen order. [Printed detail and native drawings](../../artifacts/local-tension-ornament/plate/images/PrintedComparison.png) · [Plate 81 in Meyer's band chapter](https://digital.ub.uni-paderborn.de/download/pdf/7739354.pdf).

Paid trials can repeat bytes. Series members are deterministic and reusable. The large totals measure computational work and include intentionally correlated trials. Every primary finalist and all 224 native search ledgers and 32 committee-evaluation ledgers reproduced in a second run with four workers. A clean runtime directory reproduced the 48 primary demonstration histories, including all pressure witnesses. The previous path experiment's 360 replay traces also retained their exact fingerprint through the original entry point. All 6,383 repository tests passed across 18 test projects. No external model is necessary to run the saved generators.

The next mechanism I would test is a pending repair that crosses a bay boundary. Let the sender retain the address of the requested seam or interval, and revisit that same promise after the pen's action can become visible. Compare it with the current next-read timing and with fixed routing on the same drawings. The present data show exactly where an otherwise useful connection can lose credit.
