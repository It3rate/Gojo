# Negotiated live ports: fixed first protocol

Status: exploratory local policy over Gojo structure, frozen before trial
results. No parameter tuning to make a producer win after observing outcomes.

## Question and scope

Can a small population offer useful live attachment points, negotiate bounded
prices, exchange actual finite resources, and revise participation when demand,
input or funding changes? What remains expensive, supplied or ineffective?

An attachment point is exposed structure AND changing data. It may select an
intermediate calculation, a terminal prediction, or a synthesized read. It is
not a symbolic result label. A consumer connects to the exposed relation and
receives the current participation through an actual live publication.

This first pass fixes the population and its possible exposed surfaces. It
tests pricing, resource accounting and connection adaptation, not autonomous
discovery of new predictors, new ports, reproduction, arbitrary merging, or a
universal market law. Fixed occupancy bounds retained population size. There is
no per-arrival retention rent; dormant structure is bounded, not costless in a
general unbounded ecology. Operating and contact costs are supplied resource
prices, not measured physical energy or exact CPU cost.

## Connected shape

```text
three retargetable source sites and native presence
  -> persistent differences a and d
  -> primitive and richer predictor structures
  -> exposed live ports, asks and operating-cost floors
  -> local consumer bid, opportunity and receiving capacity
  -> bounded paid contact / counteroffer
  -> actual drained payment and active-service cost
  -> selected live publication into consumer socket
  -> frozen service promise
  -> immediate subsequent observation on consumer's basis
  -> native error/relief/value comparison
  -> renewal, revised offer, local exploration or withdrawal
```

The same world also connects a finite resource reservoir, consumer/provider
wallets, budget caps, a spent-cost sink and transfer witnesses. Data publication
is observational; resource payment drains its source through explicit native
transfer. Inspecting a read or copied witness does not create another payment.

## Supplied population and consumers

Let `d` be the latest change and `a` its predecessor. Both are native difference
reads from the live input sites. Expose four offers from three providers:

| Provider | Offered read | Operating cost | Initial ask |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Cheap direction | signed direction of d, synthesized from its change read | 1 | 3 |
| Direction duplicate | independently wired same direction read | 2 | 5 |
| Richer interpreter | intermediate d, also used inside its extrapolator | 2 | 5 |
| Richer interpreter | extrapolated next change 2d-a | 3 | 7 |

The richer provider's two surfaces share its physical resource wallet and
one-service-per-arrival capacity. Display names describe the experiment only;
the learner traverses relations, carriers and native claims. The direction read
must be structurally inspectable, including its signed branches, rather than a
host arithmetic classifier with a numeric answer inserted afterward.

Consumer zero requests next direction; consumer one requests next change in
source-difference units. The coarse consumer reads both predictions and later
observations through its native direction surface. The fine consumer assesses
the offered signed number in source-difference units. Any cross-basis use must
expose its supplied correspondence; a direction of one is not secretly an exact
prediction of a source change of one. For this experiment, explicitly calibrate
direction +/-1 to a proposed +/-1 source-difference tick. This one-tick step is a
supplied coarse forecasting hypothesis; sign alone did not infer its magnitude.

Their exploratory bid caps are respectively 4 and 8 resource ticks. Use a
separate native learned continuation willingness, bounded to [0,10], from the
last evaluated service. Renewal uses this continuation willingness; a new trial
uses the fixed exploratory cap. Four resource ticks per positive error-relief tick is
an explicit supplied calibration; this estimates service value, not new money.
Compare with a zero/no-service prediction in the same consumer read. Report
baseline, delivered error, signed relief, gross calibrated value and paid cost
separately. Relief can be negative. Repeated flat observations are valid data;
they need not create any useful service beyond the zero baseline.

## Local negotiation and participation

Each consumer has one socket and a cyclic cursor over the locally exposed
offers. Alternate consumer admission order to make shared provider capacity
contention visible. The host supplies admission order; it does not rank offers
by waveform labels, future samples or host-computed fitness.

- At most one new candidate contact per consumer per arrival.
- A fresh contact costs one resource tick, transferred to the spent sink.
- If ask fits bid and the full service is affordable, pay the ask from consumer
  to provider; pay operating cost from provider to the spent sink. There is no
  free service after an incomplete payment.
- If ask exceeds bid, the paid contact can lower that ask by one native tick,
  bounded by the operating-cost floor. This counteroffer is for later use; no
  service is retroactively delivered. Advance the local exploration cursor.
- An incumbent can renew while its last evaluated gross benefit exceeds its
  frozen service price and the new service remains demanded and affordable.
  A first trial whose net including contact is zero can still justify renewal
  when continued service has positive net value.
- After four completed services on a connection, fund at most one local
  alternative contact. A failed contact can leave the incumbent in place if
  it remains affordable; an agreed challenger gets a prospective trial.
- Harm or lack of sufficient continuation value withdraws participation. Harm
  can also lower the offered ask by one tick, respecting its cost floor.
- Prices only fall in this first supplied policy. Strategic price increases,
  general auctions and globally optimal partner selection are not implemented.

Keep an unchanged selected publication attached across renewals. Clear its
previous live pulse before changing or withdrawing the connection. Detachment
alone does not erase already delivered state. Missing immediate outcomes remain
unresolved and cannot be replaced with a later convenient observation.

An issued service captures prediction, interpretation basis, price and demand
before its outcome. Future offerings may change; captured obligations must not
silently change with them. More general adaptive coarsening is a future law,
not a behavior inferred merely from choosing among supplied surfaces.

## Finite terrain and funding

Each run begins with one reservoir of 4,096 resource ticks. Transfer initial
wallets of eight ticks to each consumer and four to each provider from that
reservoir. Consumer wallet caps are 16; provider reserve caps are 32. The
environment supplies requested per-arrival inlets from the SAME reservoir,
normally five ticks for the direction consumer and eight for the fine consumer.
Admission is limited by reservoir balance and wallet headroom.

Before a sale that would exceed the provider reserve cap after operating cost,
recycle the computed excess from its existing reserve to the reservoir. Keep
that recycle distinct from spent computation. No subsidy is created by reading,
copying, attaching or successfully predicting. Total resources across reservoir,
all wallets and spent sink remain 4,096, including any unresolved material
tension. Unmet draws and partial transfers must remain visible.

The terrain here is supplied input variation and consumer obligations, not a
geometric grid or claimed emergent species fitness. A source can be observed
through several ports; doing so must not multiply its funding inlet.

## Fixed trials

Start with source level 100, then admit these increments:

1. Twenty changes of +3.
2. Twenty-four changes repeating [2,3,4,5,6,7].
3. One unknown observation, explicitly disclosed as a missing input.
4. Twenty-four changes of -5.
5. Sixteen known flat changes.
6. Twenty-four changes of +4.

Run this source through four fixed controls:

- Both demands present; normal inlets; initial local cursors [0,0].
- Fine demand withdrawn during the changing-positive and negative blocks, then
  restored; funding otherwise unchanged so demand and affordability are distinct.
- Both demands present; inlets reduced to [1,2] during those two blocks, then
  restored to [5,8].
- Normal demand/funding, but initial local cursors [1,2], checking sensitivity
  to initial encounter order. Do not require order-invariant winners.

Labels and future values are external stimulus/report data. They do not enter
the learner. Funding and demand apply to the newly issued service; feedback
honors the previously frozen service, including at phase boundaries.

## Evidence and checks

Keep reusable operational structure, bounded current traces, at most one pending
service per consumer and the latest diagnostic evidence. Historical report rows
are outside the learner. Save selected native witnesses and final worlds rather
than every full observation graph.

Measure actual delivered data, port identities, agreements/counteroffers,
payments/costs/recycling, unused and blocked capacity, outcome qualification,
consumer errors, renewals/switches, final asks, wallets and native conservation.
Count evaluated versus unresolved promises separately. Report retained graph
size and temporary allocation honestly; supplied accounting costs do not prove
the implementation is computationally cheap.

Focused checks cover live intermediate/synthesized ports, native signed reads,
prospective evaluation, frozen contracts, pure reads, transfer conservation,
full affordability, bounded local search, real detach/reattach, stable equation
identity and bounded retained history. Do not require an intended winner or
overall performance improvement to make the checks pass. If observed behavior
is informative, render an observer-only native Avalonia/Skia replay with the
actual offers, live readings, connections and resource transfers.
