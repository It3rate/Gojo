What this tests

A straight line can describe the past exactly and still be a poor prediction. Reversals and sudden steps test that distinction; gaps test whether unknown outcomes stay unknown. No errors are sent back to select a more successful rule.

The hold-last comparison uses ordinary exact arithmetic outside Gojo. A control that makes no predictions has zero coverage and no error score. This run does not establish a general winner, learned model selection, or a benefit over conventional mathematics.
