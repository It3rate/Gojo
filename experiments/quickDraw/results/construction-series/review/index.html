What this experiment establishes

The steps are authored recipes. Geometry, references and checks are read from the retained native structure. Fading or hiding a guide changes this view; it does not erase that structure.

Proportions remain numeric. Coarse steps omit detail while keeping their construction references. This is a foothold for future hint compression and learning, rather than a trained model of drawing intention.

Earlier steps remain inspectable. A more detailed result adds a new reading of the construction; the rough structure remains a valid guide.
