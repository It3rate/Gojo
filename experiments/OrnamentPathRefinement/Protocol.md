# Can specific construction witnesses change what the tournament learns?

First-wave winners sometimes looked like ladders or subdivided boxes when the brief called for a winding key or a spray. This separate follow-up selects two briefs after that observation. Its question is whether a new reader of the actual pen history can select programs that produce more of the requested construction on unfamiliar bases, and at what cost to the earlier judges.

## Registered before training

- Use the same sealed path engine, judges, four training bases per replicate, eight validation realizations and 24 reserve realizations (12 paired parents) as wave one. The follow-up cannot open reserve until every finalist is locked and the shared release is authorized. All stored programs are portable original Gojo bytes and draw without these observers.
- Two briefs: a pottery key with a single returning course, and a spray with a through-stem and attached subsidiary arm. Compare three separately trained arms at identical budgets: baseline ranks by the exact original full brief weights, blend ranks by the arithmetic mean of original utility and the specialized history utility, and history ranks by the latter alone. All three measurements are recorded even for rejected trials. The new reader rewards structure explicitly; the study tests learned use of that read, not spontaneous invention of the reader.
- Four independent replicate search streams, each with 64 generations of 64 charged proposals. Eight training elites survive a generation; each trial has its own deterministic seed, identical across arms within the brief/replicate. The first generation and every eighth opportunity thereafter draw from a mix of the original six encoded foundations, variations of them and free bodies. Other trials mutate training elites or cross their owners. Retain every paid proposal, including duplicates. The 98,304 proposals run 393,216 training drawings. Choose one finalist from eight by validation, independently for each brief/arm/replicate; lock all 24 before opening reserve. Completed island files support resume without altering random streams.
- Register six directional predictions for each brief: blend over baseline on history and on material-witness frequency; history over baseline on history and on material-witness frequency; blend over history-only on original full utility; blend and baseline approximately equal on original full utility. A practical margin is 0.04 for the two utility scores and 0.10 for witnessed-cell fraction. Average over the two paired realizations and four replicate comparisons within each reserve parent. At least eight of the twelve parent differences meeting the margin supports a direction, eight meeting the reverse margin contradict it. Equivalence requires eight strictly within the margin; eight beyond it on a common side is contrary. Otherwise retain an inconclusive result.
- All matching seeds, validations, controls, failed hypotheses and outputs stay in the report. The parent is a parameter-basis family, not an independent historical culture. Separately show a common ordinary reserve basis chosen **in advance** (parent zero, view zero) and all four replicates. Choose representative replicate by mean validation over the three arms, ties to lower replicate. Render the actual cells from the stored programs at one common scale per row. Examples are illustrative; do not select an attractive reserve case to replace the prescribed one.

## What the new observers require

The returning-key observer scans each owner in actual drawing order in each bay. A site discontinuity, diagonal or direct reversal breaks the candidate chain. Consecutive straight edges collapse into one directed leg. A qualifying interval includes at least five legs with successive quarter turns, both horizontal directions and both vertical directions, horizontal advance, and separate upright and returning passages. Strength depends on the smaller forward/backward and up/down excursions and horizontal advance relative to pitch. It also values returning near the starting register. All material in the drawing is charged for overdraw, including reversed and partial overlap. A row of ladder rungs or a bag of detached corners cannot be one witnessed key. There is no requirement that the interval be an authored meander.

The spray observer requires three distinct nonzero rays at the same identity-bearing site. A main owner actually enters, then departs that site along its forward direction (a turn of up to 45 degrees is allowed). A chronologically witnessed branch draws a distinct arm from that same site. The stem, arm, and their longitudinal and lateral extents must survive the selected scale. An additional connected arm segment increases the read, but the arm need not make a mirror pair or close into a loop. A root with two detached strokes or several coincident positions cannot satisfy it. All geometry must be read from the actually executed lines. An intentional arm can be visually useful without this stricter stem law.

The specialized score for either brief is the per-cell best qualifying strength averaged over the actual cells, multiplied by the overall unique/deposited length ratio. A prospective independent witness counts a strong cell (strength at least 0.40 before the overall overdraw penalty), also reported separately. This witness shares premises with the specialized score. It is a stricter structural confirmation, not an independent aesthetic validation. A six-percent pitch cutoff for each participating leg or required reach excludes microscopic accents; scores reach full scale credit at one fifth of the pitch. Original metrics retain their missing-opportunity handling. Calibration fixtures and visible counterexamples are separate from training.

The historically grounded reasons for selecting meanders and returning paths, and the distinction between mathematical and aesthetic reads, remain documented in the [first-wave criteria](../OrnamentPathCampaign/Criteria.md). All constants and weights above are experimental choices. No human votes train either observer.
