# Where operations live in Gojo

Discussion note · 25 November 2026 · Repository baseline `bece9fc9`

## Position

An operation belongs in **connected structure**. A small operation can live in one wired relation. A larger one can be a local structure with ports, internal laws and settlement of its own. A plain connection can supply a participant to either. The receiving structure decides what to do with that participation. Its meaning should still be discoverable if we remove the name “multiply” and traverse the binding, basis, output and any route that acts on the output.

I would solidify this distinction before choosing a general numeric representation. I would use a few tiny counts and measurements alongside the qualitative cases. Without a bounded numeric check, “combine,” “scale” and “repeat” can look identical until we ask what unit comes out, what a receiver can accept, or where a remainder goes. The current EncodingC track is pre-numeric in the sense of starting from order, occupancy and a few distinctions. It already has finite unary counting and qualitative arithmetic. The older Gojo numeric kernel gives us a second set of reference cases for partitions, carry and measurement. The two surfaces have different execution contracts and a general bridge remains open. [Canonical prompt, §§4.6–4.7, 8 and 9.6–9.7](GojoSystemPrompt.md) · [EncodingC, what runs and what remains open](../../Gojo.EncodingC/README.md#what-runs-itself-and-what-the-host-still-does)

My preference is to give a readable result an **output role** first. That role can be open, read through a connected view, materialized independently, or bound deliberately to a receiver. A new carrier per calculation would make live operations expensive and obscure the difference between observation and state. Direction matters where one role supplies a basis or controller and another supplies participation, and again where a result is applied. Equality of two scalar results is a separate read.

## What changes when we say add, multiply or Boolean?

### Addition has a receiving basis or a join

Before numbers, two traversals can be joined at a shared or explicitly corresponding site. Two arrivals can also occupy one receiving scope together, retaining their sources. These are already discernible acts. A scalar sum is a further compatible read. For example, appending a two-tick traversal to a three-tick traversal constructs an intermediate site and a route. Depositing two and three units into a common stock raises the accounted amount to five if both are admitted. Two observers can start at the same site and fold their reports to five while leaving the stock available.

A pointwise sum needs a correspondence of positions and compatible contributions. Different dimensions or different tick scales can keep their separate participation until a common calibrated read exists. Adding positions is generally origin-dependent. Translating a position by a displacement, composing two displacements, adding two lengths, and joining two paths all give different promises about the output. For cancellation, retain source identity and orientation. Equal-looking but separately observed samples provide weaker evidence than the same sample used in two opposite roles.

### Multiplication has more than one physical construction

| Construction | What lives in the structure | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repeat a contribution** | One contribution or reusable pattern, an ordered set of occasions or placements, a repeat controller, and any cumulative read. Distinct material copies require supply and identity. | Four placements of three apples give twelve apples if all twelve are supplied. Four reads of one stock of three are four observations of three apples. |
| **Form a product** | Two factor roles, their independent or deliberately repeated participation, and a resulting product basis. An occupied product can pair admitted positions from those factors. A later measure can fold the pairs without storing every cell when the exposed factorization suffices. | A rectangle made from `3 m` and `4 m` has `12 m²`. An unbound edge leaves a partially assembled area with one known factor. Equal-length edges retain their separate identities. |
| **Apply a gain or ratio** | A controller or calibrated ratio, the target's exposed displacement or contribution, an origin or pivot when relevant, and the transformation's output correspondence. The target's dimension is preserved for a dimensionless gain. | `(3 m / 1 m) × 4 m = 12 m`. Changing the reference to `2 m` changes the output to `6 m`. |

These constructions can agree on $\mathbb{N}$ when the only question is a count. They can disagree about units, identity, material effects, and which value acts as a controller. A product between predicates on the **same** partition selects common admissibility per cell. Pairing predicates from **independent** axes instead selects a set of tuples. In the latter case counting all independent pairs can yield the product of counts. On a shared axis the overlap can be anything from zero to the smaller count. Shared position or sample identity must be carried into the relation, especially when a law reads one role twice.

I would take the rectangle intuition literally when we have two axes and an area question, and the stretch intuition literally when the selected structure exposes a displacement and a gain. They share mechanics such as ordered roles, alignment, projection, and counting. The differences deserve structural evidence, even when the final code can evaluate both by the same integer multiplication.

For a pair of two-sided factors, keeping the four pairwise contributions is useful independently of any numerical display. A current kernel conformance example retains the source pair for each term and reads through those sources when it folds the result. An expanded term's face can record involvement of an opposed operand. Selecting a final scalar sign or complex coefficient requires retaining the role of both operands and the chosen fold. [Expanded product conformance](../../Tests/Gojo.Tests/Core/OperationConformanceTests.cs#L105)

### Boolean is a read over a scope, and a gate is an action

Take a scope from 0 to 12, with occupied spans from 1 to 7 and 5 to 10. Partition at their endpoints. The interiors from 1 to 5 and 7 to 10 have exactly one source. The interval from 5 to 7 has both. The rest of the supplied scope is uncovered. Read the boundary sites separately according to the declared endpoint inclusion. Geometric overlap persists if both spans run in the same direction. The current span reader's **opposed** overlap measures cancellation opportunity and can be zero in that very case. Conversely zero net participation can retain two opposing contributions. [Span participation reads](../Core/Relation.Reads.cs) · [Span partitioning](../Reads/RelationReads.cs)

For an exhaustive, closed membership question we can call the uncovered part a complement. An open boundary, an unbound contribution, or a blocked lane leaves a different witness. EncodingC's presence polarity also belongs to a particular plane. A zero bit can say a tuple is inadmissible, a site is unoccupied, or a sign is the recessive member, depending on what that plane shows. A present ODV sample below detection, an unselected site, a known empty region and a contradictory support each carry different evidence. For three or more inputs, name the selected evidence explicitly. Two of three occupied inputs satisfy “at least two.” Three of three satisfy “all” and odd parity. Exactly one requires a single occupied input.

A Boolean relation may answer which cases are admissible. Driving flow through a selected case requires a wired transition, a receiver, and a rule for accepted and blocked units. A shared gate can be read many times, while one conserved unit can go to one receiving route. This is where the low-level connection can physically realize logic through its topology. ## A larger structure as the target

Take a plate with a rectangular outline, a hole, mounting datum, and an interface to a surrounding assembly. The following requests give four different outcomes.

| Request | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Repeat the plate three times | Three instances with explicit placements and correspondences. Each instance has its own identity. The same design can be shared by reference. |
| Stretch it threefold along one exposed axis | Displacements along that axis are transformed. Area scales by 3 in a calibrated plane. A circular hole generally reads as an ellipse if its geometry participates. A demanded circular passage can stay a connected mismatch. |
| Dilate the plate uniformly about the datum | Selected points follow $p'=o+3(p-o)$. In-plane lengths scale by 3 and areas by 9. Thickness and mass have their own participation. With constant thickness and density, mass scales by 9, or by 27 if the thickness scales too. |
| Form a product with a separate thickness extent | The plate's occupied area and the thickness can supply a volume basis. A hole's exclusion needs correspondence into that product when it is meant to be a through-hole. |

The target is a **focus and an exposed promise**, for example “the metric outline and hole about this datum.” The focus may discover joined endpoints or dependencies on use. Keep genuine shared sites shared in the output, keep independent equal-coordinate sites distinguishable, and carry parts outside the focus with their context. If a proposed plate movement conflicts with a fixed mounting site, the boundary must expose which choices are admissible. The application can include the linked site, preserve the interface if the transform permits it, or retain an open result with the mismatch. Similarity by a positive gain can preserve enough structure for a proxy to answer coarse questions. An exact reified patch may need more evidence. Which result is allowed to commit is a separate question. Uniform negative gain in a plane is a half-turn plus stretch, while reversing just one axis reflects. Zero gain collapses the selected geometric extent and leaves the inverse open.

In a workspace, a candidate sees the source by reference and holds changes where it needs them. Scope can start at the head exposed by the ports and refine to local uses. The candidate can be compared or dropped. Writeback needs the application's checked correspondence and appropriate settlement, including a stale-source check and a coherent group where changes depend on each other. A workspace gap is evidence for a supposition. Provenance for a candidate hole, attachment or receiving surface points to where it was introduced. The observations of the original plate stay independently readable. EncodingC implements narrow workspace commit and fork semantics. The general transform/geometry and kernel bridge remain open. ## Does A × B change B?

Give the relation concrete input and output roles. Whether $B$ is changed follows from what the output is bound to and how its execution is timed. With gain 2 and initial value 5, the table compares how each use treats the would-be product 10.

| Use | Connected evidence after the first result |
| --- | --- |
| Read $A$ and $B$ | The result 10 can be read through its output. The provided inputs remain available for further reads. |
| Publish or crystallize | Publish a live output held against a separately exposed baseline, or capture an independent output with a stable tick promise. A live read tracks later input. A captured value has its own state and provenance. |
| Apply once to $B$ | Bind a receiver to $B$ and sample $B_0=5$ at the application's before boundary. Request 10 and let the explicit value or structural port settle or decline the change. Record requested and accepted change, including a partial or refused application. |
| Feed back to $B$ | Connect each committed after-state to the next before-state. With an exact unconstrained gain of 2, snapshots are 5, 10, 20, 40, and so on. The repeat controller, stop condition and time boundary matter. |
| Ask $B=2B$ at one instant | This is a constraint on one shared sample. In exact scalar arithmetic its only solution is 0. With $B$ known to be 5 it is incompatible. Neither read authorizes erasing the state of $B$. |

The difference between the third and fourth rows is easy to lose if each settlement pass rereads the receiver as its own operand. Settling one event and advancing time are distinct acts. Aliasing an output to its input needs a captured before-state, an independent baseline or intentionally wired feedback. Use the outcome after the receiving boundary as evidence of what happened. A request for 10 with capacity 8 leaves a claim about the shortfall after acceptance. A separate transfer witness establishes how much physical stock is waiting to enter.

With the exact scalar question $A B=0$, a known zero factor leaves the other factor open. A solved role provides evidence for a possible action, and the receiver still governs writes. ## Where numerics enter

A partition is already useful with only order and selected landmarks. Equal steps require additional evidence. A rung says which distinctions are available at one scale and how it corresponds to the next. A carry uses an explicit exchange and settlement route. The origin, unit shape, quantum, capacity and exchange need their own connections. Two equally sized schedules or parallel sets of marks need a calibration before their ticks can measure a shared magnitude. A bridge can be useful before it claims any scale. There is already a small example of the bridge inside EncodingC. A supplied 3:1 carry law handles seven fine arrivals as one fine unit and two coarse units. The represented stock is conserved in the selected fine basis, $1+3(2)=7$. Without the carry, the fine stock holds three and four arrivals are blocked. This is one selected two-rung circuit. The Gojo numeric kernel likewise has explicit wrap, carry, radix and ratio fixtures. Its selected ratio projections and its material refinement paths are distinct. Three examples constrain what a future ladder must promise.

- **Add and then observe, or observe and then add.** With exact source reads, $17+28=45$ gives four tens and five left over. If only whole tens are exposed per source, the immediate sum sees $1+2=3$ tens. It may separately retain residues 7 and 8 if offered. Recombining those residues crosses another ten. A deliberately lossy port may lack that refinement and should keep its weaker promise. [Numeric conformance](GojoMathConformance.md) · [Finite-read fixture](GojoFiniteReadFixture.md)
- **Product needs the available factor resolution.** From $(10+7)(20+8)$, the whole-tens product is 200. With the two refinements connected, the remaining terms are 80, 140 and 56 and the exact sum is 476. An exact positional product of digits in radix places $i,j$ contributes to place $i+j$ before carry. Rungs with different units or mixed calibration may require an explicit map into a compatible product basis. If a receiver asks a cheaper question and has only tens, retain the resulting coarse evidence under its selected loss law.
- **Quantizing is an observable operation.** In the native finite fixture with capacity $[0,15]$, $(14+3)-3$ reads 12 after intermediate clipping while $14+(3-3)$ reads 14. Exact arithmetic and bounded execution can both be lawful within their declared promises. In a qualitative ODV read a selected positive sample below detection can still contribute to a later detectable sum. Absence, algebraic zero, and a zero observation at one resolution remain distinguishable. [Finite read and tests](GojoFiniteReadFixture.md) · [ODV addition](../../Gojo.EncodingC/Measure/Odv.cs)

There is a productive tension between making primitives coarse and wanting exact arithmetic. A pre-numeric law can establish joining, admissibility or progress. An exact reader can verify a declared finite integer case, and a ladder can refine what is seen at a cost. Give each law its selected promise, and make the choice to request an exact or finer read explicit in the connection. This leaves room to develop numerics as partitions and rungs once we know what the operations need from them.

## Historical constructions worth borrowing

### Old Babylonian calculation

Jens Høyrup's translations and reconstruction distinguish several actions that a modern rendering can flatten into multiplication. A table asks for a stated number of steps of an abstract number. Raising can give the measured quantity for a concrete proportion, such as scaling a standard-thickness base to a chosen height. Making two sides hold constructs a rectangle and may be followed by a separate read of its measure. Physical repetition or doubling can instead assemble a rectangle from two copies of a right triangle. Høyrup also distinguishes accumulating measuring numbers from physically joining same-kind magnitudes. That vocabulary is evidence for distinct procedures in the texts he studies. The Gojo inference is that one numeric backend can serve several constructions while their factor roles, units and state effects remain readable. [Høyrup, *Algebra in Cuneiform*, chapter 1](https://www.mprl-series.mpg.de/media/textbooks/2/2/textbooks2ch2.pdf)

The multiplication tablet catalogued as P493017 lists eight steps of a written `7:30` as a written `1`. As a modern absolute normalization, read `0:07:30` as $7/60+30/3600=1/8$ and get $1$. Read the same factor digits as $7(60)+30=450$ and the corresponding output rung is $3600$. The table supplies a relative digit pattern. The application supplies a scale. Christine Proust's analysis of Nippur school tablets likewise distinguishes metrological surfaces from the positional computations used with them. Her study of large-number multiplication reconstructs partial-product procedures from stereotyped errors. That procedure is a plausible historical reconstruction and a useful analogy for carrying across Gojo rungs. [CDLI tablet P493017](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/493017) · [Proust on Nippur](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.24033/rhm.144/) · [Proust on large multiplications](https://www.numdam.org/item/RHM_2000__6_2_293_0/)

Reciprocals give a second lesson. In sexagesimal notation $1/4=0;15$ is finite. Multiplying 30 by it gives $7;30$, i.e. $7.5$. The reciprocal $1/7$ has no terminating sexagesimal expansion, yet $21/7=3$ settles exactly. Whether a particular quotient is exact, whether a reciprocal fits the chosen radix, and whether a table contains a row are separate questions. A missing row can motivate another resolution or procedure while retaining the original query. [CDLI reciprocal tablet P252955](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/252955) · [Høyrup, chapter 1](https://www.mprl-series.mpg.de/textbooks/2/2/index.html)

In BM 13901 problem 1, Høyrup reads the procedure as constructing a unit-width strip from the side, rearranging it around the square and filling the corner with a half-unit square. In modern dimensional bookkeeping, with side $x$ and reference width $u$, $x^2+ux=3u^2/4$ becomes $(x+u/2)^2=u^2$. The positive side comes out $u/2$. The moment worth carrying into Gojo is the attached unit-width strip. It turns a side measurement into area participation that can be joined to a square. The chosen positive geometric side supplies a domain. Both details would disappear in a bare symbolic adder. [Høyrup's translation and reconstruction, chapter 3](https://www.mprl-series.mpg.de/media/textbooks/2/4/textbooks2ch4.pdf)

### Greek magnitude, commercial arithmetic and a calibrated product

Euclid's *Elements* VII defines numerical multiplication in ordered repeat roles and proves equality when they are exchanged in VII.16. Book V compares ratios of same-kind magnitudes by equimultiples. That comparison can be meaningful without representing both magnitudes as integer multiples of a single tick. Book II.1 makes distributivity visible by dividing a side of a rectangle and composing the smaller rectangles. Book VI.19 relates the area ratio of similar triangles to the duplicate ratio of corresponding sides. For Gojo, commutativity belongs to a proved or selected output read. ## The landmark oscillator

The landmark intuition works with two state roles. Displacement $x$ is read relative to the landmark, and velocity $v$ carries the previous motion. The restoring acceleration is opposite to displacement and proportional to it, $a=-\omega^2x$. Speed increases on approach, the velocity continues through the landmark where acceleration is zero, and the restoring acceleration on the other side slows it. At a turning point velocity is zero and the restoring acceleration is largest in magnitude. Constant-magnitude attraction would give a different waveform. In the ideal linear undamped model, $x$ and $v/\omega$ are same-unit quadratures of one state, with sign and phase fixed by the chosen orientation. [OpenStax, simple harmonic motion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/15-1-simple-harmonic-motion)

There are two physically readable realizations. One starts with a landmark, a position and velocity basis, a restoring gain, and a rule taking the old state to the next elapsed-time sample. The other starts with a phase contour, a seam and calibrated radius and advances the phase, exposing two projections in quadrature. A numeric reader can later evaluate either when its promise is to recover the same quantities. Even a discrete contour can give useful phase evolution before an arbitrary-angle sine read exists. For example, the exact rational turn

$$
(x',u')=\big((3x+4u)/5,\;(-4x+3u)/5\big), \qquad u=v/\omega
$$

preserves $x^2+u^2$. It takes $(5,0)$ to $(3,-4)$ and then to $(-7/5,-24/5)$. A whole-tick execution cannot keep those second coordinates exactly. A refinement, explicit approximation or retained loss belongs in the transition witness. This is a fixed phase-step model. It agrees with sinusoid samples under the corresponding elapsed-time calibration. A separate physical oscillator model needs its restoring response and a selected time law. Repeated rounding can drift or damp a discrete orbit. The quarter positions, direction of crossing and change of speed can already be qualitative evidence.

Core4's boundary overflow axioms already proposed a landmark oscillator, and its trolley experiments exercised terminal reflection. The latter establishes a discrete boundary response, which is a different mechanism. Current EncodingC has a toggle and a qualified direction/side vocabulary. Its order-four generator is deferred. The existing Gojo numeric contour fixture applies discrete turns using supplied radial geometry. Continuous phase, arbitrary geometric projection and integrated spring dynamics remain open. These precedents support building the trajectory and reading the waveform from it when the behavior itself matters. ## Three approaches to compare

| Approach | What it makes simple | What would expose a weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Locally wired relation first** | Bind selected source and output roles to an admitted law. Keep input supports and output evidence visible. A compact bounded table can implement a small relation, while a product can also be constructed through factored row and column participation. Existing relation laws already give a model for reversible questions over a forward-readable result. | A growing table becomes an operator dispatch in disguise if it imports all of its semantics from a host predicate. Examine whether a tiny bounded product read agrees with counting distinct pairs in the constructed product, preserves repeated sample identity, and keeps reverse ambiguity. Larger ranges belong in composable rungs and factored structure. |
| **Scoped transform first** | Express a reusable gain, repeat or fold over the slots a structure exposes. The application holds the focus, datum or source basis, counterpart bindings, carried complement and patch. This fits geometry, nested systems and comparison of alternative applications. | Requiring a new graph for every scalar read wastes the live structure. A genuine assembly example should show which shared sites and constraints carry through, which remain unresolved, and whether a coarse proxy can answer before a full patch is needed. |
| **Coupled flow first** | A route and a transition turn an operation into something that changes the world. This fits a gated transfer, accumulation in a receiving stock, an amplifier, a repeat event, or an oscillator. Accounting and time make the physical meaning testable. | Flow alone can conflate an observation with an expenditure or a settlement pass with a time step. Ask whether two observers may reuse the same input, whether two takers can both spend it, and what a contested or blocked transition exposes to its next neighbor. |

I favor a locally wired relation as the shared starting point, a scoped transform where a target needs correspondence, and a coupled transition where state or time is part of the requested behavior. These can compose. If the admitted law can expose an output, a receiver can choose to observe it, bind it into a candidate construction or enact a response under a separate commitment.

The most telling comparison would put the same few participants through three readings. An exact count product from distinct ordered pairs and a same-unit repeat should both read 12 from 3 and 4, with different factor roles and witnesses. A common-partition AND should follow the actual shared occupied sites even when both counts stay fixed. Then bind a gain to a scoped target and ask what differs among a current read, a single writeback and a timed recurrence. If the exposed structure explains all of those distinctions before we inspect the helper name, we have a strong account of where the operation belongs.
