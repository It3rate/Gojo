# A hundred editable lattice constructions

This extends the [first ladder study](GojoLatticeConstructionLadders.md) into
100 programmatically generated, unfilled scaffolds: twenty construction
families, each with five bounded variations. A rung can withdraw constraints,
replace a focus, regroup paths, introduce detail or apply a transform. It is
not restricted to adding information.

Open the [interactive catalogue](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/lattice-catalogue/gallery.html),
the [whole sheet](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/lattice-catalogue/overview.html),
or the [construction strips](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/lattice-catalogue/construction-strips.html).
The gallery includes local ratings and export/import for a later preference
study. These ratings do not modify the native programs.

## What a rung is allowed to change

The existing native `ReplacePaths` contract already permits selecting and
removing actual old paths and replacing the complete current port and closure
contracts. This experiment exercises that behavior explicitly. Every family
has a release step before its replacement or assembly. A one-contour source
can open its closing span and withdraw its closure obligation. A source with
several paths can carry one and release the others.

The old path, role and closure promises remain present in the earlier rung.
They are no longer obligations of the edited current program. This is a
structural edit; it does not pretend to retract previously delivered flow.
Selecting the whole old focus and replacing it with another construction is
legal, with a visible replacement correspondence and no claim of preserved
endpoints or topology.

A focused test also reformulates four independent sides into one closed box
path with the same visible outline. The actual path grouping and publication
roles change even though the picture does not. This distinguishes a change
of concept/read from a geometric displacement. Attempting to leave a literally
empty current path program is still blocked by the present program contract;
the failed intent and original source remain visible.

Historical records are not silently rewritten. A revised starting definition
can be a new branch, but automatic rebasing and replay of every downstream edit
after changing an old definition are not implemented by this catalogue.

Twenty cases have an additional actual coarse compartment rung. The fine
diamond and fan stages carry their actual envelope paths and publication roles;
the paired-box and lozenge stages explicitly regroup their coarse compartment
into closed siblings. Their coarse concepts are available through native
ancestry rather than only a caption.

The twenty templates are authored. The five variations are generated by a
program, not individually supplied images or LLM descriptions. They vary
slip, notch, partition, hub, span and assembly choices. The catalogue does not
claim autonomous discovery of these templates, or that a grid logically
implies every later pattern merely because it is retained as an ancestor.

Starts and chord displacements are native motion words. A chord is simultaneous
displacement, not an implied sequence of horizontal then vertical cursor
movements. Transform, copy, closure and seam receipts remain connected to the
participants they actually use. No picture is read back from SVG or PNG.

## Rules and evidence

The mandatory assessment requires a complete native unit, execution and
declared seam contract; two independent repeat directions; no zero chord; and
no positive-length span retraced within an authored path. Opposed directions
separated by other motion are legal. Repeated traversal of the same span in
one path fails even when it is not an immediate reversal.

Several other reads remain separate:

| Read | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Shared boundary histories | Two cell outlines use the same already-joined support. This is not automatically a path retrace. |
| Joined branch | Actual common-site incidence, authenticated through exposed roles. |
| Unjoined crossing | Intersecting projected strokes without a common site. This is allowed in an intentional strand design. |
| Unjoined contact | Projected endpoint or T-contact without a corresponding shared site. Disclosed, never silently joined. |
| Overlapping projected support | Positive-length overlap beyond the already-shared endpoint-pair support grouping. A refinement or span-contact question may be needed. |
| Finite frontier | An offered neighbour lies outside the selected sample. This is distinct from a failed interior seam. |
| Open/blocked obligation | The declared native promise is incomplete or rejected. It cannot be repaired by a plausible drawing. |

All 100 final three-by-three fields pass the mandatory assessment. They also
have zero projected overlap pairs and zero unjoined contacts under the selected
common-reach diagnostic. Ninety are connected finite fields. Five fret cases
have nine components and eighteen unjoined crossings each; five ribbon cases
have six components. Those are deliberate separate-strand readings rather
than claims of a connected cell tiling.

The read retains native accepted, blocked and open witnesses. Two counterexamples
show an actual immediate retrace and an invalid neighbouring-role displacement.
The gallery links their native failed evidence.

## Comparison, compactness and the meaning of pre-numeric

Each saved construction packet contains the current ladder and executable
periodic program, plus all displayed stage declarations. It is imported through
the existing exact ladder reader before the final field is executed and assessed.
Current motif identity and retained ancestor identities are checked after
transport. Construction roots contain relations and placeholders, with no
carriers or carrier locations.

This is uncalibrated finite repetition, not number-free authoring. Host integers
choose how many occurrences of one shared reach enter a word. No measured
length, scalar angle or arbitrary floating coordinate is needed by the native
executor. Display bases choose screen proportions. A later common-reach
diagnostic can count formal direction participation to compare those structures.

The finite support fingerprint ignores translation, uniform positive integral
rescaling, drawing order, duplicate boundary histories and straight degree-two
subdivision. It retains actual junction partitions, so joined and unjoined
crossings differ. The current 100 fingerprints are distinct. This is neither
general graph isomorphism nor an infinite-pattern classification theorem.

Detailed live rule witnesses are larger than the generating programs. By default
the runner saves five representative full rule packets and the two rejected
examples, while retaining summaries for every case. `--full-witnesses` saves
all full rule packets when that extra evidence is wanted. It does not change
generation or assessment.

## Seeing the edit and rating the result

Each design has a sequence of actual native stages shown side by side. The
slider selects one. Its local change diagram uses the same scale for source,
result and overlay:

- rust dashed paths are the selected old focus;
- grey paths are carried participation;
- teal paths are introduced current participation.

The renderer takes these sets from `LatticeCatalogueStepRead`, which reads
native source/focus/result correspondence. It does not infer changes from
colour, captions or image differences. A word refinement can recreate an
unchanged path wrapper; actual preserved start/chord identities and native
correspondence still justify its carried read.

The complexity estimate is an explicit, coarse count-based diagnostic. It is
not autonomous conceptual substructure discovery or a universal beauty score.
Review cues identify crossings or other support worth inspecting. The more
useful next evidence is which designs people rate highly and why.

Ratings from one to five and notes are stored in the browser where available.
Exported JSON includes the question, design ID, support fingerprint and packet
hash. Import accepts matching fingerprints and valid ratings. The browser can
filter by family, rating, crossings, contacts and overlap. No ratings are sent
to a service or used to silently change the native construction.

## Reproduction and next questions

```powershell
dotnet run --project experiments/QualitativeFrieze -c Release -- --lattice-catalogue
dotnet run --project experiments/QualitativeFrieze -c Release -- --lattice-catalogue --samples 2 --output artifacts/lattice-catalogue-small
dotnet test Tests/QualitativeExecution.Tests/QualitativeExecution.Tests.csproj -c Release --filter "FullyQualifiedName~LatticeCatalogue"
dotnet test Gojo.slnx -c Release
```

The retained `diagnostics.json`, `verification.json` and browser check report
record the executed scope. Focused tests cover all hundred native constructions,
withdrawal and regrouping, actual coarse-envelope identity, negative edits,
support comparisons, uncertainty and rejected rules.

Useful next experiments are preference-guided proposals that change one native
instruction, explicit whole-span contact agreements, and matching an unfamiliar
field against several of these constructions. A learned preference should be
tested against held-out families rather than simply rewarding density or
symmetry everywhere. Automatic rebasing, unconstrained aesthetic search,
occupied-face tiling proofs and Escher-style curved interlocking contours
remain future work.
