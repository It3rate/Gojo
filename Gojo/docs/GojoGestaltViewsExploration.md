# Gestalt cues and constructive ornament views

Exploration plan, 2026-10-20. This records the next proposed experiment after
the [interior lattice study](GojoLatticeInteriorStudy.md). The mechanisms below
are distinguished from a general implemented perceptual or concept-learning
system. This round publishes the recorded lattices; it does not run the new
Gestalt experiments.

The subsequent [construction-context experiments](GojoConstructionContextExperiments.md)
execute a narrower first round: actual path edit scopes, explicit reformulation,
retained versus captured history and alternative observation views consumed by
new construction. Colour-based discovery and the 48 cue conditions below remain
proposed work.

## One source can support several useful reads

A grouping cue can propose a useful context without assigning the entire
source to one exclusive interpretation. A red family, a red/yellow compound
motif and a diagonal course may refer to overlapping actual faces. A later
question can inspect the original source again or consume an earlier read.
The next input need not be only the previous complement or residue.

This resembles successive extraction of useful structure, but it does not
inherit PCA's orthogonal linear components or variance objective. Compression
is one possible preference among recurrence, coverage, predictive support,
ambiguity, query cost and the operations a representation makes available.

Colour can make a grouping more conspicuous while leaving geometric distance,
topological contact and physical ownership unchanged. A bounded region supplies
closure evidence; discovering or completing an unseen boundary is a separate,
stronger capability.

Physical fill and conceptual observation have different contracts:

```text
physical partition: each actual atomic interior has one owner
observation cover: one actual source face can participate in several reads
```

An observational overlap is not duplicated material or independent evidence.
Different questions can legitimately produce different groups. Two candidates
asserting incompatible ownership of the same boundary under the same premises
must retain that conflict rather than being excused by different labels.

## Research grounding and its limits

Palmer's common-region demonstrations show that enclosure can influence
grouping beyond proximity and similarity and can support nested organization.
This motivates independent region, proximity and colour witnesses rather than
one unexplained grouping score. It does not establish arbitrary overlapping
concept formation. [Palmer, 1992](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/001002859290014S).

Kimchi and Razpurker-Apfeld distinguish grouping into rows/columns from more
demanding shape formation and figure/ground segregation. Their results support
testing several grouping processes with different attentional requirements;
they do not show that all interpretations are simultaneously conscious.
[Kimchi and Razpurker-Apfeld, 2004](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/BF03196621.pdf).

Larkin and Simon distinguish informational equivalence from computational
equivalence: a representation's available operators and search organization
affect inference cost. This supports testing the future operations enabled by
each construction, beyond matching its present drawing.
[Larkin and Simon, 1987](https://mechanism.ucsd.edu/bill/teaching/F12/cs200/Readings/larkin.whyadiagramissometimesworth.1987.pdf).

The broader suggestion that a portfolio of these reads approximates human
concepts is a research hypothesis. A structurally valid Gojo grouping is not
evidence that a viewer naturally notices it.

## What is already implemented

`OrnamentView` builds non-draining, potentially overlapping covers of actual
bounded scaffold faces. It preserves group identity, member faces, actual
strokes/sites, internal/boundary/cut seams and complement. Foreign or copied
lookalikes cannot substitute for actual membership.

`OrnamentViewOverlap` distinguishes shared faces, shared strokes and shared
sites. Overlap is many-to-many and does not become transitive equivalence.
`OrnamentViewComposition` allows several parent organizations to share actual
child reads. `OrnamentViewNetwork` can turn different selected views into
different exposed construction networks, while keeping contact and overlap
distinct and inventing no planar faces.

The focused perspective and view tests already exercise crossing organizations,
several levels and different constructions derived from the same source.
`OrnamentQuestionComparison` compares supplied questions through recurrence,
coverage, branching/conflicts and description participation, preserving ties
and incomparable alternatives. Hierarchical recognition fixtures exercise
bounded requests for missing local evidence through exposed surfaces.

These are tested structural mechanisms. They do not automatically discover
salient colour groups, infer all Gestalt organizations, learn cue weights, or
select a general concept.

The I001–I100 catalogue uses a different exact source contract:
`InteriorCellPartition` consumes an atomic complex, exclusive ownership and
formal embedding. Existing view readers consume scaffold face reads. The first
implementation step is a narrow exposed adapter or a justified shared reader
with actual face/edge/site correspondences. Reconstructing concepts from SVGs,
or making a second metadata-owned semantic tree, would evade the experiment.

Palette roles also need native exposed bindings. Rendered RGB alone should not
become the only source of a colour grouping's meaning.

## Small experiment sequence

Start with four contrasting fields: I011 (L rooms), I061 (hexagonal rooms),
I090 (stars and companions) and I094 (negotiated broad seams). Three palette
assignments and four questions give 48 read conditions before perturbations.

| Experiment | Hold fixed | Change or ask | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Several views of one field | Actual cells, seams and source identities | Same-role families; adjacent mixed-role motifs; continuing strands; larger disposition | Exact member correspondences and visible overlaps, without forcing one ownership tree |
| Cue competition | Geometry and physical fill | Neutral, motif-emphasizing and competing palette assignments; then change the selected question | Separate valid membership, salience preference and query success; colour changes do not fabricate topology |
| Read becomes construction context | Original field and retained alternatives | Use one recovered group as a unit for a further repeat or derived network | The result consumes the actual chosen read; sharing source ancestry alone is insufficient |
| Same square, different potential | A fully justified square and explicit correspondence | Turning path, parallel-family and radial constructions; ask for route, rectangle and pentagon edits | Compare retained/withdrawn premises, missing laws, local operations and query cost |
| Selective revalidation | Prior candidate identity and unaffected source | Change one colour role, seam or connection | Only dependent reads become stale, open or refuted; surviving alternatives still explain the field |

All read conditions retain source, question, cue evidence, selected
participation, complement, correspondence, unresolved claims and provenance.
Compatible alternatives stay available; the active query requests only the
additional detail it needs. A mismatch is initially a read remainder. A route
that turns it into material change or carrier tension must be explicit.

The first round should use supplied grouping proposals and simple local
generators. It should not depend on training a large model to conceal an
unclear observation contract. Learning can later rank proposal routes or cue
combinations by held-out query utility, with its evidence and costs exposed.

## The square needs equivalent promises

Four right-angle turns permit a rectangle unless side equality is also
established. Two pairs of parallel lines permit a parallelogram unless the
needed perpendicularity and span constraints are supplied. Four contour
markers do not establish a regular square without an actual contour and an
equal-partition promise.

Once those premises are justified, the present square may be shared while its
construction contexts differ. A path readily exposes ordered traversal; line
families expose span changes; an equally partitioned contour exposes polygon
arity. Changing the question can therefore select a representation with less
work, rather than identifying one representation as universally best.

## Measurements and counterexamples

Record held-out recurrence, distinct supported participation, invalid claims,
ambiguity retained, actual source relations inspected, missing facts requested,
and cost to answer the selected question. Keep description participation as
one criterion. Wall time is an optional execution measurement, not the sole
meaning of utility or conceptual complexity.

Negative cases should prevent: copied membership acquiring actual identity;
an overlap chain becoming equivalence; several rereads counting as independent
support; a colour cue altering physical ownership; unbound proximity becoming
contact; or identical drawings certifying equivalent construction promises.

The intended result is an inspectable network of useful overlapping contexts,
with enough information to choose which one to extend for a question. It is
not a claim that every legal interpretation must be computed or retained.
