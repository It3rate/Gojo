Two spaces, changing views.

Each space can change its own interpretation. It receives a published observation from its neighbour, not access to the neighbour’s private model. Step through what happens when those observations arrive.

The large numbers share A’s comparison unit. B’s native basis has two ticks for each A tick: a publication of 14 from B reads as 7 in A. The connecting calibration supplies that conversion.
