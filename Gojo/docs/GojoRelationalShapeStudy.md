# Relational shape descriptions, equivalence and revisable closure

Exploratory above-kernel study, 22 October 2026. This round asks whether a
receiver can construct increasingly varied shapes from local joints, pairwise
spatial relations and separate equivalence streams, while preserving the
difference between evidence, a hypothesis, a default and a requested result.
The proposal and critic are authored mechanisms; this is not model training.

Open the [comparison gallery](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/relational-shapes/gallery.html),

## What changed from the joint reconstruction round

The preceding [joint study](GojoJointCompositionReconstruction.md) did not
publish an ordered perimeter or an original edge list. Its horizontal and
vertical buckets were **orders on projected positions**, rather than a path
through the joints. Nevertheless, a complete pair of bucket sequences is a
substantial layout clue. The new receiver packet strips both sequences from
the joint message and offers spatial comparisons through a separate port.

Those comparisons say that one anonymous role is before, after or at the same
projection as another on a selected axis. They do not connect the roles
physically, specify their distance or tell the receiver which arms belong to
one stroke. A candidate layout and its traversal order are receiver hypotheses.
When a generated graph acquires an ordered boundary, that order belongs to the
generated hypothesis and its read, rather than being silently inherited from
the teacher's authoring history.

Length equivalence and angle equivalence are separate from global symmetry.
A pair of unrelated passages may share a length without participating in a
mirror. Two corners can share an angle while their arm lengths differ. An
entire mirror or rotation also needs a correspondence between participating
roles and passages. These are related clues with different scopes.

## Information flow and privacy boundary

```text
independent native source execution
  -> publication of actual sites, passages and native displacement words
  -> joint, equivalence, spatial and symmetry analysis
  -> fresh anonymous receiver packet
  -> native transport and exact local readers
  -> candidate ordinal layout and unmeasured gap sampling
  -> anonymous ray pairing and optional completion
  -> message-only critic with supported, contradicted and open claims
  -> bounded revision and selection

original teacher correspondence -> assessment after selection only
```

The source is created before its decomposition. Source family names, intended
completion targets, teacher edge partners, original position words and private
recipe history do not select receiver behavior. They remain in a separate
audit. Source labels and family names in the gallery are diagnostic controls.

Observed connectivity, coordinate coincidence and a projected crossing remain
different reads. In particular, discovering two crossing chords does not make
their crossing an actual shared site. Component evidence says which joint
roles are mechanically connected; it does not supply their perimeter order.

## Equivalence without a measured value

The native supplement has separate lanes for exact length groups, exact angle
groups, coarse angle hypotheses and unsupported comparisons:

```text
(joint roles -> advertised ray slots)
  -> ((extent groups -> turn groups)
    -> (coarse turn hypotheses -> (open extents -> open turns)))

group = common unmeasured promise -> Closed(member -> promise)
extent member = anonymous ray slot
turn member = pair of local anonymous ray slots
```

The common promise binds a comparison, not physical stroke identity. It does
not imply that grouped rays meet, belong to the same object or share a source
quantity. A receiver can satisfy equal-length arms at many absolute sizes.
No measured extent or source word is carried through the supplement.

There is one selected endpoint occurrence per source passage in the length
groups. Publishing both endpoint occurrences as a group would disclose an
edge partner for free and mistake the same observation arriving twice for
independent repetition. Several independent source passages are required for
a published repeated-length group. Such a group can still legitimately narrow
the possible pairings; that constraint is the intended information.

The present exact length reader distributes native repeated reaches into
same-axis squared products and cancels matched participation. Under the
selected shared-reach and equal-turn geometric basis, the two axis products
share one squared-extent interpretation. Without that basis, different-axis
length equality remains open. Different unresolved reach identities do not
acquire equal scale from matching display labels.

These calculations count actual repeated native participation. They do not
read source coordinates as floating-point measurements or fit a proposed
polygon to an image. A finite implementation can compile cancellation counts
or membership indexes, but the participating products and comparison witnesses
remain connected native structure. This is pre-numeric in its communicated
contract and uncalibrated interpretation; it does not claim that counting or
computation never occurs inside an exact reader.

Exact angle comparisons use the selected native quarter-turn/reflection
substitutions and magnitude-independent proportional direction reads. They
support a conservative family of justified correspondences. They do not
provide arbitrary-angle rotation or a general geometric equality solver.
Coarse directional similarities occupy a separate revisable hypothesis lane;
two arms lying in the same directional quadrant do not thereby have exactly
equal angles. Unsupported finer comparisons remain open.

There is no general near-length tolerance in this round. An all-arms-similar
expectation can be a weak default, but it is different from observing that two
lengths fall within some specified variation. That latter read needs a declared
coarse comparison or tolerance basis rather than an arbitrary hidden threshold.

## Evidence contexts and qualitative confidence

`StructuralEvidenceStreams` connects four different participation contexts:

| Context | Meaning | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation | An offered current read with its cause and dependencies | The truth of an intended completion |
| Hypothesis | A proposed explanation or sampled arrangement | Observed source structure |
| Default | A defeasible choice when evidence leaves room | A requirement or current fact |
| Goal | A requested result to try to construct | Evidence that the result already exists |

Each stream has separately connected support and opposition rungs. The local
ladder is an actual native chain: no opinion, weak, medium, strong. There are
no distances between the rungs, no percentage probabilities and no implied
statistical calibration. Strong support and weak doubt can coexist; the doubt
is retained rather than subtracted away into a single confidence score.

Confidence, kind of evidence and geometric direction are separate concerns.
The direction of a line does not say how reliable an observation is. Likewise,
opposition to a claim is not the reverse traversal of its physical passage.
The separate support and opposition faces preserve that distinction without
inventing an arithmetic certainty value.

Repeated routes do not automatically accumulate confidence. The local read
retains causes and dependencies and compares the strongest offered rung on
each face. This is a conservative authored policy, not a general learning law
or a claim that all supplied observations are statistically independent.

### Closure is a default, not an obligation imposed on the observation

An observed endpoint strongly opposes the claim that the current component is
closed. It can defeat a weak closed-area default. The system can then retain
an open object, or consider a closed parent with missing participation as a
different hypothesis. It does not need to erase the endpoint to preserve the
idea of a familiar parent shape.

Requesting completion changes the goal context. A new bridge may satisfy that
goal while remaining an assumed passage in its own scope. It does not become
evidence that the source already contained that bridge. Open ends alone do not
decide whether an object was damaged, intentionally open or incompletely
observed. Those alternatives require further context or evidence.

This connects to reason maintenance: Jon Doyle's
[A Truth Maintenance System (1979)](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/5377b306-4ecc-4687-b1f5-78cbb4a0543a)
records reasons for assumptions and supports their revision when new findings
conflict. This study retains similar explanatory dependencies, but does not
implement Doyle's dependency-directed backtracking or claim formal equivalence
to a truth maintenance system. Its qualitative support ladders and bounded
shape search remain a distinct exploratory mechanism.

## Corpus and search protocol

The authored corpus contains 100 sources in 20 families, five independently
executed examples per family. It includes familiar enclosures, concave and
notched outlines, steps, pointed shapes, multiple disconnected components,
symmetry-related groups, damaged objects and open physical junctions. Greater
variety tests composition; no family identifier acts as a decoder switch.

Each source is compared under four packet contexts:

1. Observed relations with equivalence evidence.
2. A requested completion goal.
3. A sparser set of spatial comparisons.
4. A control withholding equivalence evidence.

The full planned budget is 64 native attempts per source and context: 25,600
bounded attempts. A smaller diagnostic run can use fewer cases or attempts.
Candidate positions are native words built from selected unmeasured gaps.
They sample a possible realization of the inferred ordinal constraints; they
are not fitted source measurements. A consistent topological realization may
look different while satisfying the same packet.

The critic assesses claims from the packet and candidate only. Checks include
actual connection choices, local ray directions, opposed and perpendicular
pairs, relative positions, component participation, selected symmetry,
equivalence, observed endpoints, assumed bridges and supported crossing or
overlap reads. A capability-limited read must remain open. A bounded search
that fails to find a complete realization has not proved no realization exists.

Teacher-only connectivity or completion comparisons occur after candidate
selection. They measure recovery, not what the receiver was allowed to know.
Agreement with a teacher is not a substitute for inspecting other satisfying
solutions, and a different-looking realization is not automatically wrong.

The run retains aggregate assessment across the search and a small selection
of native receipts per case. This is deliberate bounded history: it exposes
the search policy, recorded counters and retained examples, without claiming
an exact replay archive of every discarded candidate and its complete causal
graph.

## Results capture

### Baseline: 100 sources, 64 proposals per profile

The initial full run requested **25,600 proposals**, assessed **22,201 native
candidates**, and blocked **3,399 inconsistent ordinal hypotheses**. Source
analysis and search took **471.17 seconds before archive and gallery output**
on the local machine. The numbers below describe that baseline; a subsequent
extent-guided repair experiment should be reported separately rather than
silently replacing it.

| Profile | Assessed / requested | Exact source edge inventory | No hard conflict or unpaired ray | Added assumed passages | Blocked layouts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Evidence first | 6,400 / 6,400 | 99 / 100 | 89 / 100 | 0 | 0 |
| Requested completion | 6,400 / 6,400 | 88 / 100 | 89 / 100 | 11 | 0 |
| Sparse relative locations | 3,001 / 6,400 | 58 / 100 | 45 / 100 | 34 | 3,399 |
| Equivalence withheld | 6,400 / 6,400 | 99 / 100 | 97 / 100 | 0 | 0 |

An exact edge inventory means matching anonymous endpoint partners in the
teacher audit; it does not promise the same proportions or satisfaction of
every geometric claim. Eleven evidence-first selections recover the exact
connections but still have hard conflicts. Conversely, removing equivalence
removes checks: the control's higher conflict-free count is **not** evidence
that omitting those constraints improves reconstruction. It makes some errors
invisible to that critic. All 100 evidence-first selections retain open checks
(1,064 selected open witnesses in total), so zero hard conflicts is not an
assertion that every question has been settled.

The full spatial stream still provides enough equality groups and directed
comparisons to recover its complete ordinal layout. Removing the bucket list
separates this evidence from path order and construction, but does not by
itself demonstrate less communicated information. The sparse profile is the
stronger test of inventing missing arrangement. It retains projection
equalities and only every third published before relation, while keeping the
other joint, symmetry and equivalence streams. It recovers all five triangles
and all five quadrilaterals, but none of the five notched outlines or five
stepped enclosures. Its 582 unpaired rays across selected outputs, explicit
order assumptions and blocked hypotheses expose a substantial composition
gap. More proposals alone did not supply a generally reliable missing-order
policy.

### What the separate streams expose

The exact length supplement supplies groups in 95 sources, exact angle groups
in 87, and additional weak coarse-angle hypotheses in 17. Withholding
equivalence changes the selected native geometry in four cases: R054 (four
notched lobes), R086 (an outward tab), R097 (earlier L00064), and R100 (two
closures linked by a bridge). All four changes are visible in the selected
SVGs. This establishes that the extra relations participate in selection,
without implying that the other 96 cases did not need their additional checks.

Useful inspection cases are:

- **R022, side notch:** all eight source connections are retained, but one
  equal-length check fails. The ordinal sampling can produce the correct
  notch topology while failing the relation between longer boundary spans.
  This is a compact target for a local extent-guided repair mechanism.
- **R043, three translated diamonds:** all twelve connections are recovered,
  yet 82 hard checks remain contradicted. The reported exact-length and
  exact-angle conflict counts are both zero; those 82 failures therefore
  belong to the other core joint, symmetry, component or interior checks.
  Correct incidence alone does not establish a coherent realization across
  several translated siblings.
- **R094, diagonal returned ribbon:** the receiver selects ten of twelve
  teacher passages plus two different passages, with zero hard conflicts and
  41 open checks. This is an underdetermined alternative under the current
  published checks, not proof of either a wrong concept or an equivalent one.
  A focused question about the differing returns would be more informative
  than another global score.
- **R081 and R096 (L00016):** evidence-first preserves the known opening;
  requested completion adds one separately recorded bridge. The original
  one-ray observations remain available and do not become closed observations.
- **R074, crossing enclosure outlines:** an exact source reconstruction still
  receives overlap-related default penalties. Those penalties are revisable
  preferences, not universal rules excluding intentionally overlapping or
  unjoined constructions. Four evidence-first cases have such crossing or
  overlap penalties.

Exact one-ray endpoint evidence defeats the weak closure default in **22
sources**. An explicit completion goal adds one bridge in eleven sources,
including intentionally open paths as well as damaged templates. Eight of
those eleven satisfy the current closure critic; three retain an unsupported
closure goal despite the added passage. Across the whole completion profile,
19 goals remain blocked, including five sources without observed endpoints.
The current positive-boundary reader can therefore be too limited to justify
closure even when a teacher knows a closed contour is present. Endpoint
evidence also does not decide whether the relevant context is a damaged
boundary, an intentional open path, or a closed area with an attached open
branch. That scope still needs a question or further evidence.

The completion profile's lower exact-source count is expected: a newly
proposed bridge is additional participation in the teacher audit, even when
it is the requested action. The sparse profile's 34 assumed passages are also
visible hypotheses, not newly discovered source edges.

These observations support separating topology, geometric equivalence,
context and completion goals. They also identify concrete weaknesses in
joint-to-joint composition and constraint-guided sampling. They do not show
training improvement: the policy remains authored, the source corpus is
synthetic native structure, and no raster recognition, arbitrary-angle
geometry, near-length tolerance or learned model is being evaluated. Original
measured positions, private recipe history and teacher edge partners remain
withheld; the retained joint, component, symmetry and interior evidence is
nevertheless substantial and should not be mistaken for unconstrained
recognition from a bare picture.

### Follow-up: let an equivalence group change its realization

The [extent-feedback comparison](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/relational-shapes/extent-repair/gallery.html)
repeats 15 selected diagnostic cases at the same 64-proposal budget. It requests
3,840 proposals, assesses 3,204 and blocks 636 inconsistent layouts, taking
206.31 seconds before archives. These cases were selected after examining the
baseline; they are not an independent validation set or a training result.

The optional tactic follows an advertised equal-length group into the retained
candidate's actual passages and then into the proposed ordinal gap intervals.
It verifies those intervals against the current native words before trying up
to 16 local changes. The participating gaps use the candidate's actual shared
reach; there is no teacher dimension to restore. The receipt retains clauses,
interval expansions, before/after words, accepted changes and unresolved groups.
Changing a gap count changes a selected realization of an unmeasured promise;
it does not put a length value into the communicated equivalence group.

Within this selected cohort, cases without a hard conflict or missing ray rise
from **4/15 to 10/15**. R022, R030, R087, R091, R099 and R100 lose their remaining
hard conflicts while preserving their observed endpoint partners. These six
revisions are selected at proposal four. The added stream can therefore guide
construction, rather than merely reject a final image. Fourteen of the fifteen
still recover the teacher's exact connections; R094 retains its alternate read.

R028, R041, R043, R044 and R050 remain unresolved. The tactic deliberately leaves
a length group open if any member cannot be represented by a justified
axis-aligned interval. It does not weaken the group by silently dropping the
diagonal members. In particular, R043's translated diamonds require action
correspondences to coordinate their relative displacements, not just a repair
of axis-aligned lengths. The sparse profile is unchanged because changing
ordinal layouts would invalidate this tactic's retained gap correspondence.

This repair is a bounded authored local search, not a general constraint solver.
Its count-based disagreement and sampling cost are search priorities, not
confidence measures. The extra work has a cost: this diagnostic is evidence of
better constraint-guided proposals, not a speed improvement benchmark.

## What this makes ready for a larger experiment

The receiver now has independently transportable joint, spatial, equivalence,
symmetry and context streams. Source construction order is not an input path.
Observed endpoints can defeat a weak closure default without erasing a damaged
parent hypothesis, and a requested repair remains separate from observation.
The four-rung support/doubt mechanism is reusable, but its operational use in
this study is concentrated on closure selection. Most exact geometric claims
are still offered as strong observations and assessed as hard checks; this is
not yet a general confidence-weighted geometric reasoner.

The next useful targets are direct action-correspondence feedback between
sibling motifs, a better policy for hypothesizing missing joint connections,
and coarse comparison streams that can express similar lengths without claiming
equality. Those should be judged on unseen combinations and on whether a
query can stop with a valid coarse answer. Increasing the training corpus
alone will not fix the current composition and capability gaps.

The browser audit checks all local references, mobile layout, selection states
and visible dashed assumptions. It does not establish conceptual correctness
from visual appearance; native claims and their unresolved states govern that
assessment.

The SVG gallery remains a presentation of native samples. The receiver does
not parse those pixels or consult a renderer to discover source relationships.
There is no actual trained model, probability calibration, automatic
cross-domain concept learner or general recursive truth-maintenance solver in
this round. Read residuals and conflicting claims also do not automatically
become material carrier tension.
