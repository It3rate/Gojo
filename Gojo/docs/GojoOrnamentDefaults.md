# Ornament defaults: requirements, preferences and deliberate omissions

The [interactive study](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-defaults/gallery.html)
holds evidence fixed while changing the authority of the question. The
[construction overview](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-defaults/overview.html)
shows all 72 alternatives. These are candidates and counterexamples, not 72
endorsed designs. The study does not select one universal best realization.

## The distinction being tested

| Selected role | Known agreement | Known conflict | Unavailable or conflicting evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Required claim | Satisfies that obligation | Excludes the candidate | Keeps the obligation Open; an invalid read is Blocked |
| Strong default | No default departure | Lowers ordinal preference | Retains a range of possible departures |
| Weak preference | No preference departure | Matters after the strong tier | Does not justify a false precision in ordering |
| Unused | Carried outside this question | Carried outside this question | Carried outside this question |
| Explicitly waived default | Waiver retained | Conflict and exact waiver retained | Waiver does not invent an answer |

A default is not a required claim with a small weight. Its violation does not
make the concept false. Nor is an unused distinction a weak expectation of
absence: its truth is deliberately irrelevant to the selected question.
Exceptions identify the actual rule on the actual policy surface. They cannot
waive a requirement, another lookalike rule, or a rule in a different context.

Within each tier, the current reader counts witnessed departures. Strong
departures precede weak ones. Unresolved cues contribute intervals rather than
invented probabilities; only disjoint intervals establish preference, and a
later tier is consulted only after an exact tie. This is an authored policy,
not a claim that human perception uses these thresholds or tier ordering.

## Corpus, questions and native encoding

Six families have twelve alternatives each: closed compartments, hub-and-lobe
scaffolds, rails and bridges, two-pole route structures, returning meanders,
and pointed closed contours. They extend the vocabulary of the existing
Bourgoin, rosette and Greek-key experiments. Names group the catalogue; they
do not classify a drawing or select a verdict. Ordinary defaults impose no
hidden family-membership or topology requirement.

Twelve available predicates combine three exposed services:

1. Whole branch-and-route and every-site incidence correspondence to a
   reference, using the existing native arrangement reader.
2. A bijection between fresh expected roles and distinct published mechanical
   group rows, either all rows or only rows declaring an end-to-start closure.
   No stored scalar count supplies this truth. Closed groups include a rim as
   well as its petals; this is a construction inventory, not a universal petal
   detector or a count of visually enclosed regions.
3. An optional finer word-basis supplier for explained stroke encounters,
   positive collinear overlap, closed-contour breadth, overall aspect, an
   explicit scene boundary, occupied-interior disjointness, mirror occupancy
   and quarter-turn occupancy.

The geometry supplier explicitly interprets repeated native position words
under one common axis reach. It is not extracted from a history-blind graph
and does not read SVG pixels. The selected standards are four repetitions for
ordinary aspect, eight for breadth resolution, and a scene square from -8 to
+8 on both axes. They are inspectable native participation, with numerical
labels in the report. The supplier derives integer word coefficients and uses
arithmetic comparisons under this declared basis; these are not additional
measured inputs. This is an optional refinement with more commitments
than topology alone; it must not be described as deriving proportion from
order without a basis. Different standards may reasonably change the result.

Policy authority and expected polarity are also connected structure:

```text
rule        = predicate -> (Closed(positive predicate) -> Closed(negative predicate))
policy      = Closed(required) -> (Closed(strong) -> (Closed(weak) -> Closed(unused)))
evidence    = predicate -> (supported -> (refuted -> (open -> blocked)))
observation = source port -> Closed(evidence)
exception   = actual policy -> Closed(actual waived rules)
```

The policy decision reads these categorical receipts. Its witness retains the
finer publication, producer evidence and correspondence; it does not erase
them or authenticate arbitrary supplied assertions. The runner derives every
fact from the actual same-candidate producer receipt. Labels and case names
do not provide evidence. Supported and refuted evidence for the same predicate
are both retained and make a required read Open, rather than choosing whichever
reply arrived first.

## What the run shows

The run executes **3,120 native policy reads**, independently regenerates all
72 native recipes and checks their **864 predicate replies** again, then
searches **6,144 finite descriptor subsets**. Each family has eleven read
contexts plus single-rule authority changes and exceptions from the ordinary
context. A snapshot of the resulting distinctions follows:

| Family | Whole reference arrangement admitted | Every-site topology admitted | Same closed-group inventory admitted | Exact single conjunction of the other tested reference cues? |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Compartment | 8 | 7 | 8 | No |
| Hub and lobes | 6 | 5 | 9 | No |
| Rails and bridges | 6 | 6 | 12 | Yes, within these twelve alternatives |
| Two poles and routes | 6 | 4 | 12 | No |
| Returning course | 9 | 4 | 11 | No |
| Pointed contour | 10 | 8 | 9 | No |

Several cases make the implications concrete:

- **C10, the thin compartment:** whole branch arrangement and every-site
  topology still agree with the square. Breadth, aspect and the chosen scene
  boundary conflict. Ordinary defaults demote it; a strict boundary excludes
  it; deliberately requesting an elongated unbounded form prefers it. The
  encoding can distinguish unusual from impossible.
- **C06 and C07:** four separately constructed sides can form the same joined
  enclosure as a single turning path. Coincident but unjoined endpoints do
  not. Mechanical grouping, geometric contact and topological closure are
  independent evidence.
- **P02, P03 and P07:** two, six and four triangular petals show why count,
  whole incidence and detailed site inventory are useful optional identifiers.
  The four triangular petals preserve the reference branch arrangement while
  changing its every-site topology. Count alone also admits wrong junctions.
- **S05 and S06:** six- and eight-point closed stars share the coarse cycle
  concept with the four-point reference. Every-site topology distinguishes
  them. Quarter-turn occupancy agrees with four and eight points, but not
  six. Neither cue alone is the meaning of a four-point star.
- **L09:** an explicitly declared brace crossing can explain a proper stroke
  crossing without supplying a join or an over/under order. Allowing crossings
  is separate from allowing coincident positive-length retracing.
- **C08:** intersecting compartment outlines fail the unexplained-encounter
  default. Occupied-interior overlap remains Open because no filled ownership
  is offered. Making that unavailable service required leaves all 72 drawings
  Open; the study does not manufacture a tessellation verdict.

The selected boundary can demote an otherwise ordinary reference, including
the three-bay ladder. This is evidence that a scene convention changes
preference, not that the reference is a poor ornament. The gallery can switch
between fitted drawings and the explicit envelope so the cause is visible.

## Minimal steering and counterexamples

The descriptor search targets each reference's whole-branch-compatible set.
It cannot use that whole-arrangement predicate as its own descriptor. It tries
the other ten available cues, excluding unavailable filled ownership, with
polarities derived from the reference's actual replies. All subsets are
checked; selected descriptions are then executed again as actual native
required policies. The arithmetic search must agree with those receipts.

The report keeps a frontier over three quantities: extra admitted alternatives,
valid alternatives lost, and number of clauses. It does not combine them into
one score. Equivalent error-count and clause-count choices are collapsed for
display; native participant cost is reported separately and is not minimized.
That cost includes the connected predicate standards and is not a bit estimate.

Five families have no exact separating conjunction of the tested reference
polarities. This is a limit of that restricted rule language, not a claim that
no rule over the available cues could separate the examples. Several alternative
conjunctions or a conditional rule could admit different valid profiles. The
complete branch contract already expresses the target without enumerating them.
Both the extra examples and the valid examples lost are useful counterexamples
for deciding the next question.

There is also a stronger, concrete information gap. **T04 and T08 have identical
answers to all ten secondary descriptors**, yet T04 preserves three complete
routes between the poles while T08's middle route stops short. Even an arbitrary
Boolean combination of those ten cue answers cannot separate this pair. The
connected arrangement question does separate them. The other five families
have no such opposed full-profile collision in this finite corpus; their failed
single-conjunction descriptions alone do not establish a missing-evidence gap.

## Limits and next experiments

No probabilities or default strengths were learned. Aspect and resolution
thresholds, tier ordering and contexts were supplied. Each selected cue has a
thresholded reply; the current preference does not estimate how improbable or
how far beyond a threshold a realization is. Breadth applies to
declared closed mechanical contours, not every visible face, nearby pair of
open lines or narrow ladder chamber. Symmetry compares complete segment
occupancy at the published word resolution; it does not establish topology,
mechanical provenance or symmetry under an arbitrary affine interpretation.

Scene containment does not check repeated-neighbour seams. Filled ownership,
bounded-face extraction and periodic seam compatibility need separate exposed
contracts before claiming non-overlapping tessellation. Those are more useful
next suppliers than silently strengthening the current overlap default.

A follow-up can hold out new constructions, vary the engine's capabilities,
and learn which optional cue best removes a particular ambiguity while retaining
the desired alternatives. The target should remain preservation of explicit
claims across realizations, with unknown service, counterexample and exception
provenance intact. The current run prepares all facts eagerly; it makes no
claim of compute saved by selectively asking fewer questions.

## Reproduce and inspect

```powershell
dotnet run --project experiments/QualitativeFrieze -c Release -- --ornament-defaults
node experiments/QualitativeFrieze/tools/check-ornament-defaults.cjs
dotnet test Gojo.slnx
```

The source is in `OrnamentDefaultsExperiment.cs`, `OrnamentDefaultsPolicy.cs`,
`OrnamentDefaultGeometry.cs`, `OrnamentParticipationCount.cs`,
`OrnamentDefaultCases.cs` and their tests. Results retain native recipes,
source-free questions, publications, concrete producer and policy witnesses in
`defaults.gojo.json.gz`, alongside the diagnostic table and optional SVGs.
The archive is an execution audit with many repeated trials, not a proposed
compact concept message or training checkpoint. No kernel primitives changed.

Forty focused native tests cover the new mechanisms, counterexamples and
transport boundaries. Browser/data checks verify all 288 SVGs, 471 selected
browser states, 365 local references, all finite subset descriptions and the
reported policy decisions. Desktop and narrow-screen views were inspected;
the evidence colours do not imply that Yes means accepted or No means rejected.
Full solution verification passed 4,722 tests across twelve projects, with no
failures or skips. The runner also builds with no warnings or errors.

The browser checker uses the local Playwright/Edge setup used by the earlier
studies. `GOJO_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` and `GOJO_BROWSER` select other installations;
`--data-only` checks the saved tables, finite searches and SVGs without a browser.
