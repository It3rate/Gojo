# Structural potential and query-directed completion

Experimental layer above the kernel, 21 October 2026. This study connects two
operations over the same native construction obligations: deriving a candidate
from partial evidence, and completing that candidate under explicit assumptions.
It does not add geometry or probabilistic primitives to the kernel.

[Interactive cases](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/structural-potential/gallery.html)
and [overview](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/structural-potential/overview.html).

## Question

Can a reader establish useful structural possibilities without choosing
coordinates, and then fill only the missing obligations needed by a narrower
question? Can a generated completion remain separate from evidence about its
source, including when a later source reply refutes the earlier assumption?

The chosen construction is a pair of finite supports in each of two families.
Each support has its own endpoints. The source initially has four independent
passages and eight endpoint sites, with an open crossing-observation surface.
It does not already contain a cell or its corner sites as material joins.

## Native shape

```text
family = direction -> (transverse span -> Closed(actual support passages))
encounter = (actual A support -> actual B support) -> open corner role
coverage = actual support -> Closed(start, encounter, encounter, end)
boundary = Closed((corner -> next corner) -> actual support)

candidate = actual support source -> (encounters -> boundary)
rule = Closed(actual premise predicates) -> actual candidate predicate
program = Closed(rules)
publication = actual source port -> Closed(predicate evidence)

evidence = predicate -> (Supported -> (Refuted -> (Open -> Blocked)))
completion = source read -> (allowed assumptions -> (introduced evidence ->
             (conditional read -> derived boundary observation)))
```

Predicate diagrams contain the actual families, passage identities, endpoint
order, quarter-turn correspondence, or shared-reach binding being requested.
The rules connect these conditions to their selected construction. Names,
example IDs and UI status words do not choose the result. The native program
and publication readers operate without the authoring helper or its labels.

The premise contracts have specific scope:

- Straightness puts each whole supporting passage on its family direction.
- Separation asserts strictly ordered transverse positions. Distinct support
  identities or ordered membership alone do not prove noncoincidence.
- Independence includes a common planar placement, not merely differently
  named directions. Different coarse heading labels alone are insufficient.
- Perpendicularity is an explicit geometric quarter-turn promise. It is not
  inferred from a kernel orthogonal outlet or the renderer's axes.
- Equal spacing binds the separation reach of both families to one uncalibrated
  reach. Uniform repetition within each family leaves those two reaches
  independent until a correspondence is supplied.
- Finite coverage asserts both relevant encounters lie inside each support,
  with their order retained. A possible supporting-line intersection is not
  proof that a finite stroke reaches it.

These are authored exposed premises in this experiment. No claim is made that
the fixture independently inferred them from its coarse endpoint observations.

## Six reads

| Selected candidate | Necessary obligations |
| --- | --- |
| Supporting-line boundary potential | Straightness, both transverse separations, common-plane independence |
| Supporting-line rectangle potential | Boundary potential and perpendicularity |
| Supporting-line square potential | Rectangle potential and equal separation reach between families |
| Finite four-sided boundary | Boundary potential and all four support-coverage clauses |
| Finite rectangle | Finite boundary and perpendicularity |
| Finite square | Finite rectangle and equal separation reach |

The rules define these particular conjunctive construction contracts. They are
not material implication: a failed premise refutes this selected construction,
not every possible square or rectangle elsewhere in an ornament. A supporting
line potential can remain supported while the finite boundary is refuted.

The reader follows only the selected goal's dependencies. Explicit premise
order checks angle and equal-reach refuters before expanding the finer boundary
obligations. It stops on a decisive refusal, conflict or blocked read, retaining
unasked premises. An unknown premise does not stop it from finding a decisive
refutation elsewhere in the conjunction. Repeated dependencies share a read
within that query.

Missing source premises, explicit open accounts, opposing evidence, illegal
contracts and exhausted query slots remain distinct native evidence. A closed
publication inventory is not a closed world of false unstated facts. There is
no scalar confidence or learned probability in this reader.

`Receipt.ToEvidence()` publishes the result through the existing evidence
surface, retaining the entire producing receipt. A conflict inherited from a
child remains an open root with a conflict trace; it does not manufacture two
complete opposed proofs of the root construction. Import into another program
is explicit and retains its source context.

## Conditional completion

The receiver is given a closed set of actual premise identities that it may
assume. For a finite-boundary question it adds only permitted missing leaves
reachable from that question, then rereads the same construction. Refuted,
conflicting or blocked source evidence cannot be waived. A computational cut
is not a missing geometric premise and cannot be filled by an assumption.

A successful conditional read emits a fresh observational boundary with four
sites and four edges. Each edge corresponds to its original supporting passage;
each corner corresponds to the pair of source passages that would meet there.
The original mechanical paths and endpoints remain unchanged. The existing
motif induction reader independently discovers the emitted four-edge circuit.

A rectangle question adds no equal-reach binding. Its independent open reach
parameters are not an inequality assertion. A square question requests a shared
reach and carries the required quarter-turn promise. Neither sample has measured
coordinates. Diagram sizes and angles are illustrative presentation choices.

The sample is a partial relational model sufficient for its query, not a fully
rendered world or new evidence that the original source has those properties.
Its angle and reach promises are carried construction constraints; only its
boundary topology is independently inspected by the motif reader here. No
empty-face, filled ownership, Euclidean numeric conformance or material
connectivity is inferred from that circuit.

Completion currently fills absent premises. A separately published explicit
Open assessment remains active even alongside an assumption, and can prevent
completion. Narrowing or superseding that assessed account would require an
explicit further context/refinement contract; this experiment does not erase it.

## Inspectable cases and checks

The twelve cases include uncalibrated separated families, an additional angle
promise, a common reach, known unequal reaches, one clipped support, conflicting
angle accounts, coincident family positions, independent within-family uniform
repetitions, missing planar independence, delegated assumptions, a restricted
assumption scope, and repetition without a second independent direction.

The gallery offers all six questions and three query capacities. It shows
available authored premises separately from the predicates actually consulted,
and separates source answers from conditional completion. Status uses words
rather than red/green encodings of candidate acceptance.

The runner compares every source read after independently transporting laws,
publication and question aliases together. It also checks actual emitted circuit
witnesses, source nonmutation, and stores connected evidence in
`evidence.gojo.json.gz`. `summary.json` records the ordinary-capacity audit;
the gallery additionally includes limited and zero-capacity reads.

Focused tests cover omitted versus false evidence, missing geometric promises,
query-specific assumptions, unsupported completion, capacity cuts, actual source
identity, downstream evidence, raw native transport and contradictory accounts.
A revision test retains an earlier conditional square, then supplies actual
unequal-gap evidence: the new square completion is refused while the earlier
sample and its qualifications remain intact.

Recorded ordinary-capacity results:

| Read | Supported | Open | Refuted | Conflicting |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 72 source construction reads | 22 | 29 | 17 | 4 |
| 36 finite completion attempts | 14 | 10 | 10 | 2 |

All 14 emitted boundaries have independently discovered four-edge circuit
witnesses. All 72 source reads preserve their answers, missing-premise counts
and visited-predicate counts after native transport. Browser checks cover 216
prepared query states, 226 browser interactions, 26 SVGs and 67 local references.
The 34 focused tests and the full 4,790-test repository solution pass. These are
mechanism checks on authored cases, not recognition accuracy or training results.

## Interpretation and remaining work

This is a shared mechanism for assessing necessary construction conditions and
proposing their conditional completion. It does not yet learn the conditions,
invent templates, select candidate support pairs from a large ornament, compute
all crossing orders, extract elementary faces, or rank competing constructions.
There is no global solver or automatic conversion of uncertainty into certainty.

The next useful connection is a supplier that derives these premises from real
native line families and partial encounter/order observations, with witnesses.
That would let the same receiver operate on information extracted from a larger
ornament, instead of the directly authored premise ports in this study. Broader
sampling can then compare several candidate constructions while preserving the
same source evidence and query boundaries.

The follow-up [native lattice evidence study](GojoNativeLatticeEvidence.md)
implements that supplier for two directional families, including executed
Bourgoin graphs, bounded candidate discovery and public-port transport. Its
conditional geometric model and unsupported cases remain explicit.
