## Decision

Start with finite local observations and open roles. Try small executable interpretations of what has settled. When one predicts a useful read, retain the equation, the basis that makes it meaningful and the evidence for its scope. When the same read is used often enough, derive a native execution plan for that scope. Keep any other live roles needed for intervention, inverse inference, accounting, or later construction.

A finite law can represent an arbitrary relation on its declared domain. Settlement can then propagate locally through the relations and openings provided. A finite history may support many functions, and an arbitrary inverse can demand a new search or fail to settle. The proposal gives those cases somewhere to remain open.

I think this is plausible. Regularities such as a constant difference, a reusable step, an operand symmetry, or a separable product can expose a much smaller interpretation than a sample table. The same locality that helps Gojo settle also gives a search a starting point. A receiver can see which source roles were sampled, what it can perturb, and what a read omits. Those are useful inputs to discovery even when magnitudes are unavailable. For an arbitrary mapping, a small equation may exist only in certain coordinates, for a subset of the domain, or under one schedule. A mapping with no useful regularity can remain a local table or a set of witnesses.

**Use small exact numbers now. Keep pre-numeric structure active alongside them.** Order, occupancy, shared sites, turn and direction, port access and candidate alternatives already carry information. Exact bounded counts give us a way to distinguish competing interpretations that coincide at the bit grade, test predictions and compare cost. Introduce a new rung when a receiver has a concrete question the current grade cannot answer.

## The three questions to settle

**What agrees with the evidence?** An observation gives the result of a selected read at an identified time and basis. A forecast commits a particular candidate and expected read before the next observation arrives. Agreement narrows local alternatives. An unobserved point can remain open. Several distinct explanations can agree on all currently observable inputs.

**What would meet a goal?** A goal constrains an open choice, path, or output. Settlement can produce candidate input bindings, and a receiver can ask whether a particular candidate would succeed. Product 12 over factors `0..4` admits ordered pairs `(3,4)` and `(4,3)`. Asking for that result does not provide evidence either pair is present. A separate authorized action would bind a pair, book a request, or transfer material. A mismatch from an issued prediction remains a claim residual until a connected response route uses it. [Prospective calibration](GojoProspectiveCalibrationFixture.md) · [Prediction and fit witnesses](../Equations/README.md#difference-and-fit)

**What can be substituted?** An equation can become an exact substitute for a selected, bounded read when the covered contract is checked. It can be an approximate alternative where the receiver requests a finite error envelope and accepts the remaining loss. A material transition needs its own agreement on what is changed, what is conserved and what remains owed. A schedule needs its own agreement on activity, quiet, and the first tick a result can be observed. All three can be needed in one world.

For example, the expression `a*b` can describe an exact forward number both from a direct relation and from a lattice. With an inverse question about product 5 over `0..3`, the direct relation rejects the query and the lattice can leave open marginals. Accumulated delivery of 9 units against a 12-unit request needs available stock and space, a bookable order, and retained evidence of the missing packet. Equal formulas on settled operands say nothing by themselves about either of those reads. [Factoring contract](../../Gojo.EncodingC/Tests/OperationFactoringTests.cs) · [Delivery and booking](../../Gojo.EncodingC/Tests/OperationRequestTests.cs)

## What a search can use

The fit should see a selected local window, not a flattened snapshot of every carrier. A publication has enough structure to identify its source instance and exposed port, actual ordered roles, sample or event identity, the selected calibration and resolution, and whether a result is exact, closed possible support, or open. A timed read connects source time, local availability, the transition or step order, and requested result. A goal, prediction, and later observation occupy different relations. Keep selection omissions and unresolved roles visible.

```text
observation   = exposed source and ordered samples -> selected read + witness
candidate     = open pattern -> bound operation surface -> proposed result
assessment    = issued proposal -> independent observation -> mismatch or open evidence
refinement    = assessment or distinguishing question -> exposed request -> new receipt
application   = accepted use -> permitted binding or change -> result + residue
```

This notation describes relations and ports. The first experiment can use a small host to enumerate candidates and choose probes. Its candidates, bindings, witnesses, and unresolved cases must be inspectable even without the search procedure. A column view, expression tree, score or GPU buffer can be derived for this purpose. The associations between columns and sites, units, shared sources, occurrence order and open tails must remain recoverable. Save the working state in planes or retain selected native observations as appropriate for the experiment. A packed bit position needs an explicit correspondence before it stands for a native carrier, an ordered site, or a source of shared uncertainty. [Native and packed interoperability](../../Gojo.Encoding/Core/INTEROPERABILITY.md)

| Hint from the source | Candidate to try | Evidence that would distinguish it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equally spaced samples have a constant difference | Affine read in those frames | Change the datum, use held-out spacing, and cross an actual capacity or quantization boundary. A constant difference inside one cell can be a local approximation. |
| For equal ticks, increasing `a` one step changes `p` by `b` | Bilinear product in that basis | Swap the two roles and check a second increment or a corner of the domain. With compatible exact count axes the mixed difference of a plain sum is zero, of `a*b` is one. A constant mixed difference can leave independent additive terms on each axis. Inspect the axis profiles, bounds, clipping and units before accepting a pure product. |
| Actual successor links repeat one action | Repeat controller, recurrence, or a composable step | Perturb the operand mid-run and look at the next transition, held demand and the admission schedule. Samples `0,1,2` alone fit both a rising trend and a three-step ramp. Their next values differ. [Prior learning experiment plan](GojoSignalPatternLearningPlan-2026-09.md) |
| Occupied lanes correspond after a polarity change | Boolean candidate or a symmetry of a selected read | Compare the original and transformed participation through actual site correspondences. Product and AND agree on all four pairs of one-bit values. A bigger count, a different role, or a gate that acts can distinguish the uses. |
| A reflected or rotated input gives a correspondingly transformed output | Equivariance in a declared pair of frames | Change the landmark or traversal and test the transformed output, including a fixed point if relevant. An invariant coarse reading can hide different orientations. Flow-face duality, scalar negation, reversal, and basis change each need their own correspondence. |

Multiply by stretching and multiply by repetition illustrate the difference. A live stretch around datum `d` has a natural frame `y=d+k(x-d)`. It can keep a fixed point and couple several sites through one gain. A repeat may copy one admitted sample into a frozen controller or sample afresh on each authorized step. It has a successor path, opportunities to hold, and a timeline. All can publish the same `3 × 4 = 12` on a selected count read with a zero datum and fixed input. The evidence suggests different candidate structures. The equation alone cannot predict what happens after a gain edit, a gate closure or a changed sample between repeats.

Gojo can keep those candidates beside a shared output read. A future request to extend a path, adjust a span, or explain a product tells us which relationships are useful. Keep both candidates when no available authorized observation distinguishes them. A rejected deeper claim can leave its coarser read intact. Existing equivalence-revision fixtures already exercise that pattern. ## Three ways to discover a candidate

1. **Recognize a known law in a closed table.** Try a short list of operation surfaces and prove agreement across the declared table. This is cheap to reason about and gives us a positive control. Neon already does this for several transition families. A hand-authored truth table makes recognition possible. A table inferred from partial observations can still carry exclusions based only on insufficient evidence. [Neon recognizer](https://github.com/It3rate/Gojo/blob/6f655fc2/Gojo.EncodingC/Compilation/CompiledPlan.cs#L258) · [Learned table](../../Gojo.EncodingC/Learning/LearnedTable.cs#L117)
2. **Build a small candidate from patterns and ask for a distinguishing read.** This is the next experiment. Begin with a supplied library of affine, balance, product, occupancy and repeat patterns. Bind them using the exposed roles. A selected port may rule out candidates on applicability before we spend any probes. Finite differences, actual motif reuse and equivalences can suggest a proposal or a new role binding. Reject on a witnessed disagreement, retain unexplained cases, and ask another permissible question only where it would change what we know. If the source is opaque, the next answer must come from an authorized port. If we only have a snapshot, coverage stops there. Distinguishing live alternatives does not guarantee the true source is in the library. Gojo already has a useful baseline. In its evidence-selection exercise, a simple disagreement policy and a fitted ranking both identified the expected candidate in 32 of 32 held-out cases with 1.25 requests per case under a shared supplied vocabulary. When the true structure was omitted, a survivor could look unique and still fail independent assessment. [Evidence selection experiments](GojoEvidenceSelectionExperiments.md)
3. **Fit broader expressions or learn new reusable patterns.** Later, use symbolic regression for noisy magnitudes or coordinates, and factor recurring bound subgraphs across multiple worlds. Search over finite laws, exact equation surfaces and repeat templates before introducing much larger grammars. Test transfer with new sources and role bindings, so a reusable abstraction is more than a shortened expression for the original observed pairs. When two candidate graphs have equivalent declared behavior, rewriting can help find cheaper derived executions. Equality of a pure expression is too narrow a premise for rewriting a whole material route or timeline.

Claude's latest proposal works backward from anonymous table rows to a balance and a binding. For a small exact domain, try primitive integer weight vectors that make the admitted tuples sum to zero. Use the supplied ordered roles and a declared unit site if trying an affine offset. A common integer divisor can be removed and an orientation chosen when it preserves the relation. Agreement on admitted tuples only supplies a candidate. Several independent weight vectors may fit the same observations. For exact use, reconstruct the balance with its binding and compare rejected tuples and old output states as well. A free remainder role needs an already justified basis and remainder law. An unconstrained hidden variable can explain every mismatch. [Claude's exact fitting and recognition proposal](https://github.com/It3rate/Gojo/blob/6a7d3f17/ops_claude/GojoOperationsProposal-2026-11.md#32-exact-fits-for-small-tables)

## What to store and what to compress

For discovery, choose narrow, structured observations before choosing a compression algorithm. Store role order, source and occurrence correspondence, exact values or possible supports, timing where applicable, and the rejected or unexplained part of a read. Use shared templates for repeated site and wiring patterns. An exception has an actual route or observation it changes. Preserve a reference to a named pair or prior event only where a later read needs it. Project a matrix, a sample table or derived features from this structure for the search. A missing value remains open, an observed zero participates, and two observations of one source remain dependent.

For a selected read, distinguish four things we can actually measure:

| Change | What it buys | What to check before accepting it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encode repeated address patterns once | Smaller persistence for the same graph | Loading restores role order, local sharing, occurrence identity, overrides and schedule. Hold out a batch, then deliberately swap one row's reference or share a factor between two roles. The compact pattern must preserve the exception or use the general format. |
| Share a bound operation template | Reuse one structure across new held-out bindings | Retain each use's own sites, state and usable continuation. Verify held-out instances and look for a counterexample to presumed symmetry or independence. |
| Replace a requested read with a compact equation | A smaller executable interpretation for a selected basis and scope | Reproduce the exposed answer, admissibility, unresolved support and any promised residue. Preserve a separate joint, inverse or history route if that read still matters. For an approximate read declare the validation envelope, loss and fallback. |
| Lower the selected execution to hardware | Less work per repeated call | Verify valid codes, widening, clipping and residue, observable timing, dependencies and safe output ownership. Rebind or fall back when premises change. Keep the same declared read for the benchmark. |

The last comparison made the codec opportunity concrete. With 1,024 aligned two-bit products, the direct relation saved in 7,227 bytes and the radix-2 lattice in 70,394 bytes. Law payload was already shared. Their role references occupy 30,720 and 471,040 bits respectively, even though this fixture's references follow `i -> i`. An explicit address rule with overrides could compress the layout. The current measurements do not include such a format. This is a good separate control for discovery. Recovering a known repeated address pattern proves a layout property, while recognizing multiplication proves a property of an exposed read. The description-length idea from Claude is useful if the accounting includes the shared library, bound uses, guards, exceptions, and retained evidence as well as the nominal equation. Track the working state and runtime separately. Implicit polynomial fits are another later source of hypotheses for relations that read in multiple directions. A polynomial that vanishes on finite observations still needs new evidence or a complete finite comparison before substitution. Keep code size, saved size, transient memory, amount of evidence, qualification cost, and repeated execution cost visible independently. Compare eligible candidates on the same obligations first. A smaller prediction can be worth retaining alongside a more expensive explanation or action surface. Reversible storage, exact replay and a forecast have different evidence needs. ### Native execution

The plain CPU arithmetic is already feasible. Neon lowers a checked bounded transition to widened integer multiplication. It still reads and writes fields lane by lane and checks whether the wiring supports substitution. On small inputs the operation itself can be cheaper than decoding the fields, checking guards and publishing results. Neon also has a separate SIMD path for bit-plane transitions. Packed machine words are another derived layout with their own costs. The goal for a follow-on benchmark is a captured batch of independent settled roles, straightforward packed integer operands, widened outputs, and an explicit role correspondence. Time compilation and guards, packing, kernel, and publication separately and report the end-to-end break-even. Test malformed values, stale wiring, incompatible clipping, shared writable sites and schedule differences as well as the clean case. [Current multiply implementation](https://github.com/It3rate/Gojo/blob/6f655fc2/Gojo.EncodingC/Compilation/CompiledPlan.cs#L548)

A short GPU kernel can perform the same arithmetic. A useful GPU backend needs a sufficiently large or fused batch and a plan for keeping values resident through more than one operation. Treat that as an engineering step after measuring the packed CPU path. If the caller can read intermediates, unresolved alternatives, or the clock, the backend must publish those at the promised boundary or hand that work to the executable source. A traditional-looking `y=a*b` is a valuable read of the checked graph, and it can become a native equation surface for future consumers. The compiled form is derived from that surface and its contract. [Existing relation-shaped equations](../Equations/README.md#sum-and-product-surfaces) · [CUDA guidance on device residency](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-c-best-practices-guide/index.html#what-runs-on-a-cuda-enabled-device)

## The numeric boundary

The pre-numeric experiments stay honest about what the system has actually established. Ordinal order and role direction are observable even when a calibrated distance is absent. Occupied, absent, outside scope, and unavailable have different consequences. A coarse gate can answer a useful question without estimating the magnitude. Staying entirely pre-numeric would prevent us from testing whether differences are constant, whether two calibrations agree, whether a controller sampled six units or nine, and whether a compact law extrapolates to another tick. An exact calibrated count lets us ask those questions and keep the answer conditional on its read. [Pre-numeric assessment](GojoPreNumericAssessment.md#qualitative-and-quantitative-refinement)

The initial fixture can use existing bounded shapes with exact counts `0..3` for factors and `0..9` for the product. Keep direction, identity, open alternatives and conservation roles in the wiring. Relating the count basis to a different carrier requires a correspondence wherever we claim shared units or scale. A binary encoding of the count and a choice of physical unit are independent. On the bit grade, AND and arithmetic product are extensionally identical. At the next grade `2 × 3 = 6` is useful evidence for a count law. It still cannot tell us whether the six came from a stretch or six actual pair occurrences.

The first added rung should answer a concrete request. A coarse port reports two complete groups of four for both quantities 9 and 11. A receiver requesting 10 cannot settle availability from that port. If the fine remainder is exposed, one remainder is 1 and the other 3. Rereading either source changes no stock. A distinct permitted transfer can spend a packet. The numeric representation can use two radix-4 digits over 0 to 15 with a justified 4:1 correspondence. In the current take/give convention, grouping upward uses `Convert(4,1)` and refining downward uses `Convert(1,4)`. Include an explicit staging or pending route for the sixteenth arrival when it cannot be represented. Include a refinement refusal and a source that publishes only the coarse cell. The result should stay open in that case. This is one small setting where place value, read resolution, truthful loss and material carry can all be distinguished. [Exact bounded quantities](GojoBoundedQuantityFixture.md) · [Finite read loss](GojoFiniteReadFixture.md) · [Native refinement transfer](../Structures/RefinementTransfer.cs)

Signed quantities, fractions and wider ladders can follow concrete counterexamples that require them. Keep structural dual direction independent of signed coordinates and test reference reversal separately from either. Later an oscillator should compare two samples at the same position with opposite velocities, and check a changed time step or a displaced center under the chosen integration order. A sine read might then be an excellent finite-horizon prediction. It needs its own error envelope and cannot establish which internal dynamics supplied it.

## Original implementation proposal

Build one small **discovery and conformance fixture** before generalizing the binder, changing the save format, or adding a backend. Use an authored supplier through a narrow observation port, a candidate workspace and an independent comparison. The proposal library and probing budget are supplied by the fixture. The source's recipe is available to the verifier when appropriate. The candidate selector sees only authorized read evidence. This tests learning a local interpretation before we ask the system to invent its own vocabulary.

For the first executable pass, use the existing EncodingC bounded product and balance-style relations, `Supports` for each proposed answer, a published observation, and a comparison such as `Settling.ValueGap`. Keep speculative wiring and any enactment in an appropriate local workspace or fork. Use the native calibration, conformance and repeat structures as design precedents for the corresponding witnesses. The first pass stays in one representation. A later native bridge needs an actual correspondence for participants, basis and open support under the selected read. [EncodingC read and workspace contracts](../../Gojo.EncodingC/README.md) · [Shared encoding core](../../Gojo.Encoding/Core/README.md)

Use one closed-table control from Claude's proposal. Present an anonymous weighted sum such as `a+2b=s` on declared bounded roles, recover candidate integer weights, and compare the entire table in the same binding. Keep a one-entry mutation and a deliberately irregular table as rejection controls. This shows what known-family recognition can do before testing learning from incomplete observations.

1. **Find a discriminator.** Give the selector `(0,0) -> 0` and `(2,2) -> 4`. An ordinary sum and a product both fit. Bind executable alternatives and record their forecasts before the next receipt. If the receiver can ask for `(0,3)`, a result of 0 separates it from plain addition. A held-out `(2,3) -> 6` exercises nonzero factors. For an opaque source which exposes only the initial observations, retention of both candidates is a successful outcome. Freeze the candidate before receiving held-out cases. Use the existing qualification patterns and compare every selected case with the concrete reference. [Evidence selection](GojoEvidenceSelectionExperiments.md) · [Finite-law conformance](../../Gojo.Encoding/Core/Native/FiniteLawConformance.cs)
2. **Check the range of the claim.** Compare the resulting read against all 16 exact operand pairs for the authored reference. Add a second source whose published corner at `(3,2)` is 5 even though `(2,3)` is 6. The compact product must fail exact acceptance over that domain. Keep the discrepant pair, retain a known fitting subdomain with an explicit exception route if useful, or stick with the table. A missing answer stays open and can only justify scoped qualification. Test a restricted or open output, a same-site factor bound twice, and incompatible units or role orientation. Report whether the check covers concrete forward products, reverse joint support, or both.
