## Numerical boundaries

Light acquisition is opaque. Its presence publication can be1,0 or open;
these mean present, known absent or unavailable in this local sensor contract.
Presence0 is not zero brightness. A present acquisition retains an open
magnitude slot and can participate in requested pair-order comparisons.
Orientation is a directed instrument-frame to body-frame calibration, separate
from whether a comparison sign is positive or negative.

One center acquisition updates relative banks every two physical ticks. Fast
attention leaks3/4 and slow31/32, initial weight64, maximum48 references.
Comparison scores in−16..16 are normalized weighted signs, not amplitude,
probability, brightness difference or distance between landmark indices.
Zero from mixed/rounded votes remains distinct from every pair being equal.
The odd intervening tick explicitly reuses a read with age1. Unavailable
acquisitions retain old banks and expose open current evidence; known absence
on an acquisition step decays memory without adding a numeric-zero anchor.
Leaks count valid acquisition steps rather than elapsed physical time.

Energy and heat have separately declared local unit carriers, each finite
capacity−100..100. A body partition publishes irregular coarse regions; the
world never retains the private fine value inside the coarse witness. Finer
body reads use the receiver's finite capacity and remain subject to clipping.
Time is a third carrier. Native affine correspondences connect horizon4 to
energy allowance16, heat allowance20 and the wait-memory response4. This is an
authored conservative stationary-wait contract, not calibration of light or a
claim that all arbitrary future actions fit that resource bound.

Under the declared query prices, four stationary non-overheated ticks cost
at most four basal energy units, at most three initial finer reads0.75, and
fewer than0.4 reference-comparison energy units. They request no spatial read.
Allowance16 dominates that expenditure. Heat20 bounds four active-near-source
ticks at5 each. A supported forecast≤74 keeps the admitted wait below the
world's heat90 damage boundary. Outcomes still test the physical result.

Coarse energy minus a coarse publication of the allowance is a conservative
native difference image. Computed arithmetic, a native region-pair table and
captured packed rows must preserve the same action/resource/query trajectories.
The captured plan is a derived execution projection and checks its source
law/table. Coarse support that straddles an action remains unresolved; selective
body refinement may supply exact available integer units. Finite subtraction
and heat addition retain open endpoints/loss instead of recovering discarded
precision. A coarse heat union is conservatively enveloped when entering the
finite arithmetic adapter; that loses detail without inventing supplier data.

## Native response grammar

SupportDecisionSurface builds and reads:

```text
condition = G(role, closed required frame)
rule      = G(G(conditions), response)
policy    = G(ordered rules)
binding   = G(role, publication)
response  = G(source coordinate, affine calibration)
```

The helper admits a condition only if the entire published support is inside
its same-axis closed frame. Point witnesses, finite qualified support and
coarse region unions have local readers. Empty support, foreign basis,
unavailable publication, partial overlap or open endpoints cannot silently
settle a condition. Fully disjoint support is rejected; intersecting but
insufficient support remains unresolved. Neither path reads private source
ancestry. Malformed qualified/coarse shapes cannot fall through as exact
scalar results.

Policy order is explicitly authored. The first completely admitted rule wins.
A later unconditional fallback is an explicit response route, retaining earlier
failed/unresolved rule reads; it does not establish those unknown conditions
as false. Selection witnesses retain selected rule, admitted bindings, earlier
reads and the selected rule read. Root response projection captures the current
memory coordinate and calibration before `Set` delta settlement.

The controller's heading, feed/cool mode, remaining wait and re-arm delay are
native carriers. Query, mode, dip, action and memory rules are connected groups.
Hot-state departure has priority over low-reserve seeking. Recent rising
evidence can keep heading without buying spatial order. A short decline against
positive longer context may admit a bounded wait only when reserve and heat
forecasts support it. Otherwise eye order guides seek-toward or cool-away
motion. The world publishes observations and executes the resulting push; it
does not change these memories or inject an internal reward.

## Experiment and evidence

The external31-cell world has seed-defined moving patches, short clouds, source
off windows and instrument downtime. It has recoverable starvation/heat costs.
A completed cycle requires a new feeding threshold crossing below80 to at
least80, followed by cooling to20 at least3 cells away with energy at least20.
Remaining full outside food cannot re-arm the outcome counter every tick.

Nine controllers remove one connection or service at construction: presence
response routes, orientation, recent evidence, longer context, wait budgets,
finer body reads; always-fine and uninformed always-right are additional
comparisons. Same seeds, sensor laws, fees, physics and initial states are used.
Unused relative banks still incur the same eager observer cost. This isolates
policy use, not a zero-computation neutral organism.

Focused encounters establish changed native actions or saved service requests:
missing/absent/present-zero, mirrored eyes, already-sufficient coarse support,
79-versus80 energy, a short decline, unsafe energy/heat waits and a clear rise.
Backend, private monotone brightness transformations and mirrored/forward
instruments require full trajectory signatures to match. Final archive
readback checks exposed policy grammar, within-graph carrier aliases, current
memory, observer weights and amount law/table correspondence.

Use counts are selected native condition bindings plus explicit dependency
expansion. Precision is credited only if the selected action/mode consumes its
publication; a purchased but unused finer value is not useful by definition.
Participation is not proof of benefit. Aggregate outcomes and focused matched
decisions are reported even when a removed capability performs better.

The replay uses SVG/HTML and recorded JSON, separately from the existing Skia
aquarium. Detailed historical step projections are retained; only final native
operational graphs are archived. No Jev or learning is involved. Future
experiments can evolve these native rules or select local interpretations after
the usefulness of each boundary is established.
