# Adaptive wallpaper: where training fits

This note separates existing mechanisms from proposed training over pre-numeric
motif machines. It supplies an experimental direction, not campaign results or
a claim that Gojo already learns wallpaper symmetries. The accompanying
[machine report](GojoPatternMachineExperiment.md) records executable checkpoints.

## Adaptation and training are different

A neighbour's signal can select another motif under an unchanged finite law.
That is live adaptation: the machine executes an authored response. Training
requires evaluated consequences to change retained policy, variant preference
or connection proposals, so a later decision differs because of that evidence.

The useful boundary is the same for both: an offered edit must produce actual
motif structure, publish its current ports, renegotiate applicable seams and
preserve pressure ownership. A learner cannot declare a junction accepted,
invent a successful drawing receipt or clear blocked packets through a score.

## Current reusable mechanisms

| Existing source | What it supplies | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| [FriezeLearning](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/FriezeLearning.cs) | Bounded search over actual phase-to-word law bindings; parent edits, evaluation, selection and archive history | Learning new phase structure, routes, reaches or word multiplicities; its ranking uses optional measured observers |
| [PatternAestheticProfile](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/PatternAestheticProfile.cs) | Native criteria, actual supporting observations, preferred/passed/violated/unknown reads | Universal human beauty or independent validation of an observer's supplied evidence |
| [ConnectedPatternScoring](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/ConnectedPatternScoring.cs) | Separate native graph and measured profiles; finite slot bands for native feature participation | A calibration-independent area or clearance law |
| [QualitativeStrokeGraph](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/QualitativeStrokeGraph.cs) | Junction reads and checked site/stroke/chord bijections | Every graph correspondence being a geometric rotation, mirror or isometry |
| [QualitativeGraphJoin](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/QualitativeGraphJoin.cs) | Actual join declarations, position checks, accepted/blocked evidence and source correspondence | Contact inferred solely from equal rendered coordinates |
| [QualitativePacketTransport](../../experiments/QualitativeExecution/QualitativePacketTransport.cs) | Identity-preserving current ownership, explicit passage capacity and checked moves | Continuous signed carrier tension or a general autonomous scheduler |
| [AdaptiveWallpaperAssessment](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/AdaptiveWallpaperAssessment.cs) | Captured categorical checks for settled accounting, required seam fulfillment and resolved choices | An aesthetic score or retained policy improvement |
| [AdaptiveWallpaperSymmetry](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/AdaptiveWallpaperSymmetry.cs) | Actual motif graph maps, exact port aliases and formal quarter-turn seam correspondence | Directed flow symmetry or an arbitrary Euclidean metric claim |

The source program and its observer can have different refinement levels.
Existing frieze search keeps numeric carriers out of the motif source while
using numeric utility observations in the trainer. That is a valid explicit
composition, but the complete search must not be described as number-free.

## Feasibility before preference

Reject or retain as unresolved a proposal that cannot prove these obligations:

- Each imported pressure packet has exactly one current owner.
- Delivery uses the current accepted seam and the applicable motif/port state.
- Withdrawal or failed renegotiation preserves queued pressure and its cause.
- A stale receipt cannot accept a changed motif, port or target contract.
- Required neighbour obligations remain in the comparison, even when blocked.
- Motif, phase, port and retained proposal capacity stay inside declared scopes.

Deleting every offered port must not turn a failed required connection into
success. Keep the original required-seam surface and expose each obligation as
accepted, blocked or unresolved against the candidate. An intentionally optional
connection can be withdrawn, but its withdrawal is a different witnessed action.

All-hold, all-disconnected and uniformly filled candidates are useful negative
controls. They can be simple or symmetric while failing activity, connection
or role-contrast requirements. A known failure and an unavailable observation
must remain different reads; missing evidence cannot contribute favorable zero.

## A small native training contract

The candidate is connected structure, not a hidden array of semantic labels:

```text
candidate = (parent -> proposed-policy) -> actual-binding-edit
evaluation = (candidate -> observation-window) ->
             (feasibility-witnesses -> preference-read)
selection = (incumbent-evaluation -> candidate-evaluation) -> retained-support
```

Legal edits can rebind finite-law outputs, select a compatible motif variant,
change an exposed phase transition or offer another declared port correspondence.
Each edit must retain the old/new participants and applicable interface contract.
Changing a required obligation or an evaluation criterion is a separate edit,
not an unrecorded way to make a candidate win.

For symmetric constructions, edit one orbit representative and derive its peers
through explicit transform applications and correspondence. This reduces the
search space while preserving why motifs agree. It supplies a symmetry premise;
it does not demonstrate that a learner discovered symmetry unaided.

The present adaptive machine chooses between two authored reflected templates
per grammar. Training only these choices cannot invent a new contour family.
Broader pattern generation needs an expanded legal edit surface over the native
path/chord bindings, followed by the same port, closure and symmetry readers.
Cross-grammar neighbours would also need exposed boundary correspondences;
today each field shares one grammar. A candidate contract must name which
bindings may change. Editing a held reference policy or a required obligation
is a separate experiment, not an unrecorded way to improve the candidate.

After feasibility, use [categorical aesthetic criteria](GojoPatternAestheticRules.md)
with actual feature witnesses: compatible required seams, requested transform
correspondences, bounded vocabulary, junction variety and restrained repair churn.
Keep alternative useful families instead of forcing every tradeoff into one sum.
An optional numeric score may rank these observations under an exposed basis;
its weights and missing-data policy are authored interpretation, not kernel law.

Motif graph symmetry, controller/flow symmetry and rendered metric symmetry are
separate questions. A symmetric drawing does not establish equal queue states
or an equivariant conditional policy. A flow symmetry needs correspondence for
ports, phases, transitions and packet participation as well as drawn strokes.

## Candidate training approaches

These are proposed integrations; the wallpaper experiment does not yet compare
their training performance.

| Approach | Useful role and native representation | Essential comparison control |
|---|---|---|
| Enumeration / constraint repair | Enumerate the small legal edit grammar; retain candidate, violated obligation and repair witness | Exhaustive small cases; unchanged-policy and no-feedback controls; preserve no-solution outcomes |
| Genetic search | Mutate law bindings or whole compatible motif modules; retain parents, actual edits, evaluations and archive admission | Random legal proposals with the same budget; fixed grammar/capacity; held-out initial arrangements |
| Market | Purchase bounded inspection, motif proposal or finer observation through actual offer/publication/consumer ports | No-market and fixed-allocation controls; finite conserved funding; charge discovery and failed service costs |
| Transformer / language model | Propose native binding edits from advertised observations and counterexamples; retain proposal provenance | Same independent validators, grammar and budget as other proposers; evaluate later outcomes rather than trusting explanations |

A diversity archive can keep useful alternatives indexed by verified symmetry
correspondence, occupied junction signature and settled versus oscillatory
response. The [MAP-Elites paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909) motivates searching
for diverse high-performing repertoires. Its algorithm has not been integrated
here, and choosing archive descriptors remains an explicit experimental choice.

The [FunSearch paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6) provides
a relevant precedent for a frozen language model proposing programs against a
systematic evaluator. Here the corresponding proposal would be a constrained
Gojo edit. A model's explanation can be provenance; it cannot replace a proof
of packet accounting, seam compatibility or an observed improvement.

## Market reuse and limits

[NegotiatedPorts](../../experiments/NegotiatedPorts/README.md) already separates
non-draining information publications from drained resource payment and retains
pending promises before their outcomes. [UtilityEcology](../../experiments/UtilityEcology/README.md)
adds learning permission and progress observers. Both use numeric resource
structure; they would be explicit outer layers around a pre-numeric machine.

The [market assessment](../../experiments/MarketTraining/ASSESSMENT.md) records
that paired probes did not establish preferential selection of cheaper or more
valuable useful providers. A market is therefore a candidate allocation
mechanism, not an established optimizer or a source of aesthetic truth.

## First comparison protocol

One concrete target is reducing the key field's 60 accepted motif replacements
while preserving its 40 required seams, formal quarter-turn symmetry and packet
accounting. A learned policy could delay redundant redraws or choose which
advertisement to query, even when its final contour matches the fixed-law run.

Start with bounded enumeration or greedy repair, then compare genetic proposals
and model-assisted edits on the same grammar. Use independent worlds with shared
input tapes, equal proposal/query budgets and retained rejected counterexamples.
Evaluate unseen initial motifs, seed locations and phase arrangements as well
as the training window. Record accepted required seams, remaining blocked
ownership, policy changes and structural capacity separately from visual ranks.

Withdraw a seam after enqueueing, then restore compatible ports: the same held
packet should become deliverable without duplication. Rebind the complete
exposed world through a rotation or reflection to test equivariance independently
of screen coordinates. Keep a frozen-policy adaptation arm so improved execution
is not mistaken for learning a better policy.
