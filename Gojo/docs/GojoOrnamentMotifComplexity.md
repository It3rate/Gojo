# Ornament motif complexity: a declared read

This experiment separates **reviewed conceptual construction estimates** from
**recovered encoding statistics**. The former is an editorial hypothesis at a
stated granularity. The latter is a local read of the actual native enrichment,
attachments and retained prototype correspondences. Neither is autonomous
conceptual discovery.

## What is being counted

The reviewed granularity is one repeating unit's *ornamental construction
vocabulary*: reusable shapes, internal articulation, symmetry/repetition and
attachment rules. A petal used eight times is one reusable shape plus an
arrangement rule; it is not automatically eight concepts. Long and short
petals may be one parameterized family or two separately held shapes, which
is one reason to report a range. A large motif does not become more complex
merely by taking more space.

Underlying lattice construction, palette, display fillets and repeated field
instances are outside this motif estimate. They remain relevant to a larger
composition estimate. Colour separation, occlusion, line density and placement
can make a small vocabulary visually crowded; this estimate does not replace
those aesthetic judgments.

The suggested **12** is a review trigger, not a Gojo law or a demonstrated
psychological limit. A range crossing 12 asks whether parts could be grouped
more coherently or whether a simpler reading would help. It does not invalidate
the structure. A useful next review can keep two plausible chunkings rather
than forcing one number.

The code has an explicit reviewed entry for each of the 36 detail variants.
Field and intermediate variants currently have native statistics only; their
conceptual estimate is absent, rather than silently copied from the detail.
The terms are construction-review notes; they are not a newly encoded concept
ontology and do not supply execution semantics.

## What the native read establishes

`OrnamentMotifComplexity.ReadEncoding(enrichment)` follows admitted layer
definitions and actual addition declarations. It reads the author's retained
placement receipt, mapped path correspondence and original prototype surface.
Its selected evidence has this shape:

```text
enrichment ->
  (Closed(actual prototype -> Closed(actual placed paths)) ->
   (unattributed added paths ->
    (actual source anchor selection -> actual added path selection)))
```

The reader reports source/added path and chord counts, distinct retained
prototype identities, witnessed placements, unattributed paths and actual
source anchors. A prototype used at four locations remains one prototype
identity with four actual path correspondences. Two independently authored
but geometrically equal prototypes remain two identities. Unknown or unrelated
ancestry stays unattributed. The reader does not search arbitrary nested
relations for something that resembles a prototype and does not infer a join
from equal positions.

These statistics describe the retained encoding. A single leaf can require
several path prototypes for its outline, midrib and forked veins; many placed
paths can also share one prototype. Neither direction licenses equating
prototype count with human concept count. The receipt read is an inspection of
retained authoring evidence, not a new proof that an arbitrary historical
transformation was mathematically valid.

`Review(design)` combines the separate reads for display. Native evidence keeps
its actual participants; JSON omits that live relation graph and carries the
counts, explicit estimate range, proposed terms, granularity and limits. It
does not execute a multi-cell field or inspect SVG/PNG geometry.

## Direction suggested by SUBDUE

Cook and Holder's SUBDUE evaluates a candidate with the cost of its description
plus the cost of describing the remaining graph using that candidate. Repeated
discovery/replacement creates a hierarchy, and bounded inexact matching allows
distorted instances. Its background heuristics also include connectivity,
coverage and compactness. Those are useful distinctions for a future experiment:
compression alone is not the same question as an appropriate ornamental
interpretation. See [*Substructure Discovery Using Minimum Description Length
and Background Knowledge*](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/9402102), especially sections
2, 4–6 and 8.

For Gojo this suggests an exploratory comparison of several connected local
chunkings: reusable definition cost, placement/correspondence cost, and the
unexplained remainder. Read a semantic participation surface with exposed
direction, attachment and join/pass-over distinctions; incidental codec list
cells should not win merely because every packet contains many of them. Keep
overlapping interpretations and the source complement available. Native
sharing already provides examples of reuse, but finding a new useful grouping
is still undemonstrated here. This change implements no SUBDUE search, learned
chunker, cognitive complexity metric or minimum-description-length score.

## Verification

Focused tests distinguish shared prototype identity from equal independent
construction, retain unrecognized ancestry, exercise archive import without
whole-field execution, and keep unknown sources/unreviewed modes without a
conceptual count. The 36 reviewed ranges are hypotheses for conversation and
future experiments; test coverage does not make those hypotheses empirically
validated cognitive measurements.
