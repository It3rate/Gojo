# Compound ornaments, repetition and colour intentions

Status: executable exploratory study, October 2026. This extends the native
curve/band mini-engine without replacing the previous collections. It adds
reusable compound application, two kinds of repetition, exposed seam reads
and separately assessed colour intentions above the kernel.

The [gallery](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-composition/gallery.html)
has sixteen designs, two colour readings of each, construction-frame views,
selected-unit views and native archives. The
[whole sheet](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-composition/overview.html)
shows both colour readings together. The frame overlay is a read of retained
geometry, not a reconstruction from the coloured image.

## What is being defined

The source is a connected program of curves, corresponding side profiles,
occupancy selections, prototype compounds, transform applications, repeat
controllers, roles, exposed ports and questions. A title such as “palmette”
does not generate its geometry. Conversely, the picture is one interpretation
of the program, not its complete definition.

```text
curve + corresponding side offsets
    -> band envelope + occupancy + end conditions
    -> whole-thread template
    -> reusable compound of sibling templates
    -> transform / placement plan / output-feedback orbit
    -> field and source-to-descendant correspondences
    -> selected frame, chain, motif and detail scopes
    -> colour intentions and independent ordinal publications
    -> selected rendering
```

The four role scopes select actual envelopes and remain connected to their
owner. Nested templates retain child-to-thread correspondence. Copies retain
source-envelope/output-envelope and source-site/output-site pairs. The same
native field receives both colour readings; changing the palette does not
rebuild or perturb its geometry.

## Native compound application

The reusable template is a scope of whole threads. Each thread contains actual
band envelopes. Composition retains the child surfaces and their selected
threads rather than reducing everything to an unrelated flat path recipe.

```text
template = (frame -> Closed(Closed(envelopes), ...)) -> Closed(child -> threads)
instruction = substitution -> displacement
application = (template -> instruction) ->
              ((output -> Closed(envelope rows)) ->
               (Closed(site pairs) -> residue))

envelope row = (source envelope -> output envelope) ->
               (Closed(position rows) -> Closed(offset rows))
position row = (source control -> output control) ->
               (substitution receipt -> displacement-sum receipt)
```

The existing `QualitativePatternTransform` substitutes actual direction/reach
participation. Position controls consume both that substitution and the
placement displacement. **Side-offset words receive the substitution but not
the translation.** Translating an offset as though it were a point would
inflate or displace the band incorrectly. All consumed rows and additive
receipts remain connected and are rechecked by the application validator.

Shared sites inside a prototype remain shared inside each copy. Separate
applications receive fresh sites. Equality of drawn endpoints is not a weld.
Occupancy, caps, continuation and boundary identities survive application.

Quarter-turn constructions include an explicit selected similarity premise.
Other direction substitutions may be affine, including the inclined leaves.
They do not claim Euclidean length preservation or equal circular partitions.
Unmeasured common-reach words still supply the local position/offset grammar;
the authoring counts are not measured lengths or carrier-valued pixel data.

## Two different repetition laws

**Output feedback** applies an instruction to the preceding output. This is
appropriate for a rotational orbit, repeated deformation, or a translation
chain. The native controller retains phase identities, successor relations,
actual output-to-next-input feedback, an ordinal timeline and stop/residue.

**Prototype placement** repeatedly selects one fixed compound and places it
under an ordered plan of local orientations and translations. This is needed
for alternating mirrors and dropped rows. The phase instructions, copies and
timeline remain connected, but one copy's geometry does not become the source
geometry of the next copy.

This distinction matters mathematically. Iterating a global reflection plus a
fixed translation can bring positions back toward one another. It does not
generally produce a line of independently mirrored tiles. A local orientation
followed by a separate placement is a different construction. Both recipes
are now available without hiding the difference in rendering.

The controller/window is finite. This study does not prove every boundary of
an infinite wallpaper. Timeline tick addresses record ordinal steps; they are
not geometric measurements. Carrier value and tension remain zero during
these structural constructions.

## Seam evidence and gaps it exposed

Exposed entry/exit ports retain the actual envelope, spine endpoint and both
boundary sites. A seam read asks separately about:

- coincident endpoint positions;
- compatible forward tangent directions;
- A→A and B→B side agreement;
- A→B and B→A exchanged-side agreement;
- actual joined-site identity.

Reflection made the exchanged-side question necessary. A failure of same-side
agreement can mean that a correspondence must exchange sides, rather than
that the widths differ. Conversely, compatible sides do not prove compatible
flow direction. Neither comparison automatically reverses a path or joins it.

O004's valleys are intentionally corner-like. Their correspondence is not a
promise of smooth tangency. Current seam questions are retained even where a
different join interpretation would be useful. There is no general physical
seam negotiation, capacity transfer or automatic union in this round.

Seams must move with their units. The new mapper re-reads seam questions over
the copied envelopes, while preserving old/new question correspondence in
provenance. Keeping an old witness as though it described a newly placed row
would be stale evidence.

## Colour as intention and evidence

The companion [colour-intention notes](GojoOrnamentColourIntent.md) give the
source research and exact native contract. Jones's consulted original has
**37 propositions**. Useful preferences include distinguishing parts through
colour, using strong accents against quieter masses, maintaining the distant
effect and separating adjacent coloured masses with edging. His advancing/
receding claims concern architectural surfaces; they are not applied here as
universal laws of flat wallpaper.

The source records a desired ordinal relation independently of the receiver's
published support. Each question keeps its positive and contrary alternatives,
orientation and response direction. Checks can be supported, opposed or open.
The aggregate rechecks the underlying intent and observation, so forging a
favourable diagnostic/native result scope does not create favourable evidence.

The display adapter currently publishes comparisons using **squared sRGB
code-distance**, an explicitly chosen rough proxy. It compares chain/motif
contrast against the actual ground, accent difference against its intended
host fill, and outline difference against the adjacent role colours. This is
not calibrated perceptual distance, pigment balance, area-weighted salience or
a beauty score. The outline/host comparisons are role-level checks, not proof
that every relevant boundary has the right adjacency.

An attention/recession query has separate native ports, a separate observer
frame and an open observation. It is not another uncertain copy of an already
known RGB comparison. This keeps missing perceptual evidence distinct from
known display-code contrast.

Each design receives another palette and an alternate motif-emphasis
intention. Some palettes oppose that desired hierarchy. Those mismatches are
retained; the intent is not rewritten to make every palette succeed. A flat
paint control collapses the role colours to the ground and fails the contrast
checks while preserving all geometric structure.

The conditional compatibility calculation reuses the existing estimated
15/16 hit, 1/16 miss and 1/2 unresolved channel. Related cue checks enter one
aggregate mixture, not a product of independent observations. Its resulting
fraction is conditional compatibility under this authored channel, not a
probability that humans find the ornament beautiful. No calibration or model
training has occurred in this round.

## What the exercise establishes, and what remains missing

The new designs no longer require an independent equation or silhouette
factory for every image. Shared petals/leaves become compounds; compounds
become medallions or sprigs; those repeat in dispositions. Curves, widths,
occupancy and roles survive through those levels. A receiver can select a
frame, a complete unit, its sides or its colour questions without recovering
them from pixels.

The important remaining gaps are:

1. **Attachment mechanics.** Leaves use explicit shared-frame placements.
   Most do not yet negotiate a branch landmark, joint identity or tangency
   with a parent stem. Membership and useful placement are implemented;
   general tangential botanical growth is not.
2. **Region topology.** Ruled occupancy does not certify a simple,
   nonoverlapping global fill. Union, holes, contact and border ownership
   require additional native participation and reads.
3. **Curved intersections.** The braid/basket use declared straight-section
   crossings. General curved crossing discovery and continuous knot routing
   remain open. Paint order elsewhere is a presentation choice, not an
   automatic physical depth claim.
4. **Compact recipe transport.** Prototype/instruction structure is reused,
   but review archives also retain expanded copies and detailed proof
   history. They are much larger than the recipe. A lazy program/working-set
   transport and an independently rebound curve-frame executor would be
   needed before treating this as a compact production training format.
5. **Perceptual colour and emphasis.** Area, spatial frequency, contextual
   contrast and learned attention are absent from the current code-distance
   proxy. The open perceptual query is intentional.
6. **Recognition.** These are generated, inspectable examples with retained
   answers. Their motifs, provenance and roles would need to be withheld
   appropriately in a recognition experiment.

These gaps are now easier to isolate. For example, a future attachment test
can hold the repeat and band construction fixed while comparing landmark,
tangent and joint constraints. A colour test can preserve every envelope and
change only the desired role hierarchy and observer calibration.

## Reproduction

The retained round has 16 complete templates, 32 colour readings, 360 curved
spine spans and 522 occupied band supports. Across the two readings, the
contrast channel reports 80 supported and 16 opposed checks; the 32 separate
perceptual questions remain open. Every flat-paint control has three opposed
contrast checks and one open perceptual question. These counts test the
encoding and observer contract, not recognition accuracy or human preference.

The final generation took about 67 seconds locally. The full review archives
also retain expanded proof history, so their storage is substantially larger
than the small shared authoring vocabulary.

```powershell
dotnet run --project experiments/QualitativeFrieze -c Release -- --ornament-composition
node experiments/QualitativeFrieze/tools/check-ornament-composition.cjs experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-composition/gallery.html
dotnet test Tests/QualitativeExecution.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~OrnamentBandCompositionTests|FullyQualifiedName~OrnamentColourIntentTests|FullyQualifiedName~OrnamentCompositionIntegrationTests"
dotnet test Gojo.slnx
```

`--limit COUNT` bounds source generation as well as export. The collection
writes its native archives, standalone SVGs, frame overlays, comparison data,
gallery, overview and summary. No bitmap inference is used. SVG fills and
masks remain downstream observations; masked paths are retained natively.

The focused suites cover consumed transform receipts, copied shared sites,
zero versus unknown support, recursive composition, controller feedback,
prototype plans, mirrored side exchange, open/failed applications, native
role/port selections, current seam correspondence, colour uncertainty and
forged evidence rejection. The browser checker verifies local navigation,
both colour readings, overlays, SVG references and native graph transport.
The generic archive audit reports ordinal address ranges and quiet carrier
state; it does not pretend to classify the semantic role of every address.

Recorded verification: the solution-wide run passed **5,231 tests** with no
failures or skips. After the final placement-plan and evidence safeguards,
all **35 focused tests/executions** passed (18 composition, 8 colour-intention
and 9 integration). Browser validation passed 73 states and 116 local
references with no errors, covering all 80 SVG/overlay files and 16 archives.
All sixteen ornaments and alternate readings were visually inspected; O004
and O009 were refined after that inspection.
