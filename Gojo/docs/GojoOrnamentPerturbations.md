# Gojo ornament perturbations and question comparison

Experimental checkpoint, 2026-10-18. This round asks which parts of a discovered
pattern survive when one exposed connection or position-word binding changes.
The original candidate remains connected to its original evidence. A new read
asks whether that same candidate can still apply to the revised source.

This follows [ornament discovery and emphasis](GojoOrnamentDiscovery.md), using
the same fan field and its first derived scaffold. The whole field stays visible
while selected cells and routes provide the dominant image.

Open the [perturbation gallery](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-perturbations/gallery.html).
Its three step controls select checked native revisions. The same candidate is
shown against each revised source, alongside its supported, refuted and
unresolved obligations and the question comparison.

```powershell
dotnet run --project experiments/QualitativeFrieze -c Release -- --ornament-perturbations
```

The repeat read has a different boundary. Its cell anchors use the explicit
equal-share word interpretation from the previous experiments. One through
three extra participations do not divide into four complete equal shares;
the affected cell's anchor remains open, leaving its four old successor claims
unresolved. Four extra participations permit a complete new word anchor. Those
same four exact-successor claims can then be refuted by known nonempty
displacements. More available detail has resolved uncertainty into a
counterexample, rather than restoring the old pattern.

## The experiment's distinction

A visible ornament combines several different promises:

| Promise | Evidence needed | A change that may leave it valid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A bounded chamber has a four-edge simple boundary | Actual advertised edge identities, endpoints and ordered boundary uses | Moving a site's position word while retaining incidence |
| An edge continues a selected heading phase | Actual endpoint bindings, their formal difference and the chosen phase | Changing repeated reach participation without changing the remaining directions |
| Two occurrences are related by an exact formal translation | Actual occurrence bindings and a complete displacement reduction | Changing display aspect ratio while retaining native words |
| Two paths meet at a shared role | Actual role identity and incident relations | Separating their presentation coordinates cannot remove that native identity |
| Two projected lines cross | The selected display interpretation | The crossing may occur without any shared native role |

These promises should fail for different reasons. A deformed four-edge cell can
remain the same topological candidate while losing an exact translation or a
heading match. A removed connection can invalidate a cycle while leaving the
positions of its endpoints unchanged. An unavailable binding is incomplete
evidence, rather than proof of the opposite heading.

No geometric regularity follows from boundary arity. The gallery's diamond
appearance is an interpretation of a quadrilateral cycle; its equal sides and
angles are separate questions.

## Retain the candidate and reapply its question

The useful structural shape is:

```text
original source -> original question -> original candidate and witnesses
    -> selected exposed participation
    -> revision with carried complement and replacement
    -> candidate reapplication
    -> supported obligations + counterexamples + unresolved obligations
```

The candidate's history is not overwritten. A counterexample concerns its
application to the new source under the selected question. It does not make
the original historical witness false.

A supported route reapplication establishes the retained ordered walk and its
phase grammar. It does not establish that the route is still maximal, that
every continuation is unique, or that no new alternative branch has appeared.
Those are separate reads on the revised source.

An omitted actual connection in a complete advertised scope can supply
negative evidence. An open connection scope can hide additional participation
and therefore cannot establish the same absence. An unresolved word does not
become an empty displacement or a concrete zero.

Locality belongs to the candidate's dependency surface. If one role has an open
binding, a remote route whose bindings and edge uses remain available can still
be supported. Failure to produce a fresh global discovery result is not, by
itself, a counterexample to every preserved candidate.

P03 demonstrates this distinction. The existing whole-scope strand discovery
reader publishes no fresh routes when one word input is unresolved. The new
local reapplication reader still supports three of the four original long
strands and marks only the dependent strand unresolved. The complete old
candidate is retained even when a fresh global search cannot currently
reconstruct it.

The revisions in this experiment are explicit structural counterfactuals. They
are observational applications to revised exposed surfaces, not material
transfer or carrier settlement. No input value is silently assigned and no
read residual is renamed carrier tension.

## Position-word perturbations are not measured motion

Positions are formal words in a declared additive frame. Every move retains a
direction and a shared uncalibrated reach identity. Opposed moves cancel only
through the frame's law and a matching reach identity. Repeated participation is
explicit structure, rather than a measured coordinate stored in a carrier.

Adding a rightward move can preserve an edge's right-and-up heading while
changing its slope in the selected rendering. Further participation can cancel
the rightward component completely, leaving an upward heading. Continuing can
leave a left-and-up heading. The important boundary is the structural
appearance or disappearance of a direction after justified cancellation.

An exact zero displacement still does not identify two roles. Likewise, a
different reach identity does not become the source's common reach merely
because a renderer draws both at a similar size. Uncancelled opposite
participation at distinct reaches leaves the net direction unresolved.

A slider selects from precomputed native revisions. Its index and screen
spacing are presentation controls; each step has its own actual patch and
witnesses. The slider does not establish an underlying continuous calibrated
quantity or interpolate new semantic states between those steps.

Preserving incidence does not guarantee that every position-word interpretation
is a planar embedding with disjoint interiors. A deformation can introduce a
projected crossing or a concave enclosure without changing the retained
boundary-cycle claim. Geometric embedding and occupied-area claims need their
own questions before a render can establish them.

## Comparing questions

Several questions may provide useful reads of the same source. They need not
compete for one permanent ownership of its parts. The comparison should retain
the supplied questions, their matches and their unmatched participation.

Useful evidence includes:

| Read | Meaning of the evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Recurrence on a held-out scope | A question selected elsewhere also has witnessed uses in an explicitly withheld part |
| Coverage | Which actual source participation the accepted occurrences explain |
| Branching | Where a local continuation has more than one supported alternative |
| Description participation | How much question structure was supplied to produce that read |

Counts and display scores are diagnostic projections of those native
witnesses. A preference among coverage, recurrence, ambiguity and description
cost needs a declared comparison law. There is no universal aesthetic score
hidden in the experiment.

Held-out recurrence here is structural validation within a related field. Shared
sites, words and generating structure mean that withheld uses are not
automatically statistically independent observations. A successful repeated
pattern is evidence under these premises, not a probability of correctness in
another domain.

The displayed comparison uses a declared word cut supplied before the candidate
questions are evaluated. An edge belongs to training when both endpoint words
read on one side, and to the held-out scope when both read on the other side.
Connections crossing the cut remain in the carried complement. Cell occurrence
anchors use the same cut. Native difference reads retain the classification
evidence; route names, matched families and rendered pixels do not choose the
partition. The particular perturbation target is a separate controlled probe
selected from an already witnessed route.

The baseline split has 64 actual edges in each side and six crossing edges
carried outside the comparison. The 24 cell occurrences split twelve to each
side. Each heading reader receives only its side's induced incidence and
bindings; translation readers receive only their side's occurrence identities
and bindings. The partition and its original evidence remain fixed when the
source is perturbed. A withdrawn edge is absent from its active side rather
than replaced with another successful match.

Five heading questions compare the two complementary vine grammars, a straight
heading, a doubled vine grammar, and two flat phases followed by a diagonal.
Four translation questions compare three, five, six and seven common reach
participations in both declared axes. They are supplied alternatives, not
automatically invented grammars or learned geometric measurements.

The comparison uses a declared Pareto preference: more recurrence and coverage,
less branching and description participation. An alternative only dominates
another when it is no worse under every selected criterion and strictly better
under at least one. Ties and incomparable alternatives remain explicit. The
native cardinality witnesses pair actual evidence participants and retain
unpaired remainders; the displayed counts summarize those witnesses.

This deliberately exposes two limitations of selection without a utility
requirement. A cheap question with no matches can remain on the frontier.
Also, splitting one long walk can increase the number of qualifying route
supports while reducing coverage: at P01's fourth step the falling/rising
held-out read has three supports covering 33 edges, compared with the original
two supports covering 34. Support count alone is not a quality score. Heading
support counts unique qualified ordered walks; translation support counts
repeated successor chains. These different support bases are compared only
within their own question family.

In the baseline, each vine question finds two long recurrences per side,
covering 34 of its side's 64 edges without branch alternatives. The doubled
grammar covers the same actual ordered walks but requires twice the local
question description. Those duplicated phase labels do not count as new
recurrences: an explicit correspondence maps each duplicate route to its
retained ordered-walk proof. This is a narrow deduplication of the same ordered
edge/end footprint, not a general equivalence solver for arbitrary cyclic or
transformed patterns.

Training and held-out comparisons remain separate native reads. Unavailable
training evidence does not erase an independently complete held-out read.
Conversely, a conflicted phase labelling can have complete comparison evidence:
its known conflict remains a penalty, rather than being mistaken for a valid
alternation or an unknown outcome.

## Meaning and transfer

The experiment makes a modest operational meaning inspectable: a pattern is a
set of relations that supports particular continuations, enclosures and repeat
questions, together with the ways those claims survive or fail under change.
Two identical pictures can differ in this meaning if one has a shared junction
and the other only has an overpass, or if their position words depend on
different reach identities.

This offers a useful next step toward other domains. A route grammar, alternating
relation or shared enclosure may be reusable through an explicit correspondence
to new participants. Visual resemblance alone cannot supply that
correspondence, justify a physical analogy or prove that the new domain shares
the original laws.

The current work does not establish automatic cross-domain recognition,
universal concept discovery, physical deformation, a general learning law or a
learned aesthetic preference. It makes candidate sensitivity and question
comparison available as evidence for those later experiments.

## Verification and scale

Focused integration tests retain actual earlier candidates, verify the local
heading boundary, distinguish equal-position detachment from continuity,
preserve remote support around an open word, and distinguish unresolved
equal-share landmarks from complete translation counterexamples. They also
check disjoint actual comparison scopes and that every revision, reapplication,
partition and comparison witness is reachable from the native study capture.

All 45 new focused cases pass. Browser checks pass for discrete steps, all four
read modes, preserved originals, reset, local unresolved states, equal-position
detachment, open comparison evidence, local links, SVG parsing and desktop/mobile
layout. Colour concerns the entire original occurrence claim: a refuted route
can still contain supported subpaths. Current shared branch roles have dots;
pale carried history does not create current joins.

`dotnet test Gojo.slnx` passed all 3,710 tests, including 1,280 in the
qualitative execution test project.

The generated [diagnostics](../../experiments/QualitativeFrieze/results/ornament-perturbations/diagnostics.json)
record 7,718,334 reachable native elements and no `Carrier` or `CarrierLocation`
elements. This includes the retained original field, historical construction
and alternative reads. The capture is proof-heavy; it is not a compact
representation or a demonstration of compressed execution performance.
