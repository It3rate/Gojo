Unit standards and cancellation

A journey combines speed with elapsed time. A fuel calculation uses litres per 100 km. Both invite cancellation, but a matching dimension is not permission to ignore its tick scale.

What is checked, and where this example stops

The selected cancellation read records one matched hour pair, preserves both authored orders and the standard, refuses km versus 100 km cancellation, and accepts matching 100 km ticks.
