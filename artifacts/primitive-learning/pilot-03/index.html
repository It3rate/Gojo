What this experiment can tell us

The physical relay pays for correct routing and charges for requested information. Learners receive exposed publications and later consequences; they never receive the opaque parcel's numeric load or the answer program.

  • Test freezes learned ledgers. Training and its reference comparisons, fit probes, and shadow audits are separate work.
  • A / P / Q / S show which learners updated. A zero means that component kept its initialized structure; it is not automatically removed from the information path.
  • Shuffled feedback tests whether the connection to actual consequences matters. Neutral responses and communication changes are additional controls.
  • These are bounded episodic learning mechanisms and a finite program grammar. They do not establish unbounded synthesis, closed-loop survival, or a general intelligent system.
