# Primitive numbers: a consolidated forager

Nine authored controllers across 2 seeds, 128 ticks each; 23 runs including native backend, scale and orientation checks, 2,944 habitat ticks. No training, breeding, paid AI calls or reward-written brain memory.

## Amounts and orders stay distinct

Light starts as opaque acquisitions, presence/absence and requested order. Fast/slow score is attention-weighted ordinal comparison evidence, not a brightness estimate, confidence probability or arithmetic on landmark ranks. Zero can retain opposed votes. Body energy/heat have explicit separate local tick units; a four-time-tick service supplies conservative energy16 / heat20 costs. Coarse range arithmetic and native tables filter actions; finite arithmetic/refinement operates only in those declared amount domains. Rules admit a response only when all advertised support is contained in a condition. Explicit fallback preserves earlier unresolved rule reads.

## World and service prices

- 31 cells; start at 15, charge 60, heat 0. Seeded patch locations 4..26 change after 120..180 ticks.
- Private light is 20 + max(0, 100−4×distance). Each phase has a 6-tick cloud at its seeded local 34..44 start, reducing the directional field to 3/4.
- The source is dark during local ticks 92..99. This publishes known absence; photosynthesis stops and heat cools. Light-instrument downtime at ticks modulo 211 in 51..53 leaves presence unavailable.
- Visible integer charge receives +9/+6/+3 at patch distances 0/1/2, or −1 elsewhere. Clouds divide positive intake by 3. Actual movement costs 1 charge.
- Heat rises 5 within 2 cells of an active patch, or 2 during clouds; otherwise it cools 4. Heat 90 or greater costs 3 additional charge.
- The runner supplies query fees. Their fractional remainder persists; completed charge units are debited after observations with the chosen action.
- A cycle first reaches charge 80, then heat ≤20 at least 3 cells from the patch with charge ≥20. Cycles, starvation (charge ≤5), and overheating (heat ≥90) are external outcomes only.
- Opaque light services expose presence/order only. Physiological services publish into the receiver-provided partition/capacity, without a wider numeric rescue or a write to controller memory.

Presence is acquired each tick; center reference banks update every two ticks. Decay measures successful acquisition steps. The last bank read is reused for one intervening tick. Missing or absent current evidence stays open even if old banks are still retained. The eager baseline order observer pays0.002 energy per actual pair comparison; spatial ordering costs1 and finer body reads0.25. These experimental fees are not actual machine-time prices. All variants retain the same instrument/observer unless the named connection is removed; unused slow banks still cost computation.

This is a diagnostic sandbox, not proof of learning or general intelligence. Source schedule/fees/thresholds are authored.
