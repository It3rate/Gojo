# Closer reading of the longer trial

41 sequential runs, 1,536 ticks each, 62,976 total habitat ticks. The primary
comparison is nine controllers across four seeds. All14 focused/contract checks
passed and all41 final native archives were decoded successfully. These are
authored structures, not learned organisms.

## What each information level contributed

| Information | Actual demonstrated use | Boundary of the conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown / presence / absence | Missing and known-dark acquisitions cause distinct native holds. Full completes174 cycles versus117 without those routes. | This policy chooses holding; absence does not intrinsically mandate it. |
| Orientation and order | Mirrored eye calibration restores the physical steering direction. Removing it completes0 cycles. Forward/mirrored full signatures match. | The instrument supplies the orientation; the creature does not discover it. |
| Recent relative evidence | A clear rise continues remembered heading without buying eye order. Saves1,616 eye requests against no-fast. | Aggregate feeding is worse:174 versus257 cycles. Historical positivity is not a current gradient. |
| Longer context | Twenty full-controller waits are armed across the four seeds, distinguished from current-only decline. | Removing slow context improves this habitat's aggregate feeding. A changed decision is not necessarily a better decision. |
| Coarse ranges / support | Whole ranges can settle feeding, cooling and rest without finer data. Their uncertainty stays available for later demands. | Body ranges have declared local units; light landmark ranks never become distances. |
| Calibrated amount math | Reserve subtraction and heat addition admit or refuse a four-tick wait; time has explicit correspondence to those allowances and the wait response. | The allowances are conservative authored contracts. Being locally safe does not establish opportunity value. |
| Selected precision | Same energy support70..84 resolves differently at79 and80 when queried. Full uses1,981 fine requests against12,288 always-fine. | Removing fine service has20 overheat ticks versus0 full, but less starvation. Precision has costs and changes response timing. |

These are coexisting service contracts, not a universal bit ladder or a claim
that ordinal calibration automatically becomes metric calibration. Explicit
units are supplied only where the controller performs amount arithmetic.

## Recent positivity can lag fresh dimming

**Seed7, ticks184–190:** full moves x14→15→16(the peak)→17→18→19→20.
At186, fast evidence is+16. At fresh acquisition188 the source value has
already fallen120→112 compared with186, but fast remains+4 and slow+9.
The native recent-rise rule skips eye ordering and continues. Odd tick189
reuses that read. Only190, with fast−5, queries the eyes and reverses.

The arithmetic is valid. The response rule interprets a weighted comparison to
older references as current progress. One-tick reuse extends its effect.
There are28 clean fresh-acquisition ticks in the four full trajectories where
the peak is stable, no cloud/dark/downtime intervenes, the current sample is
dimmer, and the recent-rise rule still continues.

**Seed19, ticks1454–1460:** the forager crosses the peak at1454 while cloudy.
Cloud clearing at1456 raises the relative evidence. At1458, a fresh clear-sky
sample drops112→104, but score+7 still continues, reaching x27 at1460 before
eye ordering reverses it. The paired no-fast controller orders the eyes at1458
and reverses immediately.

Across four seeds, full saves1,666.75 energy in declared query fees against
no-fast, but completes174 versus257 cycles and has236 versus9 starvation
ticks. Removing fast also removes its dependent dip waits, so the aggregate
gap is not an isolated test of only the positive-rise rule. Both retain126,050
baseline reference comparisons.

## Safe waiting can still be a poor choice

**Seed7, tick110:** full and no-slow are both x13 with peak10, energy66,
heat21, fast−4 and slow+1. Full refines energy, establishes reserve50 and
heat forecast40..64, and waits four ticks. Energy is62 by114. No-slow swims
left immediately, reaches the source with energy79 at113, and starts cooling
at energy83 at114. Full's wait satisfies the authored safety contract; it
delays useful feeding. Seed7 ends full51 cycles/20 starvation ticks versus
no-slow58/0.

**Seed19, tick186:** both full and no-budget are x22 against peak24,
energy75/heat60, fast−4/slow+7. Full rejects the wait because the conservative
heat forecast65..89 does not fit the safe region≤74, then swims right.
No-budget waits; subsequent heat reaches80 but not the damage threshold90.
This example shows conservative rejection, not proof that removing safety
would be better in other worlds. No-budget wins the aggregate comparison here.

## Finer information is not free

Always-fine pays2,576.75 more energy for finer body requests and1,225 more
for eye requests than full; temporal requests match. Total additional fees
are3,801.75 energy units across four seeds.

**Seed7, tick19:** full has energy72/heat22 and rests at x18; always-fine
already has energy65 and resumes seeking. By167 it is x27 against peak16,
energy22/heat27. It returns toward food but falls to energy4 at172 and zero
at174. Full still has energy72 at172. The histories strongly implicate query
expense and subsequent trajectory divergence; they do not separately isolate
an intrinsic harmful effect of greater precision.

Finer arithmetic does change an actual budget decision: **seed19, always-fine
tick242**, energy36/heat45 establishes reserve20 and heat forecast65, so the
wait is admitted. Its original coarse supports cannot justify that wait. It
feeds while holding, reaching energy45/heat65 at246. That local route is
informative even though always-fine performs poorly overall.

## Next useful experiment

Keep the working orientation/presence/coarse-support contracts. Test an
additional native fresh-order condition before treating positive accumulated
evidence as directional progress. Limit contextual waiting to situations with
evidence for a useful wait, rather than positivity against older samples alone.
Then compare the same seeds and fee schedule again. These changes have not
been implemented in this trial; the saved comparison remains reproducible.
