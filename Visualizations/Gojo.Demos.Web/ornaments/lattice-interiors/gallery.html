Let their boundaries follow.

One hundred finite repeated partitions, built by merging atomic interiors. Each face has one owner; neighbours share the boundary. Compare the coloured spaces with the lines they create.

All 100 togetherRules and rejected examples
This website edition includes all 100 final coloured and outline views, captured shape summaries, ratings and four rejected examples. Construction stages, full diagnostics and native archives remain in the research checkout because this site shares a hosting allowance with the other Gojo demos. These are recorded results; this page does not recompute the constructions.

This is the recorded final field. Changing the view changes its presentation, preserving the same native construction.

What the captured read establishes

Side counts collapse straight subdivisions. Reflex turns are inward corners. Boundary-detail cues are exploratory preferences, separate from partition validity.
