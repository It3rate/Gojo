Ornament grown within the spaces

The mesh sets the rhythm; the spaces hold the ornament. Flowers, fans and scrolls use centres, edges and tangent directions as construction, with colour choosing which layer comes forward.

This edition includes all twelve finished designs and their retained mesh views, copied from the recorded Gojo study. Construction guides, earlier comparisons and native archives remain in the research checkout. These pages display captured results; they do not generate new ornaments.

How the layers relate

The source mesh retains its curves, crossing order and repeating substrate. Interior frames select source boundaries or exposed landmarks. Reusable compound motifs consume those landmarks through explicit placement and transform relations; earlier decoration remains in the construction history. The mesh view is an even-width presentation of the retained centre paths, not a replacement for the native bands.

Placement and colour are authored and visually reviewed. The study does not claim automatic weighted-centre discovery, general containment or collision solving, or a learned aesthetic judgement.
