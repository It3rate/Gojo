The kernel has substantial executable mathematics. The text notation can construct many of those structures through its small vocabulary, but it has not demonstrated comprehensive mathematical coverage. Three different claims must remain separate: a graph can be authored; an appropriate kernel reader exists; that reader and its composition laws have been exercised through text.

A confirmed ratio-remainder mismatch

The following syntax constructs a valid current ratio and input:

gojo 1;
let $source = carrier();
let $target = carrier();
root point($source, 3) -> point($target, 2);
root at($source, 10);
root $target;

Using the kernel's TryProject on this parsed structure returns:

target result:   L(target, 6)
reported residue: L(source, 2)

The arithmetic performed by ProjectBetween is:

scaled numerator = 10 * 2 = 20
quotient = 20 / 3 = 6
remainder = 20 % 3 = 2
20 = 6 * 3 + 2

That numerator identity is correct. The unresolved contract is its basis: six target ticks account for nine source ticks, leaving one source tick. Treating the stored remainder as two ordinary source ticks reconstructs eleven, not ten. The remainder needs its scale retained or must be represented in an appropriate source/finer basis. Simply dividing it by the target extent in integer arithmetic would lose information in cases where that remainder is fractional in source ticks.

The issue reaches mutation: target.TrySetFrom(port, input) produces target value 6 and source tension 2. This observation-to-tension path treats the scaled remainder as source participation. Existing tests, including the backward-ratio case and carrier import tests, protect this convention, so a passing suite does not resolve the physical interpretation.

The new blend fixture's half-read and four-to-one coarse read have unit target extents; their retained remainder is directly in source ticks. Its gain-endpoint examples are exact. Those checks therefore did not expose this more general ratio problem. That limitation should narrow our earlier confidence about universal remainder preservation.

This is the first issue to settle before adding more algebra or feedback. Changing it requires auditing projection, import, operation residue, follow responses, and higher transfer readers together. Preserve the explicit remainder and its basis; do not normalize it into a misleading scalar.

Other boundaries verified directly

The local probe at artifacts/low-level-audit-2026-09-05 performed ten observations against parsed source. Besides the ratio and import examples:

  • resolution(unit, -2) and resolution(unit, 0) are accepted and return the original unit, as does factor 1. This follows UnitShape.ResolutionOf's <= 1 fallback. It is not negative resolution, inversion, or refinement. The authoring contract should make this explicit or reject misleading input.
  • The signed minimum integer parses, but projecting it through a 1:1 frame throws OverflowException when magnitude is calculated.
  • A 4,000,000,000:4,000,000,000 ratio applied to 8,000,000,000 also throws on intermediate multiplication, although the mathematical result fits in a signed 64-bit integer. Range guarantees need to cover intermediate arithmetic.
  • Text can crystallize the orthogonal of a point, a zero-length span, and a nonzero span. These are present capabilities, not merely possible spellings.

These probes characterize boundaries. They are not new regression tests endorsing the limitations or a new implementation of the missing laws.

Dimensions and inversions need specific claims

The system distinguishes several meanings that ordinary notation often hides:

negative coordinate
opposed carrier face
reversed ordered frame
backward ratio read
reciprocal unit participation
inverse transformation

The first four have executable footing. The last two are not generally solved. In particular, dual(carrier) must not be advertised as 1 / unit, and reverse(relation) does not recover information discarded by a transformation.

Repeated dimensional products such as u * u can preserve two occurrences of u. Incompatible additive units remain multi-participation structure; a shape-only crystal can exist while projection into one scalar value is refused. The active crystallized-resolution notes explicitly leave full distance/time * time -> distance cancellation for future inverse-unit participation.

There is also tested complex-like opposed-face behavior: a pair of opposed operands can produce a negative forward scalar, and fused operands distribute into visible terms. The fused-term path intentionally retains opposed participation before a scalar fold. That is useful intermediate structure, not a reason to claim complete complex arithmetic or to read every term's displayed face as its final scalar interpretation. We need explicit expanded-versus-folded agreement tests before making that stronger claim through syntax.

The next work should be mathematical conformance

Before another feedback-discovery fixture, build a compact set of authored structures with independently knowable outcomes and declared bases:

  1. Projection and remainder accounting. Exact and inexact ratios, both extents non-unit, offsets, reversed signs, and zero extents. Check quotient, remainder basis, reconstruction, and the observation/import boundary.
  2. Arithmetic composition and inversion. Signed sums and products, opposed-face cycles, repeated units, and fused products before/after an explicit fold. Compare equivalent constructions without erasing topology.
  3. Dimensional compatibility. Compatible and incompatible addition, squared/cubed units, calibration, and explicit unresolved denominator cases. Missing cancellation should remain visible rather than silently accepted.
  4. Resolution composition. Coarse and fine reads, operation before versus after projection, remainder refinement, carry, and unsupported borrow. Different rounded scalar answers may be valid if their full evidence accounts for the difference; scalar equality alone is an inadequate law.
  5. Authoring coverage and range. For each kernel primitive in scope, classify it as directly expressible, expressible through expanded relations, missing a construction path, or awaiting kernel semantics. Test numeric boundaries rather than assuming parser acceptance implies executability.

Deterministic projection residue is also different from measurement uncertainty. Knowing the exact remainder of a rounded read does not establish how a product of two approximate measurements should propagate bounds or correlated error. Those uncertainty laws should build on the exact foundation, not replace it.

This checkpoint keeps the dynamic thesis intact while giving its local participants mathematics whose guarantees can be stated precisely.
