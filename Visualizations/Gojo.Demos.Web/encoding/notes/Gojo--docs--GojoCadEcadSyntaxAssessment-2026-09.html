Gojo syntax for CAD and electronics: assessment and plan

Status: exploratory assessment, September 8, 2026. This is a documentation pass, not a new CAD kernel, circuit simulator, importer or grammar revision. The companion signal learning plan covers future observations, changing models and reward.

The contour and local-interface follow-up refines the rotation plan after the user's clarification. Begin with connected contour/radial/traversal construction; treat angle units and matrices as derived reads. It also records the existing implementation more fully and treats GojoEcad constructions as experiments rather than compatibility requirements. The subsequent rotation fixture implements the first discrete planar construction and an illustrated example. That fixture supplies coordinates and landmarks; the later correction, circle construction before coordinates, addresses the missing point/offset/radius/closure/partition sequence.

Assessment

The existing syntax is a credible structural substrate for these domains. Identity, ordered relations, exposed ports, exact selected arithmetic, open slots, bounded interpretations, dependencies and retained transformations are already useful ingredients. Bézier construction provides a concrete example of building a recognizable composite from smaller explicit operations.

It would nevertheless be premature to call CAD or electronics representation complete. There are four different levels of readiness:

  1. Storage: can the graph retain the participants and their relationships?
  2. Authorship and interchange: can a person or adapter express those relationships consistently and preserve source correspondence?
  3. Interpretation: is there a specified, executable read that derives the intended geometry, connection, equation or uncertainty?
  4. Conformance and evolution: does that read behave correctly at ambiguous boundaries, under changes, and at the required numerical resolution?

A general graph's ability to store a circle or SPICE file does not establish the last three. The largest gaps found here concern explicit mathematical and compositional contracts, more than missing punctuation. There is no evidence yet that each CAD or circuit concept needs its own new primitive element type.

What real formats require us to express

The official KiCad board format contains much more than coordinates: layers, setup, nets, footprints, tracks, zones and groups are connected concerns. Footprint placements, pad geometry, drilling, copper participation and zone boundaries make useful coverage cases. This suggests separate geometry, material, connection and design-rule surfaces, with correspondence between them.

The schematic format adds library definitions and instances, pins, wires, junctions, labels, no-connect markers and hierarchical sheet/instance paths. A drawn crossing is not sufficient connection evidence. A deliberately unconnected pin is not the same as an unknown connection. Instance identity must survive reuse of the same symbol or footprint definition.

Source or representation Useful sanity check What a Gojo representation must retain
KiCad board and project source A rotated pad, a via through selected layers, a zone with a hole and an applicable clearance rule Exact source and dialect, coordinate frames, layer/material participation, source identities, interpreted rules and unsupported remainder.
KiCad schematic Two instances of a symbol, a labelled net crossing a wire, an explicit no-connect Definition versus instance, hierarchical scope, ordered terminal correspondence, intended connections and visible unresolved cases.
STEP / boundary representation A placed part with oriented edges and trimmed surfaces Geometry versus incidence, local placements, trims, units, tolerances and assembly correspondence; only the supported interchange subset may be claimed.
Mesh or sampled boundary A coarse approximation of a smooth surface Adjacency, winding, source correspondence, approximation bounds when known and unresolved fine detail. A coarse triangle is not evidence that the source surface is planar.
Constructive or implicit geometry Difference of two solids; an inside/outside field The construction law, operands, selected metric or field basis, boundary convention and the validity of the derived boundary.
SPICE netlist and subcircuit A resistor divider, then a capacitor and a changing source Terminal order, node scope, model definition, parameters, units, initial state, source waveform and requested analysis.

Open CASCADE's STEP translator distinguishes geometry/topology/assembly translation from associated information such as names, layers and tolerances handled through its extended document tools. Its modeling guide separates geometric objects from their topological uses. These are useful comparisons for Gojo; interchange is not just a bag of points.

SPICE also distinguishes a device instance from a shared model and exposes subcircuits through ordered external nodes. Its numeric spelling is dialect-sensitive: for example, in the documented MacSpice conventions m means milli and Meg means mega. An importer cannot safely treat a value's suffix as a generic unit string. See the primary circuit-description reference.

The format survey is representative, not an exhaustive STEP, KiCad or SPICE conformance audit. Before implementing an adapter, pin an actual format version and create coverage fixtures for that version, including unknown constructs.

Preserve source without making source spelling the semantics

GojoEcad's lossless parser is a strong starting point. Exact source bytes, lexemes, spans and unsupported forms can remain available even when the local interpretation is incomplete. Keeping 1.600 and choosing an ordinary read of 1.6 are compatible operations when their correspondence and any lost detail are explicit. Decimal source precision is not physical manufacturing tolerance.

There is an important syntax boundary: the checked readable board capture (external repository reference) starts with gojo-ecad-readable 2. It is a derived presentation, not a document accepted by the current gojo 1 parser. We should plan an explicit bridge rather than imply these are already one language.

The bridge should connect source artifact and dialect, parsed occurrence, interpreted structure, chosen read basis, remaining unsupported participation and export correspondence. External UUIDs and text need lossless identity/text surfaces; an import-local $name is not a durable external identifier. Existing documentary text and source-byte structures provide precedents, but not yet a single agreed text interchange contract.

Reusable composites will also benefit from named imports, definition/instance binding, exposed interfaces and versioned references. These are authorship needs to specify after the structural contracts stabilize. Their expansion must leave the actual relationships present without requiring a name such as “pad”, “circle” or “capacitor” to be the sole meaning.

Geometry: the important missing contracts

Frames, affine objects and metrics

The structural starting point for rotation is already present: point-derived contour potential, radial attachment, calibration and ordered traversal. The follow-up review distinguishes the tested ingredients from the remaining contour-to-pose contract. That construction should establish the turn before it is read through coordinates.

The current AffineFrame.TryProject reads each target coordinate from one source coordinate. This supports explicit calibration and per-coordinate scale and offset, but does not provide a general matrix mixing coordinates. A Cartesian read of a rotated footprint includes terms such as x' = a*x + b*y + tx and y' = c*x + d*y + ty. A derived coordinate reader should make these participating axes, coefficients and offsets explicit, with domain/range and inversion conditions and correspondence to the turning construction.

For a later coordinate conformance check, choose a Euclidean frame and the matrix [[3/5, -4/5], [4/5, 3/5]]. Placing a local pad point (2, 1) mm at an origin of (10, 20) mm gives (52/5, 111/5) mm, exactly. This rotation mixes axes, yet needs no transcendental evaluation. The test should derive this read from the participating turning structure; merely authoring the matrix does not establish the Gojo construction. A displacement (2, 1) mm under the same rotation becomes (2/5, 11/5) mm; it does not acquire the origin.

Preserve the distinction between a point, a displacement and a direction. Translation acts on a point; it does not translate the displacement between two points. A shared length unit alone does not establish a Euclidean metric, perpendicularity, handedness or the transformation of a normal. Dot/cross products, determinants, distances and normals need selected laws and compatible bases. Singular maps can have a valid lower-dimensional read without a valid inverse. Unit products may have an explicit commutative dimensional read while the original ordered participation remains intact.

Circles, arbitrary rotations, roots and curve length also exceed finite rational evaluation in general. Retain the defining construction and give approximate reads an error/refinement contract. Known rational constructions should remain exact. A graphical normal vector and a normal probability distribution are separate concepts, neither supplied merely by an opposed face.

Geometry is not topology

A vertex may belong to several edge uses; an edge may have different oriented uses on neighbouring faces. A face can be a trimmed part of an underlying surface, with correspondence between its parameter-space trim and 3D boundary. Closed shells, inside/outside, holes and non-manifold cases need explicit incidence and validation contracts. Equal coordinates alone cannot create shared identity, a watertight seam or a connected solid.

GojoCAD's existing paths, shared sites and boundary experiments help here, but do not yet demonstrate this complete 3D hierarchy. Mesh, constructive-solid and implicit representations could expose different local reads of a common object. Converting between them should retain which geometry/topology was preserved, approximated or left unresolved. A signed field is not automatically a signed distance field; distance requires a metric and the appropriate law.

Robust predicates and uncertainty

For orientation, intersection, containment and clearance, specify what a read can conclude from its evidence. A useful proposed result distinguishes a proven relation, a proven contrary relation, insufficient precision and an unsupported/degenerate request. Boundary inclusion is another explicit choice. Refinement may make a predicate decidable; uncertainty should not silently turn an ambiguous contact into a Boolean success.

This does not eliminate established computational geometry. CGAL already offers exact predicates and exact-construction kernels, and Open CASCADE explicitly models shape tolerances. The opportunity is to connect geometric precision with physical uncertainty, intent, evidence and evolution. It is not established that Gojo can replace robust predicates with coarse numbers or outperform existing kernels.

Keep numerical approximation bounds, imported seam tolerances, manufactured variation, sensor uncertainty, preferred nominal values and desired clearance separate. A repair that moves geometry to close a gap is an explicit operation with displacement and correspondence, not an innocent observation. Refining a measurement need not mutate the object being measured.

Worked example: a bolt and a hole

Consider an illustrative smooth round bolt in a smooth round hole. These are assumed example bounds, not a manufacturing standard:

bolt diameter Db: [9.90, 10.00] mm
hole diameter Dh: [10.10, 10.20] mm

Under those bounds, diametral clearance Dh - Db lies in [0.10, 0.30] mm. For coaxial circular sections, radial clearance is half of that: [0.05, 0.15] mm. Suppose axis offset e lies in [0, 0.04] mm. The minimum radial gap in this selected section is g = (Dh - Db)/2 - e, conservatively bounded by [0.01, 0.15] mm. A required minimum of 0.02 mm is therefore not guaranteed across all admitted combinations, although many combinations satisfy it. This calculation assumes no additional dependence between the dimensions; known dependence should constrain the combinations rather than be discarded.

The required structure includes two object identities, their measured or specified diameter surfaces, the common unit/calibration, axis correspondence, the conditional bounds, a selected clearance read and a separate requirement. Its witness should retain the assumptions that produced the result.

If two measurements share a known additive calibration bias b, their difference can cancel that bias: (Dh + b) - (Db + b) = Dh - Db. Independent biases do not cancel merely because the readings use the same approximate category. This is a practical reason to preserve shared uncertainty structurally; it does not remove other uncertainty about either manufactured part.

“About 10 mm” alone would not justify either of the starting intervals. It can remain a landmark with an open expectation. A catalogue's nominal size is a different surface again. A favourable clearance does not establish compatible threads, pitch, handedness, lead-in, roundness, full-length alignment or a collision-free insertion path. Those can remain open without making the section calculation false.

Changing the desired gap is a design request. Learning that the drill produces a larger hole is an observation. Actually enlarging a hole is a physical transformation. They can all create relevant mismatch, but require different correspondence and change paths. The unknown source of a mismatch must not be declared “the bolt is wrong” by the clearance reader.

GojoEcad's existing plated-hole example already distinguishes a produced span from component acceptance and records directional violation. It is a useful fixture to translate into common syntax before adding a large mechanical CAD example. The fused via-family example is equally valuable: legal pairs of drill and diameter are not necessarily the Cartesian product of two independent lists.

Electronics: a good fit with specific physical obligations

Relations and exposed terminals are a natural fit for circuit topology. There are nevertheless several different connection claims:

  • A schematic or net list says two terminals should connect.
  • A chosen ideal circuit model treats them as one node.
  • Copper, plating and contact geometry provide a physical path.
  • A continuity measurement supports a claim about an actual manufactured board.

These claims can disagree. A net name does not prove a conductive path; a coordinate coincidence does not establish copper contact; a potential read need not drain charge from its source. Conversely, two separately represented conductors can couple through a field without becoming one node.

The next electrical composites need an explicit potential reference, oriented branch quantities, elapsed time, stored state and compatible units. For ideal elements under selected sign conventions, useful starting laws are:

resistor:   vab = R * iab
capacitor:  q = C * vab,       dq/dt = iab
inductor:   lambda = L * iab,  d(lambda)/dt = vab
power:      p = vab * iab

Here lambda is flux linkage. Constant R, C and L are assumptions of these simple models. At an ideal node with no separately represented stored charge, the signed branch currents sum to zero. Storage must otherwise appear in the balance. A passive resistor with R > 0 dissipates nonnegative power under the associated current/voltage convention. The SPICE element reference is a useful external comparison for terminal conventions and model parameters.

Gojo tension is not automatically voltage, a passage's capacity is not automatically capacitance, and a carrier face is not electrical phase or the imaginary unit. Signed physical quantities need explicit representations consistent with the kernel's nonnegative carrier value and signed locations, faces and tensions. A voltage read and a material charge transfer are different operations. A branch must not duplicate conserved charge across multiple accepted destinations.

Constitutive equations, conservation and integration must remain ordinary mathematics. Local settlement is a strategy for obtaining an admissible state; it does not establish that an arbitrary local update schedule will converge to the circuit solution. Inconsistent ideal constraints, floating references, algebraic loops, stiffness and discontinuous switching can remain unresolved until the selected method has a defined response. Simulation tolerances and physical device tolerances also have different meanings.

For a first static check, an ideal 10 V source across two series 1 kilohm resistors gives 5 mA and a midpoint 5 V above the chosen return node, assuming no load at that midpoint. A goal of 6 V creates a design mismatch; it does not make the conditional 5 V calculation approximate or erroneous. Tolerance, loading or a different source can be added as explicit participating premises.

A useful next dynamic example is a capacitor initially at zero connected through a positive resistor to a constant ideal voltage source. Under those assumptions, Vc(t) = Vs * (1 - exp(-t/(R*C))). Its state persists between observations; the source input, integration step and observation time are not interchangeable. A time-step refinement should approach the selected law without inventing or losing accounted charge. This is a proposed reference experiment, not current GojoECAD behavior. It connects directly to stage S5 of the signal plan.

SPICE models can initially be external, versioned black-box contributors with declared ports, parameters, state/initial conditions and applicable analyses. Their outputs can be compared with selected Gojo reads without claiming to understand every internal equation. Later, explicit reusable substructures may replace supported models. The ngspice documentation describes a pipeline from netlist preprocessing and circuit construction through matrix assembly and simulation; encoding the first stages alone does not implement the latter ones.

Evolution, uncertainty and crystallized composites

Confidence should attach to a claim and its basis: a curve may be exactly defined while the manufactured part is poorly measured; a net may be clearly specified while its copper continuity is uncertain. A single confidence value for the whole part or board would discard these distinctions. Evidence may be reused through several routes without becoming independent evidence.

Keep design revisions separate from physical time and evidence arrival. A hole's diameter can change while its intended feature identity survives; a Boolean cut can split one face into several. Persistent correspondence needs one-to-many and many-to-one histories, not only equal coordinates or a reused label. Dependencies should identify which fit, contact or prediction claims became stale. The current CAD read graph has affected-read navigation, but RefreshFrom rebuilds its reads from the root; it is not yet a general incremental evaluation engine.

History need not be retained forever. A compression or crystallization should say which useful promises survive, which evidence was summarized and what detail is no longer available. Stabilizing a unit's readable tick promise is not the same operation as packaging an arbitrary dynamic algorithm. A reusable curve or circuit composite needs exposed ports, parameter roles, internal correspondence, applicable laws and, where necessary, state and error contracts.

The Bézier fixture is the model to follow: its control points and six local interpolations explain the construction even when a control remains open. Later spline/NURBS composites will additionally need degree, knot order, weights, parameter domains and denominator/continuity conditions. A SPICE subcircuit needs terminal binding and model/state correspondence. These are compositional obligations, not a requirement to introduce opaque primitive “Bézier” or “SPICE” opcodes.

Recommended representation fixtures

These are proposed tests, not implementation commitments for this pass. Each should have an authored syntax example, numeric restoration, a selected read, negative cases and a plain-language explanation before wider abstraction.

Order Fixture What it should establish
C1 Import one exact board measure and one fused via family Source spelling versus value versus nominal choice; physical projection remainder; unsupported source retained; an explicit bridge into common syntax.
C2 Place a pad in a translated and rotated footprint Ground the turn in connected contour/radial or equivalent structure, then check its coordinate read; preserve point/displacement, inverse/degenerate cases and source-to-board correspondence.
C3 Same XY on different layers, then add a plated connection Distinguish intended net, geometric coincidence, layer membership, physical contact and selected ideal-node equivalence. An unknown layer must not imply connection.
C4 Bolt/hole or lead/plated-hole fit Nominal family, tolerance evidence, desired clearance, shared uncertainty and unresolved pose; recompute affected claims after a design or evidence change.
C5 A trimmed curve boundary with shared and merely coincident endpoints Parameter-to-geometry correspondence, orientation, boundary closure and partial construction; retain ambiguity instead of silently welding sites.
C6 A copper region with a hole and a nearby keepout Selected metric and clearance, robust predicate outcomes, scale/refinement behavior, topology-changing repair recorded as an action.
C7 A resistor divider, then an RC transient First exact static equations and gauge/sign/unit checks; then retained physical state, charge accounting, integration refinement and separate prediction assessment.
C8 Revise a footprint or split a face, then export Definition/instance identity, multiple successor correspondence, stale evidence, bounded history and version-specific source/export preservation.

For rotation, start with R1–R3 in the follow-up construction plan. Retain C1–C4 as concrete application checks that exercise shared gaps without requiring a full solid model or nonlinear simulator. C5–C8 can then justify broader contracts from concrete examples. A viewer is worthwhile for rotation, contact, fit and changing topology; pure unit arithmetic already has sufficient numerical fixtures.

The acceptance boundaries should remain separate: source round-trip, restored Gojo structure, external application acceptance, geometric validity, design-rule compliance, simulated behavior and measured physical behavior each answer a different question. Passing one is evidence for its selected claim, not a global declaration that the design is correct.
