Small concepts under change

What this experiment is trying to show

An object can change its state and still be the same connected object. A switch can be built into a Y or a T. A sequence can keep its order while its storage changes. This experiment asks which small relationships survive a change, which change in a predictable correspondence, and where each claim stops being valid.

Open Small concepts under change. Choose a concept, apply the preserving change, then try its challenge and concealed read. The concept and limit sentences are our descriptions of the experiment. They do not select its answer. The connected sources, their laws, and a small comparison circuit determine the answer.

Invariance means the selected read stays the same. Equivariance means the second read follows an explicitly connected transformation of the first. For example, exchanging a switch's outlet roles should exchange its occupied outlet. The entire encoded artifact need not stay the same in either case.

There are two kinds of trial here: a change to one retained source, and a comparison between two explicitly related constructions. The Y/T example is the latter: each apparatus receives its own initial activation and runs its own passage law. It does not claim to physically reshape a Y into a T.

A small vocabulary and its boundaries

Candidate word What it says What it does not say Concrete trial
Object One connected object can persist while its state changes. Adjusting parameters does not discover or create an object. One extent source, four uses; send an actual request to its ordinal dial.
Binding Several uses follow one source through a change. Equal-looking independent values do not establish a binding or common calibration. Compare shared uses with separate dials that initially agree.
Sequence Participants can retain order across different encodings. Order supplies no distance or duration. A chain and explicit precedence relations both establish the same before/after relation.
Loop Following the connected order returns to its starting site. Return does not establish area, simplicity, or active circulation. Change participation, then contrast with detaching the final endpoint.
Path A whole path can be read backwards with its connections intact. Reversing just one part need not preserve continuity. Reverse both occurrence order and every signed use.
Switch A control selects a route through different joint constructions. A similar drawing does not imply the same law; unknown control cannot choose. Map the three material roles of a Y switch to a T switch.
Collection Either contributor can make a common read participate. The read does not merge or duplicate the material. Swap the two contributors behind an OR presence read.
Priority A single arrival can use either inlet of a merge. Two simultaneous arrivals expose an ordering preference. Exchange contributors while retaining the serial passage order.
Perspective Changing the frame changes the answer predictably. Direction reversal is not reorientation; matching candidate sets do not identify actual outcomes. Reorient addition and its possible answers.
Mapping Opposite uses of shared extents preserve a rectangle construction. Losing the binding does not prove that the actual shape is nonrectangular. Adjust one shared extent, then compare with independent opposite extents.
Overlap Exchanging two spans preserves their shared occupied portions. Overlap specifies no measured size and needs a common ordered basis. Swap intersection operands; then reorder landmarks or omit the common basis.
Joint Several routes meet through one actual site. Meeting does not choose branching, collection, or a geometric angle. Retarget all arrivals together, then retarget only one.

These words are candidate descriptions, not automatically discovered categories. Several reads may apply to the same structure. The Y can expose joint, switch, sequence, and conservation reads without choosing one exclusive noun. In particular, the object trial checks persistence of one shared source; it is not a universal object detector. A replacement with several sources can still be an object under another connected read.

An extent is the unmeasured how-far of a source. The earlier term reach meant the same thing. The actual adjustment used here is only an ordinal rest/farther dial, driven by an existing material request. It is not a continuous length secretly stored behind the participation bit.

The comparison is itself connected structure

source A -> exposed read -> captured observation A --+
                                                   |
                          [A, B, answer] row -> correspondence law -> public answer
                                                   |
source B -> exposed read -> captured observation B --+

For a temporal trial, A is captured before the change and B afterwards. They are different observations even when they came from the same source. For a comparison between constructions, both reads are captured after their respective runs.

The law admits tuples of two selected three-bit read codes and a positive-context Boolean answer. An identity law asks whether those words match. A mapped law asks whether the second is the specified transformation of the first. It does not apply the transformation to either source or move material between them.

The input basis matters. Most trials expose a Boolean property. The perspective trial exposes ODV words. The switch and priority trials deliberately expose a whole three-site material pattern: its bits are presence at three different sites, not orientation, direction, and value of one element. Their role-map laws compare those patterns in the declared joint basis. Reusing the finite tuple codec does not authorize adding a joint pattern as though it were an ODV measure. This basis is supplied by the explicitly wired apparatus and its source read; the generic port interface does not yet transport a self-describing basis. Exporting these intermediate codes into an arbitrary new consumer would require that additional structural correspondence. Here only the comparison's Boolean answer is connected to the downstream consumer.

The same immutable law object is reused across different probe owners. A consumer can connect to a probe's public answer without reading its source internals. Tests connect this answer to an actual controlled passage: unresolved evidence holds an activation, a known mismatch blocks it, and established correspondence lets the same activation move. This demonstrates composition, not automatic learning.

Concrete encoding chart

Part Exact representation Meaning
First imported observation Endpoint 0 An exposed port or captured read.
Second imported observation Endpoint 2 A different exposed port or capture.
Calculated answer Endpoint 1 The output of this row; no independent material allocation.
Connection row [0,2,1], two bits per reference Six meaningful bits, low bit first; packed byte 18 hexadecimal.
Correspondence law 512 tuple bits = 64 bytes Bit a + 8*b + 64*answer admits [a,b,answer].
Answer support 01 Candidate word 000 The observed correspondence is false.
Answer support 10 Candidate word 001 The observed correspondence is true.
Answer support 11 Either Boolean word The observations do not determine whether it holds.
Answer support 00 No admitted tuple An observation or required compatible basis is absent.

The 64-byte bitmap uses the existing general three-role, eight-candidate tuple codec, shared by all instances of a law. It is deliberately not a new compact Boolean encoding. The eight-byte passage law used to move activation is a different bitmap: it relates whole before/after states of three presence sites.

For an explicit alias of the same exposed port, both inputs use endpoint 0 and the row becomes [0,0,1], one bit per reference, packed byte 04. The reader then retains the same candidate in both roles. Two historical captures are not that alias: an unknown value may have changed between observations.

The example source bytes on the page are selected excerpts, such as a complete route encoding, runtime presence byte, signed-use array, or geometric lane rows. They are not all complete source checkpoints. Comparison law and connection bytes are exact; their decoded tuple read independently reproduces the public answer.

What the interesting limits look like

Shared object versus equal appearances

One request can adjust a shared source observed by four occurrences. There is still one request, and the occurrence bindings remain the same. Four independent sources can initially have the same coarse value, but adjusting the first does not move the others. That establishes a difference in response, not a discovery of hidden physical lengths or a new source identity.

A switch across Y and T constructions

The correspondence maps Y material roles [0,1,2] to T roles [1,2,0]. The fourth role is the control and maps to itself. A known control routes one activation to the corresponding outlet in each construction. Occupied targets are tested too.

Replacing the T's controlled turn with a straight-through passage breaks that correspondence for the selected input. Concealing its control retains possible outlets in the prediction while the actual activation waits. A predicted branch is not committed material.

Collection is symmetric; priority need not be

The collecting Y keeps two receiving locations and publishes their OR presence. Exchanging the contributors preserves that presence read and the total occupied material sites. The OR result is an observation, not a claim that two parcels have become one parcel.

A capacity-one merge with explicitly ordered passages accepts the first arriving row it can service. With one arrival, exchanging inlets has the expected mapped result. With both arrivals, keeping the row order breaks that exchange symmetry: the outlet is occupied in both runs, but the remaining contributor fails the expected correspondence. A coarse invariant can hide a meaningful finer failure. The page displays the predicted second code beside the actual second code: in this challenge both actual patterns are 110, while exchanging the roles requires 101. Equal-looking outputs are not sufficient for equivariance.

A lost guarantee is not its negation

The rectangle reader has an explicit Euclidean premise: four straight lanes +u,+v,-u,-v with perpendicular axes. Opposite lanes refer to the same positive unknown extents. Those references support closure for any positive choices of A and B. Four unrelated nonzero values do not provide the same promise.

Removing opposite-side sharing therefore changes this reader from established to unresolved. It does not establish an irregular quadrilateral. Similarly, spans on two unrelated rails do not establish empty overlap: there is no common basis for that combination, so its read is absent.

Preserved uncertainty is not a selected answer

Reorienting an addition's complete possible-answer set produces the same set as adding the reoriented operands. This can be checked exactly over the finite ODV domain. Nevertheless, two independently captured uncertain outputs need not select corresponding actual outcomes. Their comparison remains unresolved.

A known false read can also stay false, producing a true equality comparison. Thus “comparison supported” does not automatically mean “the named concept is present.” The individual observations remain displayed beside the comparison.

Reuse and crystallization

The executable meaning is a reference to connected laws, ordered roles, exposed sources, and their read. The short word is an optional human label for that structure. Deleting the label cannot change a result.

The experiment also captures an already constructed passage pattern as immutable law bits and ordered role rows, then binds it into another local construction. This retains the whole passage behavior, rather than replacing it with a Switch opcode. Rebinding supplies new local ports and independently supplied state; it does not copy material from the original apparatus.

The captured Y switch occupies 20 bytes: a version byte, a four-role framing byte, and two ordered records containing an eight-byte passage law plus one packed role row. Its rows are [2,3,0] and [2,3,1]: source role 2, control role 3, and the two outlets. The first law admits flow when the control is clear; the second admits it when set. Binding roles to T ports [1,2,0,3] gives the same choice through a different joint. Neither a Y nor a T label survives in the retained pattern.

Each record is read until the byte boundary ends; there is no separate stored step count. Five-role patterns use wider reference rows. Loading checks framing, padding, reference validity, and the passage's material accounting law. These are storage checks, not additional conceptual measures.

This is a small reusable-pattern foothold. It is not automatic discovery of a concept, an inferred universal symmetry, or kernel crystallization of a new unit shape. Selecting useful transformations, learning a compact read, and deciding when evidence justifies reuse remain further experiments. The proposed direction is to retain those decisions and their evidence in connected structure too.

Bounded verification

Run the experiment and export its raw observations with:

dotnet run --project experiments/PrimitiveMeasure -- --invariance experiments/PrimitiveMeasure/data/invariance

The finite sweeps distinguish matching sets from actual outcomes, compare Y/T role maps including receiver occupancy, expose merge-priority counterexamples, exercise Boolean span laws under operand exchange, and vary participation on retained cycles. Their exact domains and counts are recorded in the generated summary. They do not exhaust arbitrary geometry, construction history, future edits, or all possible concepts.

The recorded run contains 1,848 observations: 36 displayed trials, 64 ODV operand pairs, 16 switch states, 4 collecting exchanges, 4 merge exchanges, 1,600 Boolean span combinations, and 124 cycle participation patterns. Expected limits include one exact-state merge-priority counterexample and 720 Boolean operand exchanges that need a corresponding change to the law. These are retained counterexamples, not failed implementation checks.

The interactive case records include each preserving, challenging, and concealed trial. The consumer's movement comes from its connected passage, not from a label in the exported report.

The other raw records are addition frames, joint switches, collecting inputs, merge priority, Boolean spans, and cycle participation.

Current laws establish what these deliberately assembled structures do. The observer and CSV writer report it after execution. No external classifier tells the runtime which concept it should claim.
