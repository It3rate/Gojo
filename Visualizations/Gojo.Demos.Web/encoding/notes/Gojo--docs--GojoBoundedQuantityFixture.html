Bounded quantities, shared causes and selected detail

This fixture extends the uncertainty reference. Status: implemented first fixture, September 6, 2026. The first law is a guaranteed enclosure under explicit local premises. It does not require access to an external source's private history or supply a probability, confidence-update, clustering or attention-allocation rule.

Structural contract

The following notation describes ordinary version-1 Gojo structure. It precedes the helper names; no operation tags or new primitive kinds are required.

bounded sample = group(sample slot or exact sample, support span, evidence)
contribution   = bounded sample -> (source frame -> target frame)
affine read    = group(exact offset, ports(contributions...), provenance)

The support span is read as a closed real-valued enclosure in its carrier's tick calibration. Its authored endpoint order remains present. This selected quantity read is distinct from reading the same span as occupied material. A bare span does not assert that any particular part conforms to a tolerance.

The sample slot identifies a local unresolved quantity. The evidence can be opaque or incomplete. Repeating that slot preserves dependency; using separate slots supplies no statistical-independence claim. Several bounds on the same slot are intersected in the same explicit source basis. Empty intersection, unbound calibration, mismatched basis and unsupported slot faces remain unresolved, even if a later coefficient would cancel the quantity.

Every contribution has an ordinary affine calibration:

S(source,a,b) -> S(target,c,d)
y = c + (x-a)(d-c)/(b-a)

Exact offsets and endpoints use the existing rational participation shape L(C,n) -> P(C,d) when they need a positive subdivision. They do not create fractional carrier state. The backend may calculate rational coefficients, but the resulting shared slots, intersected supports and calibrated contributions must remain ordinary Gojo structure with the original input as evidence.

Further selected operations use exposed source surfaces:

combination = ports(affine source -> calibration, ...) -> P(output,1)
product     = ports(left affine source, right affine source) -> S(output,0,1)
quotient    = group(numerator affine source, denominator affine source,
                    output carrier, standard)
conformance = group(quantity observation, permitted span, specification evidence)

Combination retains the shared causes in another affine read. Product and quotient retain their input structures and provide conservative enclosures; they do not silently manufacture independent affine causes for nonlinear results. Native output units must match the selected operation. Division checks the denominator's domain before any same-source cancellation.

Each successful read produces a common ReadWitness. The result contains exact lower and upper endpoints and an enclosing whole-tick span. Rounding is outward; the lower and upper rounding excesses remain separate exact participations. The result is observational and introduces no carrier tension.

Conformance observes the distance from a nonempty possible-value interval to the permitted interval. Zero maximum violation settles this criterion for all represented possibilities. Positive minimum violation excludes conformance. A zero minimum with positive maximum preserves an unresolved decision. It does not say which premise or participant should change.

The quantity result retains variation inside tolerance. For example, a known two-tick discrepancy has zero violation against [-3,3] and two ticks of violation against [0,0]. The accepted coarse read never overwrites that underlying discrepancy.

Implemented local reads

BoundedQuantity builds samples and affine surfaces and reads exact endpoints. Its affine implementation collects calibrated contributions by explicit slot identity. The selected operations provide:

Entry point Selected operation Result or unresolved condition
TryObserve Offset plus a flat group of calibrated bounded samples Exact affine coefficients and a conservative closed enclosure
TryCombine An ordered group of affine-source/calibration pairs into a unit output point Another affine surface retaining shared causes and all domain premises
TryMultiply Two affine inputs into a native product frame Four-corner enclosure, with a tighter nonnegative same-function square read
TryDivide Two affine inputs, native quotient output and standard Rational enclosure; zero-containing denominator support remains open before cancellation
TryObserveConformance A quantity observation and a permitted span Minimum/maximum violation distance; a readable result need not mean conformance
TryReadBounds A successful witness's result Exact endpoints and their minimal outward integer enclosure; malformed or open results fail

For a cause x_i with several explicit supports, let [l_i,u_i] be their intersection in one source basis. After applying each calibration, the affine read has the form

y = k + sum(a_i * x_i)
lower = k + sum(min(a_i * l_i, a_i * u_i))
upper = k + sum(max(a_i * l_i, a_i * u_i))

Coefficients for a shared slot are combined first. Distinct slots are allowed to vary across a Cartesian enclosure; this gives a conservative bound without asserting statistical independence. Exact rational arithmetic precedes endpoint rounding. Known sample bindings must satisfy their support, and even zero coefficients retain their domain conditions.

The normalized affine surface encodes each combined coefficient with an ordinary zero-origin calibration and retains the original constraints as evidence. No private dependency table must accompany numeric transport. Intermediate fractions use wide arithmetic; each persisted coordinate, numerator, denominator and frame endpoint must fit the kernel's stored range. An unrepresentable result throws overflow rather than silently saturating.

Current limits

  • Bounds are conditional mathematical promises, not externally verified facts. Their supporting evidence may remain opaque or partial. The reader does not inspect that evidence to infer authority, independence or ancestry.
  • The first sample supports use integer endpoints but describe closed real-valued intervals in the selected tick calibration. Exact fractional samples, offsets, projected endpoints and rounding excesses use structured rational participation. Open/disconnected sets, general discrete-candidate constraints and automatic nominal classification remain future work.
  • A sample slot must have a forward placeholder face. Carrier frames are concrete forward faces; signed coordinates and reversed frames are supported. Opposed-face quantity laws require a separate explicit extension.
  • Samples of a shared unknown use one source carrier. Compatible units on distinct carriers do not infer reference equality. Cross-basis contribution and output conversion require an explicit calibration.
  • The affine surface is a flat local construction; combination emits another flat surface. The reader does not recursively execute arbitrary expression graphs or traverse provenance in search of operands.
  • Nonlinear reads preserve the source relation but need not produce tight bounds. For x in [-1,1], this product read encloses (1+x)(1-x) in [0,4], while a stronger reader could derive [0,1]. It does not return a fake new independent affine cause or treat every point in the enclosure as attainable. Further affine composition of that nonlinear result is not implicit.
  • Division refuses an enclosure containing zero even when an additional, currently unexpressed constraint could exclude zero. Native unit promises and standard evidence remain required; no unit simplification bypasses the numeric domain check.
  • Emitted endpoints and normalized coefficients capture the selected read. They do not automatically update after an upstream change. Source and correspondence remain attached for a fresh read or explicit dependency mechanism; live references alone are not an immutable historical sample.
  • Confidence, learned similarity, utility-based weighting/atrophy, automatic attention and selection of a useful response remain exploratory policies.

Focus and opaque sources

“About 100 miles” can communicate that finer distance is outside the current focus. It need not mean the speaker lacks a finer observation. A focus can expose a coarse read and retain the fine complement without manufacturing physical uncertainty. That attention implication is contextual; a precise number alone does not prove importance or accuracy.

Outside sources may remain black boxes. A local space can retain observed reliability, timing, processing or shared-variation evidence through exposed surfaces, without traversing an entire ancestry. Learned similarity can guide selection, weighting or pruning; it does not justify exact alias cancellation. An explicit source correspondence may also be supplied by another mechanism.

An error inside a permitted tolerance can cease to demand correction for that task. Its known magnitude, uncertainty and acceptability remain separately readable. Losing that separation would prevent a stricter later query.
